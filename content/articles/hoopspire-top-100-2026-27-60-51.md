## How the list is built

**Game Score** is John Hollinger's one-line summary of a box score. It adds points, rebounds, assists, steals and blocks, and it takes away missed shots, missed free throws, turnovers and fouls.

**Availability.** A player with 65 games or more keeps his full score. Below that, the score is multiplied by the square root of games played over 65. The result is the Margin Score, and veterans are ranked by it alone. The pool is every current roster player with at least 20 games last season.

**Rookies** have no NBA box score, so they are placed on fit and projection. All three have already appeared in this countdown, and this part has none.

Five of the ten shot clearly better than the pool: Allen (66.9%), Jones (63.7%), Knueppel (63.3%), Powell (60.9%) and Clingan (60.4%). Pritchard (58.4%) and Rollins (58.3%) are within a tenth of a point of it. Miller (57.4%) is a point below. Daniels (54.2%) and White (52.9%) are 4.1 and 5.5 points below.

Volume is the other route. Miller took 16.1 shots a game, Powell 15.4 and White 14.4, the three highest in the part. Powell is the only player who is on both lists, and he has the top score. White is on the volume list and the low-efficiency list together, and he still ranks 55th. His case rests on the rest of the box: 5.4 assists, 1.1 steals, 1.3 blocks and 90.2% from the line. Daniels, at the bottom of the part, shows the same thing from the other side. He scored 11.9 points, the fewest here, and his line is 6.8 rebounds, 5.9 assists and 1.96 steals.

## What this part does not say

**The order inside this part is thin.** Ten veterans sit inside 0.73 points of Margin Score, and White and Clingan are separated by the third decimal. Call the group a tier. Do not read much into who is first within it.

**The cut for missed games matters for two players.** Only Allen (56) and Powell (58) played fewer than 65 games. Allen lost four places for it. Powell ranks 51st per game and 51st here.

**Game Score sees defense only through steals and blocks.** Whatever a defender's positioning is worth, it is not in the number beyond the steals and blocks it produces.
