[Rudy Gobert](/player/NBA/3032976) shot 66.4% true shooting last season, better than anyone else in this part of the countdown by more than four points. He did it on 6.5 shots a game, the fewest of the ten players here. [VJ Edgecombe](/player/NBA/5124612) shot 54.0% on 13.7, the worst in the group. In our ranking they sit at No. 67 and No. 69, two places apart.

That is the point of this part. Ten veterans, from No. 70 to No. 61, are separated by 0.67 points of Margin Score, 12.56 at the bottom and 13.24 at the top. They got there by very different routes. [Nikola Jokić](/player/NBA/3112335), at No. 1, scored 28.68.

This is Part 4 of 10, one a day, from No. 100 to No. 1. The method has not changed since [Part 1](/story/hoopspire-top-100-2026-27-100-91), [Part 2](/story/hoopspire-top-100-2026-27-90-81) and [Part 3](/story/hoopspire-top-100-2026-27-80-71): each veteran's 2025-26 Game Score per game, cut for missed games below 65, and nothing moved by feel.

## How the list is built

**Game Score** is John Hollinger's one-line summary of a box score. It adds points, rebounds, assists, steals and blocks, and it takes away missed shots, missed free throws, turnovers and fouls.

**Availability.** A player with 65 games or more keeps his full score. Below that, the score is multiplied by the square root of games played over 65. The result is the Margin Score, and veterans are ranked by it alone. The pool is every current roster player with at least 20 games last season.

**Rookies** have no NBA box score, so they are placed on fit and projection. All three have already appeared in this countdown, and this part has none.

Nothing in his line is loud. Game for game he ranks 85th.

Of the five players taken first through fifth in 2025, he is our third best, behind [Cooper Flagg](/player/NBA/5041939), whom Part 3 placed at 32nd in our model, and [Kon Knueppel](/player/NBA/5061575). ## What this part does not say

**The order inside this part is thin.** Ten veterans sit inside 0.67 points of Margin Score, and Gobert and Okongwu are separated by the fourth decimal. Call the group a tier. Do not read much into who is first within it.

**The cut for missed games matters for two players.** Only Nembhard and Barrett played fewer than 64 games, and each lost four to five places for it. Vučević played 64 and the cut is almost invisible.

**Game Score sees defense only through steals and blocks.** Whatever Gobert's rim protection or the positioning of Bridges and Anunoby is worth, it is not in the number.
