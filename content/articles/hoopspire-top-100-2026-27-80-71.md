Nine veterans in this part of the countdown are separated by 0.56 points of Margin Score, 11.88 at the bottom and 12.44 at the top. Nikola Jokić's score, at No. 1 in our model, is 28.68.

Getting to the same number took very different players. [Ayo Dosunmu](/player/NBA/4397002) shot 63.0% true shooting on 10.8 shots a night. [Kevin Porter Jr.](/player/NBA/4397140) shot 56.7% on 13.5 and made up for it with 7.4 assists and 2.2 steals. [Nic Claxton](/player/NBA/4278067) attempted 19 threes all season and made three.

This is Part 3 of 10, one a day, from No. 100 to No. 1. The method is the same as in [Part 1](/story/hoopspire-top-100-2026-27-100-91) and [Part 2](/story/hoopspire-top-100-2026-27-90-81): each veteran's 2025-26 Game Score per game, cut for missed games below 65, and nothing moved by feel.

## How the list is built

**Game Score** is John Hollinger's one-line summary of a box score. It adds points, rebounds, assists, steals and blocks, and it takes away missed shots, missed free throws, turnovers and fouls.

**Availability.** A player with 65 games or more keeps his full score. Below that, the score is multiplied by the square root of games played over 65. The result is the Margin Score, and veterans are ranked by it alone. The pool is every current roster player with at least 20 games last season.

**Rookies** have no NBA box score. Three are placed on fit and projection, and each placement says why. This part holds the last of the three.

**78. [Matas Buzelis](/player/NBA/4711294), Bulls.** He is 21 and played 77 games, tied with Smith for the most in this part. He blocked 1.5 shots a game, the most here, and took 495 threes, also the most, making 34.9%. He averaged 16.3 points and 5.8 rebounds. Game for game he ranks 97th.

**76. [Ayo Dosunmu](/player/NBA/4397002), Timberwolves.** The best true shooting here, 63.0%, from the fewest minutes, 27.3 a night. ## What this part does not say

**The order inside this part is thin.** Nine veterans sit inside 0.56 points of Margin Score. [Jabari Smith Jr.](/player/NBA/4432639) and [Jrue Holiday](/player/NBA/3995) are 0.015 apart. Call the group a tier. Do not read much into who is first within it.

**The cut for missed games matters most for two players.** Only Porter and Holiday played fewer than 65 games. Porter's cut moves him from 36th to 73rd, the largest swing in this part.

**Game Score favours the ball.** It counts steals and blocks but not screens, positioning or defensive assignments. Claxton is the player in this part most likely to be ranked too low.

**Team context is last season's.** Jaquez and Claxton play for new teams, and Dosunmu was traded during the last season. Their numbers come from their old roles.
