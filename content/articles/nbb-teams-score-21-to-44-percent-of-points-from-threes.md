Cruzeiro has scored 380 points in its first five NBB games and only 81 of them have come from three-pointers. Mogi has scored 404 in five and 177 have come from threes. That is 21.3% against 43.8%, a gap of more than 22 points of share, and the two clubs have nearly the same record: Mogi is 4-1, Cruzeiro 3-2.

The question this raises is what a club's scoring mix says about how it plays and how it does. The short answer after 42 games is that it says a lot about style and almost nothing yet about results.

The figures come from the league's published 2026-27 statistics and standings through games of November 4, 2026. Every player's points per game are split into three-pointers, two-pointers and free throws. Multiplying by games played and adding up the players on a club reproduces that club's listed points exactly. The total matches for all 18 clubs, so the split below is a complete accounting of the points scored, not an estimate. The sample is small: each club has played between three and six games.

## The league's mix

NBB clubs have scored 6,517 points in these games. The split:

| Source | Points | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Three-pointers | 2,301 | 35.3% |
| Two-pointers | 3,156 | 48.4% |
| Free throws | 1,060 | 16.3% |

The median club gets about 36% of its points from threes and 16% from the line.

## Three-point share, the widest spread

The range is from 21.3% to 43.8%. The table shows the three clubs with the highest share, the three with the lowest, and UNIFACISA for comparison.

| Club | Record | Three-point share | Two-point share | Free-throw share | Points scored |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mogi | 4-1 | 43.8% | 43.1% | 13.1% | 404 |
| Paulistano | 3-2 | 43.7% | 39.0% | 17.3% | 364 |
| Pato | 1-3 | 43.6% | 45.3% | 11.2% | 296 |
| UNIFACISA | 4-1 | 28.9% | 54.9% | 16.1% | 415 |
| Botafogo | 2-3 | 25.5% | 60.5% | 14.0% | 400 |
| Bauru | 2-3 | 24.8% | 59.2% | 16.0% | 375 |
| Cruzeiro | 3-2 | 21.3% | 60.5% | 18.2% | 380 |

Mogi, Paulistano and Pato score almost identically by source, within two tenths of a point on threes. Their records are 4-1, 3-2 and 1-3. At the other end, Botafogo, Bauru and Cruzeiro get about 60% of their points from two-pointers and have records of 2-3, 2-3 and 3-2.

Four clubs are 4-1: Mogi, UNIFACISA, Flamengo and Pinheiros. Their three-point shares run from 28.9% (UNIFACISA) to 43.8% (Mogi), with Flamengo at 36.3% and Pinheiros at 39.5%. The only unbeaten club, Minas Storm at 5-0, is at 34.9%.

## The free-throw line

The free-throw share is narrower, from 11.2% to 25.9%, and one club is far from the rest.

Basquete Cearense has scored 68 of its 263 points from the line. That is 25.9%, and the next club is São José at 20.9%. Cearense is 0-4 and has been outscored by 113 points over the four games, the worst difference in the league. Its 65.8 points a game are the fewest in the league. Pato sits at the other end, with 33 of 296 points, 11.2%.

## What the numbers show, and what we read into them

The numbers show a wide spread in how clubs score and no pattern yet in who wins. Four clubs with the same 4-1 record cover a 15-point range in three-point share, and three clubs with nearly the same style have records of 4-1, 3-2 and 1-3.

What we read into that is a caution, not a finding. The sample is three to six games per club, which is too small for a style to have settled or for any link to results to show. Club mixes will move as the 111 remaining first-turn games are played. The check worth running later is whether a high-three, low-two profile is stable from game to game, or whether it is mostly what one hot week looks like.
