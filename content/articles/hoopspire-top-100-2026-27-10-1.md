This is Part 10 of 10, the last part. The method has not changed since [Part 1](/story/hoopspire-top-100-2026-27-100-91): each veteran's 2025-26 Game Score per game, cut for missed games below 65, and nothing moved by feel.

## How the list is built

**Game Score** is John Hollinger's one-line summary of a box score. It adds points, rebounds, assists, steals and blocks, and it takes away missed shots, missed free throws, turnovers and fouls.

**Availability.** A player with 65 games or more keeps his full score. Below that, the score is multiplied by the square root of games played over 65. The result is the Margin Score, and veterans are ranked by it alone. The pool is every current roster player with at least 20 games last season, 406 players.

## Where the points come from

Part 6 split Game Score into a scoring part (points, plus 0.4 for each made shot, minus 0.7 for each attempt and 0.4 for each missed free throw) and everything else (rebounds, assists, steals and blocks, minus fouls and turnovers). The two always add up to Game Score.

- **Jokić** has an everything-else part of 9.73 a game. The next best in the whole pool is [Dyson Daniels](/player/NBA/4869342) at 6.75, almost three points behind.
- **Gilgeous-Alexander** has the biggest scoring part in the pool, 21.40, ahead of Dončić's 20.99. He beats Jokić on scoring by 2.45 a game. Jokić beats him on everything else by 4.71. The difference is the 2.26 at the top of the list.
- **Edwards** has the smallest everything-else part in the top ten, 2.61. His scoring part, 18.04, is fifth in the pool.
- **Johnson** has the smallest scoring part here, 13.46, and an everything-else part of 6.46, fourth in the pool.

## What this list does not say

**No. 2 is a coin flip.** Game for game, Dončić and Gilgeous-Alexander were 0.02 apart. The order here comes from the cut, and the cut comes from one game. Treat them as a tier.

**The cut for missed games is a rule, not a finding.** It treats fewer games as less evidence. It says nothing about why a player missed games or how many he will play this season. If you believe Antetokounmpo and Embiid will be on the floor, their per-game ranks, fourth and 10th, are the numbers to use.

**Game Score sees defense only through steals and blocks.** Wembanyama's 3.08 blocks are in the number. Whatever his size does to the shots that are never taken is not.
