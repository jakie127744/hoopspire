Florida played 79% of their 2025-26 regular-season minutes with players who are on the 2026-27 roster. No other team that finished in last season's BPI top 10 returns more than 57%. That one number puts the Gators first in the Hoopspire Top 32.

This is our own preseason ranking, published before the first game. It is not a poll, and nobody votes. Every team is rated by the Hoopspire model, which looks at how strong a team was last season and who is on its roster now, transfers included, and applies the same rules to all 365 Division I teams.

Last season's measure of strength is ESPN's final 2025-26 Basketball Power Index (BPI): the expected point margin per 70 possessions against an average Division I team on a neutral court. Duke and Michigan finished at 24.9, the best in the country. An average team is 0.

How to read the list: the Hoopspire rating is on BPI's scale. "Returning" is the share of last season's regular-season minutes played by players still on the roster. Points per game are 2025-26 regular-season averages. Rosters are ESPN's as of October 22.

## The Top 32

Florida (rating 20.9; last season 27-8, BPI 5th).** The core is back: [Thomas Haugh](/player/NCAAM/5080489) (17.1 points), [Alex Condon](/player/NCAAM/5174657) (15.0), [Boogie Fland](/player/NCAAM/5238195) and Rueben Chinyelu. [Denzel Aberdeen](/player/NCAAM/5105527) arrives from Kentucky. Returning: 79%.

**2. Duke (17.3; 35-3, BPI 1st).** Duke lose more than half of their minutes, but [John Blackwell](/player/NCAAM/5103630), who averaged 19.0 at Wisconsin, is the highest-scoring transfer on any of the top five rosters. [Caleb Foster](/player/NCAAM/4711256) and Cayden Boozer are back. Returning: 44%.

**3. Illinois (16.6; 28-9, BPI 6th).** A top-10 team that keeps most of its rotation: [David Mirkovic](/player/NCAAM/5311832), Andrej Stojakovic and Tomislav Ivisic return, and [Stefan Vaaks](/player/NCAAM/5311991) (15.8 at Providence) joins. Returning: 57%.

**4. Michigan (16.3; 37-3, BPI 2nd).** Last season's team was one of the two best in the country, and that still counts. [Elliot Cadeau](/player/NCAAM/4869764) and Trey McKenney return; [Kenny Noland](/player/NCAAM/5106236) (17.1 at Columbia) and Moustapha Thiam (Cincinnati) arrive. Returning: 35%. ## What this list does not say

It does not know how good a freshman is, because a freshman has no college minutes to measure. Purdue, Arizona and Alabama lean most on newcomers in the 32. Those are the teams most likely to be wrong in either direction.

It does not know about injuries or eligibility rulings after October 22, the date of the rosters we used.

A great player who spent last season on a weak team is probably underrated here.

It is a starting point, not a prediction of March.
