[Allisha Gray](/player/WNBA/3058901) scored 19.0 points a game for the Atlanta Dream this season, more than anyone on the team, and played in all 44 games. She will not play in Game 2 of the WNBA semifinals against the New York Liberty on Wednesday. Dream coach Karl Smesko ruled her out on Tuesday with an injury to her left hand, which she hurt in Game 1.

Gray went to the bench with 3:59 left in the first quarter on Sunday, came back at 8:30 of the second, and did not play again after 3:44 of the second quarter. She finished with seven points in 11 minutes. Atlanta won anyway, 92-82, and leads the best-of-five series 1-0.

Game 2 tips off at 7:30 p.m. ET on Wednesday, October 7, at State Farm Arena.

## How Atlanta won without her

The Dream won Game 1 in the third quarter. They trailed 43-39 early in the second half, scored the next 13 points and won the quarter 32-15. [Jordin Canada](/player/WNBA/3142250) scored 25 points, 12 of them in the third, with five steals. [DeWanna Bonner](/player/WNBA/869) scored 23 in 30 minutes off the bench.

[Sabrina Ionescu](/player/WNBA/4066533) scored 27 for New York. The Liberty pulled within five three times in the fourth quarter and could not get closer. [Satou Sabally](/player/WNBA/4281929) did not play for New York in Game 1.

## The other semifinal

The Golden State Valkyries host the Las Vegas Aces in Game 2 at 9:30 p.m. ET on Wednesday at Chase Center. Golden State lead that series 1-0 too.

Game 1 was decided on the glass. The Valkyries out-rebounded the Aces 52-25 and took 17 offensive rebounds to Las Vegas' two, and won 71-60. [Gabby Williams](/player/WNBA/3142328) had 25 points, 11 rebounds and five assists. [Kiah Stokes](/player/WNBA/2590093) did not score and still pulled down 14 rebounds, with four blocks.

[A'ja Wilson](/player/WNBA/3149391) scored 24 for Las Vegas on 8-of-21 shooting, and the Aces shot 34% as a team. Las Vegas had won all three regular-season meetings.

The Aces have a question of their own. [Jewell Loyd](/player/WNBA/2987869) played 17 minutes in Game 1, scored no points and left with a leg injury. Her status for Game 2 had not been confirmed by Tuesday.

## How they got here

Atlanta swept the Washington Mystics in the first round, 92-77 and 93-75, and are 3-0 in these playoffs. New York swept the Minnesota Lynx, closing it out 87-71. Golden State needed three games against the Dallas Wings and came back from 16 points down to win the decider 77-73. Las Vegas also went the distance, beating the Indiana Fever 94-83 in Game 3.

## Where the series go next

Both semifinals are best of five, and both move to the other team's court for Game 3 on Friday, October 9: New York host Atlanta at Barclays Center at 7:30 p.m. ET, and Las Vegas host Golden State at Michelob Ultra Arena at 9:30 p.m. ET.

If a Game 4 is needed, New York host it at 2:00 p.m. ET on Sunday, October 11. The time of a Las Vegas Game 4 has not been set. A Game 5 in the Atlanta series would be back at State Farm Arena on Wednesday, October 14.

## What is next

Wednesday, October 7: Liberty at Dream, 7:30 p.m. ET, on ESPN. Aces at Valkyries, 9:30 p.m. ET, on Peacock and NBCSN. A win puts either home team one game from the WNBA Finals.
