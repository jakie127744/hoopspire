The Philippines won their first game in Division A of the FIBA U18 Women's Asia Cup on Tuesday, beating New Zealand 69-64 at the Karisma Arena in Seremban, Malaysia. It was the third and last group game for both teams. In their first season at this level, the Philippines finished third in Group A at 1-2 and go on to the Qualification to Semi-Finals.

The group phase ended on Tuesday. Australia, the two-time defending champions, and Japan won their groups without a loss and go straight to the semi-finals.

## How the Philippines won it

New Zealand led 39-30 at half-time. The Philippines opened the third quarter with 11 straight points to lead 41-39, and won the quarter 20-4. They led by as many as nine, 52-43, early in the fourth.

New Zealand came back. Keija Miringaorangi made two free throws with 55.4 seconds left to put her team ahead 64-63. Rhiane Perez answered with a layup and a foul with 49.8 seconds left, and the Philippines led 65-64. They did not trail again. Zaydhen Rosano and Grace Curley closed it out at the free-throw line in the last 24 seconds.

Rosano led the Philippines with 23 points and seven rebounds. Perez had 13 points and six rebounds. Miringaorangi scored 17 for New Zealand and Madelin Eliadis 14.

New Zealand finished 0-3 and play Malaysia, fourth in Group B, for seventh place on Thursday. The loser of that game is relegated to Division B for 2028.

## The groups

**Group A.** Australia 3-0: 102-47 over the Philippines, 99-34 over New Zealand and 92-56 over Korea. Korea 2-1, after beating New Zealand 74-71 and the Philippines 86-70. The Philippines 1-2. New Zealand 0-3.

**Group B.** Japan 3-0: 83-55 over Chinese Taipei, 136-29 over Malaysia and 73-68 over China on Tuesday for first place. China 2-1, after beating Malaysia 116-30 and Chinese Taipei 95-46. Chinese Taipei 1-2, with a 106-36 win over Malaysia. Malaysia 0-3.

The format sends the two group winners straight to the semi-finals. The teams that finished second and third cross over for the other two places, second in one group against third in the other, so Korea meet Chinese Taipei and China meet the Philippines.

By winning the group, Australia also earned a place at the FIBA U19 Women's Basketball World Cup in 2027.

## What is next

On Thursday, October 8, Korea play Chinese Taipei and China play the Philippines in the Qualification to Semi-Finals, and New Zealand play Malaysia for seventh place. The semi-finals are on Friday, October 9, with Australia and Japan waiting, and the final is on Saturday, October 10.
