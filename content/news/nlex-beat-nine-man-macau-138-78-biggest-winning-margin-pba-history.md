The Governors' Cup came back on Wednesday after nearly eight weeks off, and NLEX used the first game of the restart to set a PBA record. The Road Warriors beat Macau 138-78 at the Ninoy Aquino Stadium. The 60-point margin is the biggest in league history. The old mark was 56, set when Rain or Shine beat Blackwater 151-95 on March 31.

The win put NLEX at 8-2, alone at the top of Group A, and clinched them a place in the quarterfinals. In the second game of the night, TNT beat Terrafirma 116-92 to move to 5-4.

## Nine players and no head coach

Macau came out of the break short-handed. Damian Chongqui, Jenning Leung and Ramon Cao signed with East Asia Super League clubs during the break, and the import DeVondre Perry was replaced by Oscar Lopez. Head coach Marcus Elliott was not on the bench.

Nine Macau players took the floor. Lopez and Kobey Lam played all 48 minutes. Lopez scored 36 points with 13 rebounds, and Lam had 22 points, eight rebounds and seven assists. The other seven players scored 20 points between them.

## Every quarter went to NLEX

NLEX led 38-23 after one quarter and 70-43 at half-time. The lead was 44 after three quarters, at 105-61, and NLEX won the fourth 33-17 to pass the old record.

Every quarter went to NLEX by at least 12 points. The biggest was the third, 35-18.

Schonny Winston led NLEX with 25 points. Deonte Burton, the import brought in for the injured DeQuan Jones, had 22 points, 14 rebounds, eight assists, six steals and two blocks in his PBA debut. Robert Bolick added 14, and 14 of the 16 NLEX players who got into the game scored.

## TNT pull away from Terrafirma

TNT were playing with Calvin Oftana and Jayson Castro back for the first time since the Commissioner's Cup Finals in June. Oftana scored 31, and Nick Hornsby added 26.

TNT led 27-22 after the first quarter and won the second 30-20 for a 57-42 half-time lead. The margin reached 17 after three quarters, 79-62, and TNT won the fourth 37-30.

Terrafirma had won the teams' previous meeting 111-106. On Wednesday Terrafirma's Juami Tiongson did not score. Maverick Ahanmisi had 27 points and Justin Strings 25 for Terrafirma, who fall to 3-6.

Four teams from each group reach the quarterfinals. After Wednesday, Group A reads:
