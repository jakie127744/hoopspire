[Chelsea Gray](/player/WNBA/2529122) had the ball two feet from the basket with a chance to tie it. [Kiah Stokes](/player/WNBA/2590093) blocked her reverse layup with 3.0 seconds left, [Veronica Burton](/player/WNBA/4398935) grabbed the rebound, and the Golden State Valkyries beat the Las Vegas Aces 83-81 on Wednesday night at Chase Center.

Golden State lead the best-of-five WNBA semifinal 2-0 and are one win from the WNBA Finals. Stokes did not score in 29 minutes. That block was her only one of the night.

## A 17-point lead, gone

[Kayla Thornton](/player/WNBA/2529622) opened the scoring with a three, and the Valkyries did not trail again until 1:36 was left in the game. They led 42-31 at the half. With 1:47 left in the third quarter, two Thornton free throws made it 64-47, the biggest lead of the night.

Las Vegas cut it to 66-55 by the end of the third. Golden State still led 70-61 with 8:08 left. Down 74-68 with 3:35 left, the Aces scored eight straight. [Dana Evans](/player/WNBA/4281190) and Gray hit threes from 23 feet to tie it 74-74 with 2:10 left. [A'ja Wilson](/player/WNBA/3149391) drove for a layup with 1:36 left, and Las Vegas led 76-74. It was their only lead of the game.

The Aces won the fourth quarter 26-17.

## Burton from 28 feet

[Gabby Williams](/player/WNBA/3142328) tied it with a 13-foot pull-up with 1:25 left. After Gray missed a three, Williams found Burton, and Burton hit from 28 feet for a 79-76 lead with 41.5 seconds left.

Every point in the last 9.2 seconds came at the free throw line. Gray made two to cut it to 79-78. Thornton made two, [Jackie Young](/player/WNBA/4065870) made two, and Burton made two for an 83-80 lead with 6.4 seconds left. Young made the first of two with 5.9 seconds left and missed the second. Gray grabbed the offensive rebound and went up for the layup that Stokes blocked.

## Hayes off the bench

[Tiffany Hayes](/player/WNBA/1054) led Golden State with 21 points in 17 minutes off the bench, on 6-of-10 shooting and 8-of-8 at the free throw line. Seven of them came in the third quarter, when the lead grew to 17. Williams scored 20 and Thornton 16. The Valkyries made 25 of 27 free throws and took 10 offensive rebounds to the Aces' six.

Wilson scored 24 points on 9-of-16 shooting with seven rebounds. Gray had 19 points and six assists, and Young 14 points and eight assists. [Jewell Loyd](/player/WNBA/2987869), who left Game 1 with a leg injury, did not play.

Las Vegas won all three regular-season meetings. Golden State have now won the first two in the playoffs, 71-60 and 83-81. The crowd at Chase Center was 18,064.

## What is next

The series moves to Michelob Ultra Arena in Las Vegas for Game 3 on Friday, October 9, at 9:30 p.m. ET on Peacock and NBCSN. A Golden State win sends the Valkyries to the WNBA Finals. If the Aces win, Game 4 is in Las Vegas on Sunday, October 11, at a time not yet set. A Game 5 would be at Chase Center on Wednesday, October 14.
