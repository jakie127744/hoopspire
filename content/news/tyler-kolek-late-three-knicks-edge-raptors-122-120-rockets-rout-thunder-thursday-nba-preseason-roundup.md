Two nights after losing to Toronto by 50, the New York Knicks got their rematch at Madison Square Garden on Thursday, and it came down to the last shot. With the score tied 117-117, [Tyler Kolek](/player/NBA/4433225) pulled up from 28 feet and hit it with 53.2 seconds left. The Knicks held on to beat the Raptors 122-120 in a preseason game.

On Tuesday in Toronto, New York's starters did not play and the Raptors won [148-98, leading by as many as 53](/story/zhaire-smith-overtime-jumper-thunder-beat-pacers-114-112-raptors-rout-knicks-by-50-tuesday-nba-preseason-roundup). This time [Jalen Brunson](/player/NBA/3934672), [Karl-Anthony Towns](/player/NBA/3136195), [OG Anunoby](/player/NBA/3934719) and [Mikal Bridges](/player/NBA/3147657) all started. In Houston, the Rockets beat the Oklahoma City Thunder 116-89. These games do not count in the standings.

## Knicks 122, Raptors 120

New York led most of the night. The Knicks went up 35-32 after one quarter, by 10 at 47-37 in the second, and 87-79 after three. Then Toronto scored the first 10 points of the fourth, the last two on [RJ Barrett](/player/NBA/4395625) free throws, to tie it 89-89 with 9:44 left. There were nine ties and six lead changes in all.

Kolek's 25-foot three made it 115-109 with 2:58 left, and his lob to [James Wiseman](/player/NBA/4432808) made it 117-111. Toronto came back again. [Malachi Smith](/player/NBA/4397784) hit a 23-footer, and [Jaden Bradley](/player/NBA/4432737) scored on a pull-up and made the free throw to tie it 117-117 with 1:03 left. Kolek answered with his long three, and Tyler Nickel made two free throws for a 122-117 lead with 16.8 seconds left.

Smith hit another three with 11.3 seconds left to cut it to 122-120. Pacome Dadiet then turned it over in the backcourt, giving the Raptors one more chance, but Bradley missed a driving layup with 2.4 seconds left and time ran out.

Brunson led New York with 20 points and seven assists. Towns had 13 points, 15 rebounds and nine assists, and [Andre Drummond](/player/NBA/6585) scored 14 off the bench on 6-of-7 shooting. Kolek finished with six points, both baskets from three. For Toronto, [Scottie Barnes](/player/NBA/4433134) scored 23 on 9-of-15, Barrett had 21 and eight rebounds, and [Immanuel Quickley](/player/NBA/4395724) added 18 points and seven assists. [Kawhi Leonard](/player/NBA/6450) played 23 minutes and scored six. The crowd was 19,812.

## Rockets 116, Thunder 89

Oklahoma City led 8-6 early. Then Houston scored 20 points in a row, from 8-6 down to 26-8 up, and led 33-15 after one quarter. The Thunder got within 50-47 late in the second quarter, but the Rockets scored the next 16 points, four before half-time and 12 after it, for a 66-47 lead. The lead reached 29 at 116-87 in the final minute.

[Bogdan Bogdanovic](/player/NBA/3037789) led Houston with 15 points off the bench, all five of his baskets from three. [Alperen Sengun](/player/NBA/4871144) had 14 points, seven rebounds and five assists, [Amen Thompson](/player/NBA/4684740) and [Tari Eason](/player/NBA/4433192) scored 12 each, and [Kevin Durant](/player/NBA/3202) had 11 in 16 minutes. [Fred VanVleet](/player/NBA/2991230) handed out eight assists. Oklahoma City made 5 of 36 threes. [Jalen Williams](/player/NBA/4593803) scored 18 and [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](/player/NBA/4278073) 17 in 26 minutes.
