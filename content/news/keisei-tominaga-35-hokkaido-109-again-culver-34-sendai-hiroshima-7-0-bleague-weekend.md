Keisei Tominaga had 35 points by the time Levanga Hokkaido were done with the Yokohama B-Corsairs at Yokohama Buntai on Sunday. He made 12 of 21 shots and all seven of his free throws in 28 minutes, and Hokkaido won 109-78 in front of 4,906. On Friday night at Saga Arena, they had beaten the Saga Ballooners 109-90.

That made two road wins and 218 points in three days. Hokkaido are 5-3 and have won two in a row.

It was a 13-game weekend in the B.League Premier. Sendai came from 13 down to beat Utsunomiya 85-81, Hiroshima won 89-71 at Altiri Chiba to stay unbeaten at 7-0, and Alvark Tokyo won 100-86 at Gunma for a sixth straight win.

## Hokkaido 109, Yokohama 78

**The first quarter.** Yokohama never led by more than a point. Tominaga scored on a fast-break layup and a three inside the first five minutes, and Hokkaido led 32-20 after one.

**The pull-away.** Hokkaido won all four quarters, and the lead reached 31 at 101-70 with 3:11 left. Gary Clark added 22 points and seven rebounds, and Dwight Ramos and John Harrar had 14 each. Maik Kotsar made all nine of his free throws and scored 19 for Yokohama.

On Friday, Hokkaido led Saga by as many as 23. Dwight Ramos scored 23 on 5-of-8 from three, and Clark and Harrar had 17 each, Harrar with 15 rebounds. Nassir Little scored 35 for Saga and Damyean Dotson 25 with 10 rebounds.

## Sendai 85, Utsunomiya 81

**The hole.** Utsunomiya led 31-18 in the second quarter at Xebio Arena Sendai on Saturday and 45-34 at half-time.

**The turn.** Sendai won the third quarter 27-13. Sergio El Darwich scored on layups to tie it 58-58, and Jarrett Culver's three-point play put Sendai ahead 61-58 with 29 seconds left in the quarter.

**Culver's stretch.** With Sendai up 71-65 in the fourth, Culver scored 10 straight Sendai points: two layups, then two threes, the last for an 81-69 lead with 2:51 left. Utsunomiya kept coming. Seiji Ikaruga's three cut it to 84-81 with 11 seconds left. Culver made one of two free throws, and Trey Kell III missed a three.

Culver finished with 34 points and eight rebounds, and El Darwich had 21 and made all eight of his free throws. Kell scored 26 for Utsunomiya and Otis Frazier III 20. Sendai have won four in a row and are 5-3. Utsunomiya are 3-4. The crowd was 4,708.

## Hiroshima 89, Altiri Chiba 71

Altiri Chiba led 16-11 early at Chibagin Arena. Hiroshima outscored them 19-5 over the rest of the first quarter, led 30-21, and were never caught. Kofi Cockburn made all 10 of his shots for 22 points and had 11 rebounds, and Christopher Smith added 19 points and eight rebounds. Alex Davis scored 16 for Altiri Chiba and Kotetsu Kurokawa 14 off the bench. Kai Sotto did not play. Hiroshima are 7-0 and top the table.

## Yokohama 87, Ryukyu 85

On Friday, four days after [beating the Chiba Jets in overtime](/story/isaiah-livers-overtime-three-yokohama-beat-chiba-jets-108-106-arai-33-bleague-monday), Yokohama trailed 62-60 after three quarters and won the fourth 27-23. They led 87-83 when Jack Cooley scored on a putback at the buzzer. James Bell scored 23 off the bench and Isaiah Livers 21, and Kotsar had 19 points, 13 rebounds and six assists. David Nwaba scored 28 for the Golden Kings.

## The rest of the weekend

**Ibaraki 82, Kawasaki 74 (Friday).** Vic Law scored 30 and made 11 of 12 free throws. On Sunday, Ibaraki beat Osaka 98-63 at home, with Ray Parks Jr. scoring 18 off the bench and four players scoring 16 or more.

**Akita 102, Shinshu 95.** Yanni Wetzell had 31 points and 12 rebounds, and Mark Smith scored 27, 10 of them from the line.

**Alvark Tokyo 100, Gunma 86.** Gunma led 55-52 at half-time. Alvark won the second half 48-31, and Keyontae Johnson scored 30, 14 of them on free throws. Alvark are 7-1.

**Chiba Jets 97, Shimane 78.** Yuta Watanabe scored 22 and Riku Segawa 21 in front of 9,690 at LaLa Arena Tokyo-Bay, the biggest crowd of the weekend.

**SunRockers 95, Nagasaki 63.** Rawle Alkins scored 20 and Donte Grantham had 19 points and six assists.

**Mikawa 99, Toyama 56.** Mikawa won the third quarter 36-16, and Aaron White had 21 points and 14 rebounds off the bench. Toyama scored eight points in the fourth.

**Kyoto 115, Saga 70.** Angelo Caloiaro had 29 points and 10 assists in 23 minutes and made 5 of 6 threes. Michael Phillips added 18 points and 10 rebounds off the bench.
