The Daegu Korea Gas Corporation led Goyang Sono 81-71 with 5:31 left on Thursday night. They did not score again for 3 minutes and 20 seconds, and Sono scored 13 straight points to take an 84-81 lead. Sono won 88-87 at Daegu Gymnasium in front of 2,603 fans.

[Lee Jung-hyun](/player/KBL/kbl-291488) scored 31 points for Sono and [Johnny O'Bryant III](/player/KBL/rg-24170) scored 28. Sono is 6-9 and has won two straight. Korea Gas is 6-8 and has lost two in a row.

## A close first half

Korea Gas led 24-20 after the first quarter. Sono scored 26 in the second quarter, and Korea Gas still led 47-46 at half-time. The third quarter was close too: Korea Gas 22, Sono 21, and a 69-67 lead for the home side going into the fourth.

[Sam Belangel](/player/KBL/kbl-291556) and [Keve Aluma](/player/KBL/kbl-291680) led Korea Gas through the middle of the game. Belangel scored 21 points with seven assists, and Aluma scored 20 and made all six of his two-point shots. [Ra Gun-ah](/player/KBL/kbl-290524) scored 14 points with seven rebounds in under 21 minutes.

## The 10-point lead

Korea Gas opened the fourth quarter with a basket from [Kim Min-gyu](/player/KBL/kbl-291863) for 71-67. Belangel hit a three at 8:38 for 74-67, and [Kim Jun-yl](/player/KBL/kbl-290781) made it 76-67 at 7:59. Aluma's basket and free throw at 6:25 made it 79-69, and Kim Min-gyu's basket at 5:31 made it 81-71. That was the biggest lead of the game, 10 points.

## Sono's 13-0 run

O'Bryant had already cut the lead twice, with baskets at 7:35 and 6:03. Then Sono scored 13 straight points. [Kevin Quiambao](/player/KBL/rg-200302) hit a three at 5:12 to make it 81-74. Lee scored at 4:30 and hit a three at 4:02, 81-79. O'Bryant tied it with a basket at 3:16, and his three at 2:35 put Sono ahead 84-81.

Korea Gas did not score between 5:31 and 2:11. Belangel ended the drought at 2:11 with a basket and a free throw, 84-84. Lee made two free throws at 1:23, 86-84, and a basket at 0:44, 88-84.

Belangel made one of two free throws with 16 seconds left, 88-85. Aluma then scored with seven seconds to go, 88-87. The official record shows no more points after that, and Sono held on.

## The numbers behind it

Lee made 4 of 6 threes, 7 of 11 two-point shots and all five of his free throws, and added six assists. O'Bryant made 4 of 9 threes and 7 of 12 twos, and had five rebounds and three steals. Quiambao scored 9 points with eight rebounds and three assists.

Sono made 13 of 28 threes, 39 of its 88 points, and Korea Gas made 9 of 23. Sono had nine steals to Korea Gas's two, and 6 turnovers to 12. The official team record credits Sono with 20 points off turnovers and Korea Gas with eight. Korea Gas made more free throws, 14 of 19 to Sono's 9 of 12, and had more rebounds, 29 to 24.

[Olufemi Olujobi](/player/KBL/kbl-291889) scored 8 on two threes. [Jo Seok-ho](/player/KBL/kbl-291391) had five points, three assists and three steals, and five fouls.
