Duke never led. No. 5 Michigan State went up 2-0 on a dunk by [Anton Bonke](/player/NCAAM/5240529) in the first minute on Tuesday night, and after one early tie the Spartans never trailed in a 79-73 win over No. 2 Duke at the United Center.

Michigan State is 3-0. Duke is 2-1 and has lost for the first time this season.

The Spartans led 34-25 at half-time and by as many as 14 in the second half. Duke won the second half 48-45, but it never got closer than five points after the break.

## A nine-point start

The game was 2-2 about a minute in, after [John Blackwell](/player/NCAAM/5103630) made a turnaround jumper. Michigan State scored the next nine points. [Jeremy Fears Jr.](/player/NCAAM/4711255) made a free throw, then set up a jumper by [Jasiah Jervis](/player/NCAAM/5210837), a three by [Coen Carr](/player/NCAAM/4872740) and a three by Jervis. It was 11-2 with 17:30 left in the first half.

Duke got back to within two, 13-15, with 9:29 left in the half, and never closer. The Spartans took a 34-25 lead into the break.

## The 14-point lead

Michigan State's biggest lead came early in the second half, 50-36 with 13:37 left. [Cam Ward](/player/NCAAM/5101798) scored 14 points off the bench on 5 of 7 shooting and made all four of his free throws. Jervis finished with 10 and Carr with nine in 17 minutes.

Duke came back one possession at a time. It cut the lead to 73-68 with 2:03 left, and [Jordan Scott](/player/NCAAM/5196931) answered with a driving layup at 1:50 for 75-68. Scott also had nine rebounds and six blocks.

## The last minute

Michigan State turned the ball over on a shot-clock violation with a minute left. Fears made both free throws at 0:39 for 77-68, and one of two at 0:23 for 78-68. [Patrick Ngongba II](/player/NCAAM/4873209) made two free throws at 0:22 for 78-70, and Blackwell made a three at 0:13 for 78-73 on an assist from [Caleb Foster](/player/NCAAM/4711256). Fears made one of two at 0:12 for the final score.

## The numbers behind it

Fears had 18 points, 11 assists, seven rebounds and four turnovers. He made 3 of 9 shots from the field and 12 of 17 free throws. Michigan State made 22 of 28 free throws, and Duke made 26 of 37.

Duke shot 20 of 60 from the field and 7 of 29 from three. Blackwell led the Blue Devils with 17 points, but he made 6 of 16 shots and 4 of 11 threes. Ngongba had 11 points, seven rebounds, six assists and four blocks, and made 7 of 13 free throws. Duke had 18 offensive rebounds to Michigan State's nine, and the two teams each turned the ball over 13 times.

Michigan State shot 25 of 56, 45 percent, and 7 of 20 from three. It had 34 points in the paint to Duke's 26.
