Antonio Reeves drove into the lane, floated the ball up from seven feet and gave the Minnesota Timberwolves a 125-124 preseason win at Miami with 2.3 seconds left on Saturday night. Ryan Conwell's 26-foot three in the last second missed.

It was one of seven preseason games on Saturday. Washington beat Detroit 114-108 in overtime, Atlanta won 119-117 at Indiana, and Boston beat Philadelphia 144-106. Toronto, Golden State and Phoenix also won. These games do not count in the standings, and several teams rested their stars.

## Timberwolves 125, Heat 124

Minnesota led by 14 in the third quarter, at 76-62, and Miami came back in the fourth. The Heat tied it 121-121 on a Conwell jumper with 47.8 seconds left, after he stole the ball from Zyon Pullin. Rocco Zikarsky's layup put Minnesota back in front, and Vladislav Goldin's three-point play with 14.8 seconds left gave Miami a 124-123 lead. Then Reeves scored.

Bones Hyland led Minnesota with 23 points and Pullin had 22. Conwell had 22 points and nine assists for Miami, and Goldin 18. Jonathan Kuminga and Donte DiVincenzo did not play for Minnesota, and Bam Adebayo and Bobby Portis sat for Miami, all as coach's decisions. The crowd at Kaseya Center was 19,600.

## Wizards 114, Pistons 108 (overtime)

Washington led 40-20 after the first quarter. Detroit came all the way back and led by seven, 89-82, with 8:17 to play. The Wizards were ahead 98-96 when Isaac Jones dunked with 11.7 seconds left to tie it and force overtime.

In overtime, Will Riley's two free throws broke a 106-106 tie, and Justin Champagnie, Chris Livingston and Felix Okpara scored the last three Washington baskets. The Wizards won the extra period 16-10.

Riley and Tre Johnson scored 15 each for Washington. AJ Dybantsa started and had 14 points and seven rebounds. Anthony Davis scored 10, and Trae Young two. Ebuka Okorie led Detroit with 14 points and six assists. Cade Cunningham and Jalen Duren did not play.
