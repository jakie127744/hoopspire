Dubai Basketball beat Real Madrid 78-77 at Coca-Cola Arena on Thursday in the first round of the EuroLeague regular season. Madrid had won the [first EuroLeague SuperCup](/story/real-madrid-win-first-euroleague-supercup) in Abu Dhabi five days earlier. It led this game 77-71 with 3:06 to play and did not score again.

Andrés Feliz's basket gave Madrid its six-point lead. Dubai then scored the last seven points of the game. Elie Okobo scored inside, Mfiondu Kabengele made two free throws after an offensive rebound, and McKinley Wright IV hit a three from a Dwayne Bacon assist with 51 seconds left to make it 78-77. Over those three minutes Madrid missed six shots and committed one turnover. Facundo Campazzo missed a two with 32 seconds left, and Feliz missed another with two seconds left.

It was the second comeback of the night for Xavi Pascual's side. Madrid led 44-33 with 48 seconds left in the second quarter, its biggest lead. Dubai scored the next 14 points: Bacon made two free throws, Kabengele hit a three just before halftime, Okobo scored the first three baskets of the third quarter, and Bacon finished a three-point play to make it 47-44.

Bacon led all scorers with 24 points. He made nine of his 11 two-point attempts and six of seven free throws in 29 minutes. Wright scored 12, and Kabengele had nine points and seven rebounds. Tornike Shengelia scored five points but took five offensive rebounds in 15 minutes off the bench.

## The third quarter and the long fourth

Dubai won the third quarter 25-18 and led 63-62 going into the fourth, and a Thomas Walkup basket at 8:43 made it 65-62. Madrid then scored 10 straight points, from 65-62 to 65-72 between 8:28 and 5:11, with Facundo Campazzo and Mikael Jantunen scoring twice each and Walter Tavares adding a basket. Dubai cut it to 70-72 on a Filip Petrušev three at 4:12, and Feliz answered with a three at 3:47 to make it 70-75 before Mfiondu Kabengele's free throw and Feliz's last basket at 3:06 set up the 77-71 lead. After that Madrid did not score, and Dubai closed on a 7-0 run.

Dubai finished plus-20 with Bacon on the floor. Dāvis Bertāns, who made one of two threes for three points in 11 minutes, was minus-19. Kabengele was plus-12 in 24 minutes and Petrušev plus-10 in 19.

## The numbers

Dubai took 18 offensive rebounds, twice Madrid's nine and the most by any team in Thursday's seven games. It made 17 of 40 two-point shots and committed 15 turnovers to Madrid's eight. Madrid shot 25 of 50 inside the arc. Dubai made up the gap from outside and at the line: 10 of 28 from three against Madrid's six of 18, and 14 of 18 free throws against nine of 11.

Timothé Luwawu-Cabarrot and Théo Maledon led Madrid with 11 points each, and Maledon added six assists. Mikael Jantunen and Damian Jones scored 10 each. Jones made five of seven shots in 15 minutes and took four offensive rebounds, and Tavares had four fouls in 21 minutes. Dubai had 17 assists to Madrid's 13, and Elie Okobo, who started, had eight points on 4-of-6 shooting with four assists and four rebounds in 30 minutes.
