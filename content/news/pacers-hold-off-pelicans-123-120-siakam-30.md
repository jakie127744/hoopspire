[Tyrese Haliburton](/player/NBA/4396993) drove into the lane with 1:23 left, floated in an 11-footer, was fouled by [Zion Williamson](/player/NBA/4395628) and made the free throw. That made it 121-118, and the Indiana Pacers held on to beat the New Orleans Pelicans 123-120 on Wednesday at the Smoothie King Center in the opener for both teams.

There were 13 lead changes and 10 ties. Five of the ties came in the last 6:07.

[Pascal Siakam](/player/NBA/3149673) led Indiana with 30 points on 13-of-23 shooting and eight rebounds. Haliburton had 23 points and 12 assists, [Obi Toppin](/player/NBA/4278355) scored 20 off the bench with four threes, and [Ivica Zubac](/player/NBA/4017837) had 16 points and 14 rebounds.

[Dejounte Murray](/player/NBA/3907497) scored 23 for New Orleans and Williamson 22.

## The halves

Indiana scored 10 straight midway through the first quarter and led 38-31 after it. New Orleans answered with 12 straight to open the second and won the quarter 35-18. The Pelicans led 66-56 at halftime, their biggest lead, and 92-86 after three.

## How Indiana got back

A three by [Herbert Jones](/player/NBA/4277813) and a floater by Derik Queen helped New Orleans to a 101-93 lead with 8:34 left. Haliburton then scored five straight on two free throws and a three-point play, and Zubac's alley-oop dunk from Siakam made it 101-100.

Indiana took the lead with eight straight points. Siakam scored on a layup, Quenton Jackson tied it on a running layup at 104, and [Andrew Nembhard](/player/NBA/4395712) drove for a layup and made two free throws for 108-104 with 5:06 left.

## The finish

The next three minutes were traded baskets. Siakam scored from eight feet, 14 feet, 10 feet and on a layup between 4:00 and 2:32, and New Orleans answered three of the four, the last a three-point play by Williamson that tied it at 116 with 2:16 left. Haliburton's floater made it 118-116, and Yves Missi tied it again with a dunk at 1:40.

After Haliburton's three-point play, Missi dunked again to cut it to 121-120 with 1:11 left. Murray blocked Zubac at the rim, but Jackson got the rebound and was fouled, and he made both free throws with 45.7 seconds left. Williamson missed a driving layup. Nembhard traveled with 14.2 seconds left, giving New Orleans the ball down three. Jeremiah Fears missed a 27-foot three, Williamson got the rebound and missed from 26 feet, Zubac missed two free throws with 1.4 seconds left, and Trey Murphy III's heave at the buzzer was off.

## What it means

Indiana won the game in the paint and in transition, 64-48 in points in the paint and 27-10 on the fast break. It needed both: New Orleans made 34 of 37 free throws to Indiana's 20 of 29. The Pelicans also out-rebounded Indiana 50-42 and had 15 offensive rebounds. Missi had 13 points, 14 rebounds and three blocks. Siakam, Haliburton and Nembhard combined for 71 points and 22 assists.
