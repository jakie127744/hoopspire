Australia and Japan will play for the FIBA U18 Women's Asia Cup 2026 title on Saturday in Seremban, Malaysia, after one-sided semi-finals at the Karisma Arena on Friday. Australia beat China 78-59 and Japan beat Korea 80-43.

China, champions 16 times and runners-up to Australia in the last two editions, will not be in the final for the first time since 1990. Australia are chasing a third straight title. Japan reach the final for the first time since 2018 and last won it in 2008.

## Australia 78, China 59

Australia won the first quarter 31-9. They had a 16-0 run in the game and led by as many as 22. China won the second and third quarters, 19-12 and 18-16, and were 59-46 down going into the fourth. Australia won it 19-13.

Isabel Smith scored 22 points with five rebounds. Madison Ryan had 17 points and 12 rebounds, and Olivia Olechnowicz 14 points, five assists and three steals. He Siyao led China with 22, Li Yuanshan scored 16 and Zhao Zirui had 10 points and nine rebounds.

"Australia has won the last two Asia Cups so it's important for us to make sure that we continue to try to do that," Australia coach Kristi Harrower said.

## Japan 80, Korea 43

Japan led 40-24 at half-time and won every quarter. Their biggest lead was 38, and they had a 14-0 run. Korea, who had beaten Chinese Taipei 67-64 a day earlier, made 11 of 43 shots from the field.

Marin Koike scored 15 points with five steals, Riko Katsube 13, Moe Sakata 12 off the bench and Yuuka Mizubayashi 10 points with nine rebounds. Cho Heewon scored 14 for Korea.

## Chinese Taipei 86, Philippines 55

The game for fifth place carried the last ticket to the FIBA U19 Women's Basketball World Cup 2027. Chinese Taipei led 28-15 after one quarter and 49-31 at half-time. They finished the tournament 2-3 and join China, the hosts, Australia, Japan and Korea at the World Cup, which is set for Chengdu on July 17 to 25, 2027.

Chen Jou-an scored 12 with six assists, Chang Yu-tung 11 and Lin Nian-zhen 10. Grace Marina Curley led the Philippines with 15 points and nine rebounds, KJ Guada Dave Badajos scored 12, Rhiane Meshelle Perez 11 and Tifanny Anne Ablan Reyes 10. The Philippines finished sixth at 1-4 in their first Division A tournament.

## What is next

Saturday, October 10, local time in Seremban (GMT+8): China v Korea for third place, and Australia v Japan in the final at 19:00. It is only the third meeting between Australia and Japan in this competition.
