The Atlanta Dream trailed the New York Liberty 43-39 less than a minute into the second half on Sunday. Then they scored the next 13 points. Atlanta won the third quarter 32-15 and beat New York 92-82 at State Farm Arena to take Game 1 of the WNBA semifinals. The Dream lead the best-of-five series 1-0.

[Jordin Canada](/player/WNBA/3142250) led Atlanta with 25 points on 9-of-14 shooting, four of six threes, with five steals. [DeWanna Bonner](/player/WNBA/869) scored 23 off the bench. [Sabrina Ionescu](/player/WNBA/4066533) scored 27 for New York.

## The third quarter

New York led 40-37 at halftime and pushed it to 43-39 on a [Leonie Fiebich](/player/WNBA/4683006) step-back three at 9:13 of the third.

[Rhyne Howard](/player/WNBA/4398674) drove for a layup. Canada hit a three from Howard at 8:27 to put Atlanta in front 44-43, and the Dream never trailed again. Bonner and [Angel Reese](/player/WNBA/4433402) scored two baskets each, and Reese's layup at 5:46 made it 52-43, a 13-0 run.

Canada scored 12 points in the quarter: two free throws, the three, a layup and the free throw that came with it, and two more layups. A [Te-Hina Paopao](/player/WNBA/4433431) layup with nine seconds left made it 69-55 going into the fourth.

## The Liberty push back

Bonner's basket at 9:34 of the fourth put Atlanta up 16, 71-55, the biggest lead of the game. [Breanna Stewart](/player/WNBA/2998928) scored five and [Jonquel Jones](/player/WNBA/2999101) hit a three in an 8-0 run, and Stewart's three at 7:56 made it 71-63.

New York got within five three times. Fiebich's three at 5:19 made it 75-70, and Canada answered with a three from Howard at 4:55. Jones's three at 4:00 made it 80-75, and Reese answered with a driving layup. Ionescu's 29-foot three with 51.7 seconds left made it 87-82. Howard made a free throw, Canada scored on a layup from Howard at 0:30, and Howard made two more free throws for the final margin.

## The numbers

New York turned the ball over 19 times to Atlanta's 13, and the Dream had 14 steals to six. Atlanta scored 18 fast-break points and New York none. The Dream outscored the Liberty 38-30 in the paint. New York made all 15 of its free throws and 11 of 28 threes, one more three than Atlanta.

Bonner made 10 of 18 shots and had nine rebounds. Reese had 17 points, seven rebounds, four steals and three blocks. Howard scored 16 on 5-of-20 shooting, with eight rebounds and eight assists. [Naz Hillmon](/player/WNBA/4398915) did not score in 37 minutes, and Atlanta was plus-14 with her on the floor, the best mark in the game.

Ionescu made 10 of 20 shots and three of eight threes, and turned the ball over five times. Stewart scored 19, making all six free throws, with three steals and three blocks. Jones had 12 points, 11 rebounds and five blocks.

Atlanta won two of the three regular-season meetings. The crowd was 9,745.
