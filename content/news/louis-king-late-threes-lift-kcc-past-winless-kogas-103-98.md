Daegu Korea Gas Corporation had cut a 16-point deficit to 100-98 with 46 seconds left when [Louis King](/player/KBL/kbl-291884) took the shot that settled it. His three with 26 seconds to go, his second in the final 75 seconds, gave the Busan KCC Egis a 103-98 win at Busan Sajik Gymnasium on Wednesday night in front of 2,009 fans.

King came off the bench and scored 29 points on 10-of-14 shooting, with four threes. KCC are 2-1. KOGAS, known as the Pegasus, are 0-3, the only team in the league with three losses.

## First quarter: Jeon's four threes

KOGAS started hot from deep. [Jeon Hyun-woo](/player/KBL/kbl-291194) made four threes in the space of 3:36, and the last of them, with 1:39 left, put KOGAS ahead 32-24, their biggest lead of the night. Those were his only 12 points of the game. The quarter had six ties and five lead changes, and KOGAS led 32-30 after one.

## Second quarter: KCC take over

KCC won the second quarter 28-18. King's dunk tied it at 39 with 7:25 left in the quarter. [Kim Dong-hyun](/player/KBL/kbl-291490) hit a three for a 42-39 lead at 5:55, and KCC never trailed again. [Shawn Long](/player/KBL/kbl-rg-28798) finished that 9-0 run with a dunk, scored three more baskets before the break, and KCC led 58-50 at half-time.

## Third quarter: the lead reaches 16

After [Heo Ung](/player/KBL/kbl-290776) opened the half with a basket, [Heo Hoon](/player/KBL/kbl-291085) and King scored KCC's next 10 points between them. King's basket with 6:01 left in the quarter made it 70-54, KCC's biggest lead. KOGAS chipped back with threes from [Yang Woo-hyuck](/player/KBL/kbl-291862) and [Kim Min-gyu](/player/KBL/kbl-291863), and Heo Hoon's three with one second left made it 87-78 after three quarters.

## Fourth quarter: two threes to finish it

KOGAS won the fourth 20-16. Kim Min-gyu's three made it 91-88 with 7:28 left. Heo Ung answered with a three for 96-88 at 6:24, and then KCC went more than five minutes with one free throw. [Sam Belangel](/player/KBL/kbl-291556) scored twice and [Keve Aluma](/player/KBL/kbl-291680) dunked to make it 97-94 with 2:05 left.

King hit a three at 1:15 for 100-94. Aluma and Belangel made two free throws each to cut it to two with 46 seconds left. King's next three ended it.

## The numbers

KCC outscored KOGAS 54-36 in the paint. Long made 13 of his 17 shots for 27 points with eight rebounds, though he made only one of six free throws. Heo Hoon had 14 points and 14 assists. KCC's bench scored 35 points to KOGAS's 26.

KOGAS made 15 threes to KCC's 12, on 39 attempts. Aluma had 30 points, 13 rebounds and seven assists, but also six of KOGAS's 14 turnovers. Belangel, Yang and Kim Min-gyu scored 15 each.

## What is next
