The Boston Celtics were 22 points down in Toronto on Wednesday night, and they won anyway. They beat the Raptors 98-93 at Scotiabank Arena, their fifth win in a row, and they did it on the second night of a back-to-back.

Boston is 10-2 and first in the Eastern Conference. Toronto is 5-6 and has lost two straight.

The Celtics scored the last eight points of the game. They trailed 93-90 with 1:14 left after [RJ Barrett](/player/NBA/4395625) finished a dunk, and Toronto did not score again.

## Tatum scores 11 before halftime

Toronto led 38-29 after the first quarter and 59-37 with 5:24 left in the second, a lead of 22. Then [Jayson Tatum](/player/NBA/4065648) scored 11 points in two minutes, from 5:10 to 3:11: a three, two free throws, a dunk, another three and a free throw. [Baylor Scheierman](/player/NBA/4593841) hit a three and [Derrick White](/player/NBA/3078576) scored inside, and Boston had a 16-0 run that cut the gap to 59-53.

Toronto scored the next two points, and the Celtics went to halftime down 61-54. The third quarter was level at 19-19, so Boston was still seven behind, 80-73, going into the fourth.

## The fourth quarter

Toronto opened the fourth with a 9-point lead, 82-73, after [Allen Graves](/player/NBA/5149115) dunked. It was 89-81 with 5:13 left after [Ja'Kobe Walter](/player/NBA/4684272) made a three. Boston came back one possession at a time. [Sam Hauser](/player/NBA/4065804) made a three to cut it to 89-84. [Neemias Queta](/player/NBA/4397424) made a free throw to make it 89-88 with 2:47 left. Tatum's 12-foot jumper, set up by White, gave Boston its first lead since the third quarter, 90-89, with 1:55 to play.

Barrett answered with a floater and a dunk to make it 93-90. Then Boston scored the last eight points. Queta dunked off a Tatum pass with 54.1 seconds left. Tatum made two free throws at 25.2 seconds for a 94-93 lead. Queta made two at 15.1, and after [Jamison Battle](/player/NBA/4431893) threw a bad pass that Hauser stole, Hauser made two at 13.4 for the final margin. Queta then blocked [Immanuel Quickley](/player/NBA/4395724)'s driving layup with 4.4 seconds left.

Boston outscored Toronto 25-13 in the quarter. The Raptors shot 32 of 85 for the game and 16 of 43 from three. The Celtics shot 30 of 73 and 14 of 40.

## The numbers behind it

Tatum finished with 24 points, eight assists and seven rebounds. He made 6 of 21 shots from the field and 10 of 12 from the line. White had 19 points, seven assists and four blocks. [Payton Pritchard](/player/NBA/4066354) scored 14.

Queta had 11 points, 13 rebounds and three blocks, and Boston outscored Toronto by 27 with him on the floor. He also had five turnovers. The Celtics blocked 10 shots to Toronto's four, and they made 24 of 28 free throws to the Raptors' 13 of 18. Boston had 18 turnovers to Toronto's 15.

For Toronto, Walter scored 17 on five threes in 10 tries. Graves had 13 off the bench, with four offensive rebounds. Barrett scored 12 with eight rebounds and seven assists. Battle and [Alijah Martin](/player/NBA/4702656) each scored 12. Martin made four of five threes in 16 minutes. [Trayce Jackson-Davis](/player/NBA/4431684) did not play because of illness.

## Boston has been on the other side of this

On opening night, Boston led by 22 in Detroit and lost 121-113, as the [Pistons scored 23 straight points](/story/pistons-score-23-straight-come-back-from-22-down-beat-celtics-121-113) in the fourth quarter. This time the comeback went the other way.
