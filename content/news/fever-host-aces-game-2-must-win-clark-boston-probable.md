The Indiana Fever host the Las Vegas Aces on Tuesday in Game 2 of their first-round series, and a loss ends their season. Tipoff is at 6:30 p.m. ET at Gainbridge Fieldhouse, with the game on Prime Video.

The Aces lead the best-of-three series 1-0 after winning Game 1 on Sunday, 102-85. If Las Vegas wins Tuesday, it advances to the semifinals. If Indiana wins, the series goes to a deciding Game 3 in Las Vegas on Thursday.

## Who is expected to play

The Fever's preview lists Caitlin Clark and Kelsey Mitchell at guard, Lexie Hull and Makayla Timpson at forward and Aliyah Boston at center as probable starters. It is the same five that started Game 1.

The injury report, updated Monday afternoon, listed Clark as day-to-day with a back injury and Boston as day-to-day with a right lower leg injury. Neither player was ruled out. Guard Ty Harris broke her left fibula and will miss the rest of the season.

The Aces list Chelsea Gray and Jackie Young at guard, Stephanie Talbot and A'ja Wilson at forward and Brianna Turner at center. Aces guard Dana Evans is out with a knee injury.

## What happened in Game 1

Wilson scored 38 points on 15-of-19 shooting with 16 rebounds, and Las Vegas shot 59.4% from the field (38 of 64). Indiana shot 44.8% (30 of 67). The Aces led 62-47 at halftime and by as many as 27 in the third quarter.

Clark had 15 points and 10 assists but made 6 of 17 shots and had six turnovers. Mitchell made 4 of 16, including 1 of 6 from three. Together the two guards shot 10 of 33 from the field and 4 of 16 from three. Boston led the Fever with 18 points and eight rebounds in 25 minutes.

Indiana's 17 turnovers led to 28 Las Vegas points. Mitchell led the league in three-pointers made in the regular season, with 135. The Fever averaged a league-record 96.0 points a game during the regular season and scored 85 on Sunday.

## The series so far

Las Vegas entered as the No. 3 seed at 31-13 and Indiana as the No. 6 seed at 28-16. The Aces franchise has won 10 of 12 playoff series of at least three games after winning the opener. Las Vegas is 4-2 against Indiana in the two playoff series the teams have played.

The Fever's preview pointed to last season, when Indiana lost Game 1 of its first-round series at Atlanta, won Game 2 and then won Game 3 on the road to reach the semifinals.

Elsewhere in the first round, Golden State beat Dallas 104-80, New York won at Minnesota 91-75 and Atlanta beat Washington 92-77. The winner of this series will meet the winner of the Golden State and Dallas series. Golden State leads that one 1-0.

## What is next

Game 2 is Tuesday, September 29, at 6:30 p.m. ET in Indianapolis. If the Fever win, Game 3 is Thursday, October 1, in Las Vegas at 9 p.m. ET (6 p.m. PT), on USA. If the Aces win, the semifinals begin Sunday, October 4. Because the round is a best-of-three, a Fever loss on Tuesday ends Indiana's season, while an Aces win sends Las Vegas to the semifinals.
