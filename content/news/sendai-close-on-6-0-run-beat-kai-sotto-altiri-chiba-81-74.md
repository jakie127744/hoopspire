With 52 seconds left at Xebio Arena Sendai, Altiri Chiba were a point behind and the game had already been tied five times in the fourth quarter. They did not score again. [Nathan Boothe](/player/BLeague/51000144) finished a layup with 16 seconds left, [Seiya Funyu](/player/BLeague/8737) made two free throws with seven seconds left, and Boothe scored again with one second left. The Sendai 89ers beat Chiba 81-74 on Wednesday night in front of 4,279 fans.

Both teams came in at 1-3 in the B.League Premier. Sendai are now 2-3, Chiba 1-4.

## Chiba lead at the half

Sendai scored the first six points, the last three on a [Jarrett Culver](/player/BLeague/51000531) three. Chiba answered with eight straight, two of the baskets from [Derek Pardon](/player/BLeague/51000321), the first a dunk.

Chiba's passing ran the second quarter. They had eight assists in the period and made five of seven threes. [Kei Sugimoto](/player/BLeague/8520) hit a three with 50 seconds left for a 39-32 lead, Chiba's largest of the night, before [Jake Layman](/player/BLeague/51000313) answered from deep to make it 39-35 at half-time.

## Funyu's turn in the third

Funyu had not scored in the first half. He opened the third quarter with a three, added two free throws and another three, and Sendai ran off 15 of the first 17 points of the half. Layman's second three made it 50-41 with 5:25 left in the quarter, Sendai's biggest lead.

Chiba took it straight back with a 12-1 run. [Seiya Ando](/player/BLeague/8467) and [D.J. Newbill](/player/BLeague/33085) hit threes, and [Kai Sotto](/player/BLeague/51000259) scored the last four points of the quarter. His jumper in the paint with 1:23 left put Chiba ahead 53-51 going into the fourth.

## Seven lead changes in the fourth

Sendai opened the last quarter with eight straight points, from [Yusei Sugiura](/player/BLeague/10820), [Kazuma Okajima](/player/BLeague/51000133) and a three-point play by [Sergio El Darwich](/player/BLeague/51000530). Ando answered with seven points in a row inside a 9-0 Chiba run, and his layup made it 62-59 Chiba with 5:42 left.

The lead changed hands seven times in the quarter. Newbill made three free throws for a 68-66 Chiba lead with 2:50 left. Culver scored and was fouled to put Sendai back in front. Pardon dunked for 70-69. Okajima, who scored all seven of his points in the fourth, hit a three for 72-70. Newbill tied it at 72 with 1:24 left.

Layman's three-point play with 1:07 left made it 75-72, and Ando's basket with 52 seconds left cut it to one. That was Chiba's last basket.

## Where it was won

Chiba outrebounded Sendai 40-31 and had 21 assists to Sendai's 14. They lost the ball 17 times to Sendai's 10, and Sendai scored 16 points off those turnovers to Chiba's 11. Sendai also went 17 of 20 at the free-throw line. Chiba took eight free throws all night.

Culver led Sendai with 18 points, seven of them from the line. Funyu finished with 13 on 3-of-4 from three, and his team was 23 points better than Chiba in his 20 minutes. Ando led Chiba with 19 and Newbill had 17 with five assists.

Sotto finished with five points, 11 rebounds and five assists in 24 minutes.
