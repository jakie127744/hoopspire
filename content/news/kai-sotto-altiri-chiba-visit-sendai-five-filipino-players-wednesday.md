[Kai Sotto](/player/BLeague/51000259) has played two games for Altiri Chiba and had a double-double in both: 13 points and 11 rebounds in his debut win over Kawasaki, then 12 points and 14 rebounds in a loss the next day. On Wednesday night he gets his first road game, at the Sendai 89ers, at 7:05 p.m. Japan time.

It is a meeting of two teams at the same mark. Chiba and Sendai are both 1-3 in the B.League Premier.

Wednesday is a six-game midweek round, every game at 7:05 p.m., and five of the Filipino players in the league are on the schedule.

## Sotto's first weekend

Chiba had lost 76-74 at Gunma on September 24, the day the club announced Sotto's signing. The first basket of Sotto's Chiba career was a dunk, and the 7-foot-3 center finished his debut on Saturday, October 3, with Chiba coming back from 14 points down to beat the Kawasaki Brave Thunders 99-90 in front of 4,531 fans at Chibagin Arena. [Kotetsu Kurokawa](/player/BLeague/51000359) scored 30 that night.

On Sunday Kawasaki won 82-74 and led from late in the first quarter to the end. Sotto took 14 rebounds, five of them on the offensive end, and gave out four assists for the second game running.

## Sotto against Culver

Sotto is averaging 12.5 points, 12.5 rebounds and 4.0 assists in about 28 minutes a game, and has made 12 of his 16 shots. He has started one of his two games.

Sendai's threat is on the wing. [Jarrett Culver](/player/BLeague/51000531) is scoring 26.5 points a game, fourth in the league. Only Levanga Hokkaido's [Keisei Tominaga](/player/BLeague/19755), at 30.5, the Chiba Jets' [Nate Williams](/player/BLeague/51000641), at 30.0, and Alvark Tokyo's [Keyontae Johnson](/player/BLeague/51000647), at 27.8, are scoring more.

## The other Filipino players

- **[Thirdy Ravena](/player/BLeague/32980) and [Michael Phillips](/player/BLeague/51000639)** are on opposite sides when the Toyama Grouses, 2-2, host Kyoto Hannaryz, 3-1. Ravena is averaging 10.0 points for Toyama and has started two of four games. Phillips has come off the bench in all four of Kyoto's games, averaging 5.0 points and 3.8 rebounds.
- **[Dwight Ramos](/player/BLeague/51000102)** and Levanga Hokkaido, 3-1, visit the Shimane Susanoo Magic, 2-2. Ramos has started all four games and is averaging 9.0 points. Tominaga, the league's leading scorer, is his team-mate.
- **[Bobby Ray Parks Jr.](/player/BLeague/5100000069)** and the Ibaraki Robots host the Hiroshima Dragonflies. Parks is averaging 9.0 points off the bench.

## Unbeaten against winless

That last game is the biggest gap of the night. Hiroshima are 4-0 with a points difference of plus 75, the best in the league. Ibaraki are 0-4. Hiroshima are one of four unbeaten teams, with the Gunma Crane Thunders, the Nagoya Diamond Dolphins and San-en NeoPhoenix. Ibaraki, the Nagasaki Velca and the Saga Ballooners have not won.

## Wednesday's games

All six start at 7:05 p.m. Japan time.

- Sendai 89ers (1-3) v Altiri Chiba (1-3)
- Ibaraki Robots (0-4) v Hiroshima Dragonflies (4-0)

## What is next

After Sendai, Chiba host Osaka on Friday, October 9, and Toyama on Sunday, October 11, which would put Sotto against Thirdy Ravena.
