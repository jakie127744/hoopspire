Milwaukee were down 117-115 with 7.6 seconds left in Indianapolis on Friday and missed three free throws in a row. Then [Kel'el Ware](/player/NBA/5105623) caught a pass from [Kasparas Jakučionis](/player/NBA/5214640) and dunked it with 1.6 seconds left to tie the game. In overtime Ware scored twice more, a dunk for the lead and an alley-oop with 14.1 seconds left, and the Bucks beat the Indiana Pacers 128-127.

It was one of 12 games on the last night of the preseason, and one of two that went to overtime. In Philadelphia, the Boston Celtics beat the 76ers 125-120 in overtime. None of these games count in the standings. The regular season opens on Tuesday, October 20.

## Bucks 128, Pacers 127 (OT)

Indiana led by 19 at 66-47 late in the second quarter and 71-56 at half-time. [Pascal Siakam](/player/NBA/3149673) scored 24 in 21 minutes on 8-of-10 shooting, and [Tyrese Haliburton](/player/NBA/4396993) had 17 points and six assists in 20 minutes.

Milwaukee came back in the third with an 11-0 run that cut an 89-73 deficit to 89-84, and went ahead 110-104 with 3:17 left in the fourth. The Pacers answered with nine straight, the last two on a Keba Keita dunk, for a 113-110 lead. Kam Jones tied it with a three. Kobe Brown made four free throws in the last 30 seconds to put Indiana up 117-114.

Jakučionis made one of two free throws with 7.6 seconds left. Ware grabbed the miss and then missed two free throws himself. Bogoljub Marković's tip-in missed, and Keita blocked his next try. Milwaukee kept the ball, and Ware's dunk sent it to overtime.

Marković opened overtime with a basket and a 27-foot three for a 122-117 lead. Indiana went back in front 123-122 on a Braden Smith floater, and the lead changed three more times. Ware's dunk made it 126-125 with 56.3 seconds left and his alley-oop made it 128-125. Keita scored with 1.2 seconds left, and that was the last shot.

[Tyler Herro](/player/NBA/4395725) led the Bucks with 25 points on 10-of-16 shooting, five of them threes. Ware finished with 13 off the bench, and Jakučionis assisted on all three of his late dunks. The crowd was 10,283.

## Celtics 125, 76ers 120 (OT)

Philadelphia's starters had the better of the first three quarters. [Joel Embiid](/player/NBA/3059318) scored 27 in 29 minutes and made 11 of 13 free throws, [Tyrese Maxey](/player/NBA/4431678) added 22, and [Jaylen Brown](/player/NBA/3917376) had 20 points and eight rebounds against his old team. LeBron James scored eight. The 76ers led by 14 at 86-72 and 94-84 after three.

Boston played without Jayson Tatum, Derrick White and Paul George, and came back anyway. The Celtics scored the first eight points of the fourth, and the game was tied three times in the last six minutes. Ron Harper Jr. hit a 14-footer to make it 115-115 with 51.5 seconds left.

Philadelphia led 117-115 early in overtime, but Boston scored nine in a row. Dillon Mitchell dunked off a Gabe McGlothan steal for the lead with 3:02 left and hit a 26-foot three to make it 122-117. Milos Uzan's pull-up made it 124-117 before Jameer Nelson Jr. hit a late three.

Hugo González led Boston with 21 off the bench. The crowd was 19,746.
