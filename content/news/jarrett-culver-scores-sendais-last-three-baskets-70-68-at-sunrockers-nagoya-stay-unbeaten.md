The SunRockers led 68-66 with 3:41 left, in front of 7,723 at Toyota Arena Tokyo. They did not score again. [Jarrett Culver](/player/BLeague/51000531) tied the game with a jumper in the lane with 2:45 to play, and after Nathan Boothe stole the ball from Josh Hawkinson, Culver hit a jumper with 41 seconds left. The Sendai 89ers won 70-68 on Sunday.

Culver scored 32 points with 10 rebounds and made four of his 10 threes. Sendai have now won three in a row and are 4-3. The SunRockers fell to 4-3 as well, after two straight losses.

Around the B.League Premier on Sunday, the Toyama Grouses won 94-74 at Altiri Chiba, the Nagoya Diamond Dolphins beat the Ibaraki Robots 90-78 to stay perfect at 5-0, the Akita Northern Happinets won 82-71 at Utsunomiya, and the SeaHorses Mikawa won 89-81 at Kyoto.

## SunRockers 68, Sendai 70

Tokyo won the second quarter 18-6 and led 36-24 with 5:19 left in the half, their biggest lead. It was 42-30 at half-time. Sendai came back in the third quarter. Down 50-40, they scored 10 straight points: a Culver three, a Jake Layman basket, a Culver basket and a Layman three tied it 50-50 with 2:35 left in the quarter. Culver's three with 31 seconds left tied it again at 55, and Donte Grantham's basket with three seconds left gave Tokyo a 57-55 lead after three.

The fourth quarter went back and forth. Boothe's three put Sendai up 60-57, Rawle Alkins put Tokyo back in front 61-60, and Hawkinson's three made it 68-66. Then Tokyo missed its last six shots. Grantham's three with four seconds left was the last of them, and Culver took the rebound.

Sergio El Darwich had 11 points, 10 rebounds and 11 assists for Sendai. Alkins and Daiki Tanaka scored 12 each for Tokyo.

## Altiri Chiba 74, Toyama 94

Chiba led 36-29 after two Dererk Pardon free throws with 7:05 left in the second quarter. Toyama answered with 11 straight points. Braian Angola scored, Thirdy Ravena hit a three, Haruaki Tanaka's three gave Toyama the lead at 37-36, and Ravena hit another to make it 40-36. Toyama led for the rest of the game, 49-42 at half-time and 74-60 after three, and the margin reached 20 in the last minute.

[Darral Willis Jr.](/player/BLeague/51000318) led Toyama with 27 points and 10 rebounds. Aamir Simms scored 17 and Angola had 15 with eight assists. Seiya Ando scored 16 for Chiba, who are 2-5. Kai Sotto did not play for Chiba. The crowd at Chibagin Arena was 4,752.

## Ibaraki 78, Nagoya 90

Nagoya scored 21 of the first 24 points and led 21-3 with 4:53 left in the first quarter. Ibaraki came all the way back. The Robots won the second quarter 21-11 and led 46-36 late in the half. The lead changed hands five times in the third quarter, and Nagoya led 63-60 going into the fourth.

Nagoya then scored the first 10 points of the quarter to lead 73-60. Aaron Henry scored 12 of his 19 points in the last 4:04. [Takumi Saito](/player/BLeague/14301) finished with 21 points and 10 assists, and Scott Eatherton scored 20. Noboru Hasegawa scored 17 for Ibaraki, who are 0-7.
