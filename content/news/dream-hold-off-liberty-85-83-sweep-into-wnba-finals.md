[Breanna Stewart](/player/WNBA/2998928) put back a missed New York three with 0.2 seconds left, and it was not enough. The Atlanta Dream beat the New York Liberty 85-83 on Friday night at Barclays Center to win their semifinal 3-0 and reach the WNBA Finals, where they meet the Golden State Valkyries.

[Jordin Canada](/player/WNBA/3142250) scored 19 points with 11 assists and made all eight free throws. [Angel Reese](/player/WNBA/4433402) led Atlanta with 22 points and nine rebounds, and [Rhyne Howard](/player/WNBA/4398674) scored 21. Stewart and [Jonquel Jones](/player/WNBA/2999101) scored 26 each for New York, and Jones added 14 rebounds.

Atlanta played a second straight game without [Allisha Gray](/player/WNBA/3058901), out with a left hand injury from Game 1.

## A 12-point lead, gone in five minutes

The Dream led 25-18 after one quarter and 44-39 at half-time. Howard's three and a DeWanna Bonner jumper opened the third quarter, and two Reese free throws made it 51-39 with 8:21 left in the quarter.

New York answered with 10 straight points. Sabrina Ionescu hit a three, and Jones scored the next seven, a three, a jumper and a driving layup, to cut it to 51-49. Jones tipped in a miss to give the Liberty a 56-55 lead with 4:39 left, and the teams traded the lead from there. New York won the third quarter 29-22 and led 68-66 going into the fourth.

## The 9-0 run

Ionescu's three from 25 feet made it 71-66 for New York with 9:26 to play, the Liberty's biggest lead of the night.

Atlanta scored the next nine. Howard made two free throws and a driving layup. Canada hit a three from 24 feet with 5:23 left to put the Dream back in front, 73-71, then made two more free throws for a 75-71 lead.

Howard's three-point play made it 78-73, and Bonner made all three free throws on a foul behind the arc for 81-75 with 3:15 left.

## The last minute

Canada scored inside to make it 83-79 with 1:15 to go. Jones answered with a layup off a Stewart pass, 83-81, with 59 seconds left.

Naz Hillmon missed a layup for Atlanta with 39.2 seconds left. Hillmon then stole an Ionescu pass, and Leonie Fiebich stole a Howard pass right back with 20.9 seconds left. Stewart missed from five feet with 9.9 seconds left, Bonner took the rebound, and Bonner made two free throws for 85-81 with 6.2 seconds left.

Ionescu missed a three from 30 feet. Stewart grabbed the rebound and scored as time ran out, and the basket stood after a replay review. The final margin was two.

## How Atlanta won it

The Dream made 22 of 24 free throws to New York's 20 of 21, and turned the ball over seven times to the Liberty's 10. Atlanta also took 69 shots to New York's 62.

Atlanta won Game 1 92-82 and Game 2 101-98 in overtime.
