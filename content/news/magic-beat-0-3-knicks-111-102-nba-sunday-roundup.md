[Franz Wagner](/player/NBA/4566434) scored nine of Orlando's next 11 points in two minutes, and a Knicks lead of 86-76 was gone. The Magic won the fourth quarter 40-27 and beat New York 111-102 on Sunday at Madison Square Garden.

Sunday had seven games. The defending champions are 0-3. Detroit, Minnesota, Memphis and Oklahoma City are all 3-0, and so are the Lakers, whose 132-123 win at Utah has [its own story](/story/doncic-52-lakers-beat-jazz-132-123). Here are the other six.

## Magic 111, Knicks 102

New York led 86-76 with 9:20 left after [OG Anunoby](/player/NBA/3934719) scored eight points in a minute, two of the baskets set up by [Tyler Kolek](/player/NBA/4433225).

Orlando scored the next 11. Wagner made four free throws, [Jalen Suggs](/player/NBA/4432165) a driving floater, and Wagner a jumper and a layup off a Suggs pass, plus the free throw: 87-86 Magic with 7:25 to go.

[Josh Hart](/player/NBA/3062679) hit a 24-foot three to put New York back ahead at 89-87. It was the Knicks' last lead. Wagner tied it, then put Orlando in front with a driving layup. [Desmond Bane](/player/NBA/4066320) answered a [Karl-Anthony Towns](/player/NBA/3136195) dunk with a 26-foot three, [Anthony Black](/player/NBA/4712849) made another from 25 feet, and the margin reached 11.

Wagner finished with 23 points, 13 rebounds and nine assists. Bane scored 22, 11 of them in the fourth quarter, and [Tristan da Silva](/player/NBA/4702382) 19. [Jalen Brunson](/player/NBA/3934672) led New York with 28 on 12 of 28 shooting. Towns had 22 points and 14 rebounds, Anunoby 22.

## Pistons 106, 76ers 93

Detroit led from the first minute to the last and never trailed. [John Collins](/player/NBA/3908845) tipped in the opening basket, [Cade Cunningham](/player/NBA/4432166) hit a 27-foot step-back, and the Pistons were up 20-4 inside five minutes. The lead was 21 at 56-35 late in the second quarter.

Philadelphia cut it to 79-75 with 10:18 to play, but got no closer. Cunningham had 26 points, eight rebounds and eight assists, [Duncan Robinson](/player/NBA/3157465) 21 with five threes, and [Daniss Jenkins](/player/NBA/5107199) 18. [Tyrese Maxey](/player/NBA/4431678) scored 29 for the 76ers and [LeBron James](/player/NBA/1966) 18. Philadelphia is 3-1.

## Thunder 108, Clippers 101

There were nine lead changes, and the game was tied at 67 with 3:59 left in the third quarter. [Rui Hachimura](/player/NBA/4066648) cut it to 95-93 with a three at 4:15, and [Derrick Jones Jr.](/player/NBA/3936099) made it 97-96 at 3:00.

Oklahoma City then scored six in a row: two free throws by [Alex Caruso](/player/NBA/2991350), a running dunk by [Jalen Williams](/player/NBA/4593803) from a Caruso pass, and a short jumper by [Ajay Mitchell](/player/NBA/4900671). [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](/player/NBA/4278073) scored 34, including two free throws after a flagrant foul at 1:14 and another at 1:12 for 106-99. [Darius Garland](/player/NBA/4396907) had 16 points and 11 assists for the Clippers, who are 1-2.

## Pacers 136, Nets 109

Indiana scored 41 points in the second quarter and 37 in the third. [Jay Huff](/player/NBA/4065731) scored on a three, a running dunk and a reverse dunk in the first three and a half minutes of the third quarter, and [Aaron Nesmith](/player/NBA/4396909) ran the lead from seven to 14 with a 26-footer at 8:14. A 12-0 run later in the quarter made it 100-75.

[Tyrese Haliburton](/player/NBA/4396993) had 24 points and 10 assists, [Pascal Siakam](/player/NBA/3149673) 21 points and six assists. Five Pacers scored between 13 and 16. Brooklyn is 0-3, with [Nolan Traoré](/player/NBA/5279130) its top scorer on 17.

## Timberwolves 117, Raptors 94

Toronto led 3-2 early and never led again. Minnesota won the third quarter 35-15 and led by 32 at 109-77 in the fourth.

[Anthony Edwards](/player/NBA/4594268) scored 26 in 28 minutes. [LaMelo Ball](/player/NBA/4432816) had 15 points and eight assists in 17 minutes, four of the assists in that third quarter. [Ja'Kobe Walter](/player/NBA/4684272) led Toronto with 22 and [RJ Barrett](/player/NBA/4395625) scored 20. [Scottie Barnes](/player/NBA/4433134) missed all 12 of his shots.

## Grizzlies 123, Kings 112

[Domantas Sabonis](/player/NBA/3155942) tied the game at 106 with a reverse layup and a free throw with 5:41 left. Memphis scored the next 11 points in 1:44: a floater and a free throw from [Isaiah Stewart](/player/NBA/4432810), a tip-in by [Cedric Coward](/player/NBA/4903027), and a 27-foot step-back three and a driving floater from [Ty Jerome](/player/NBA/4065733), who was fouled and made the free throw.

Jerome finished with 31 points and 14 free throws from 14 attempts. [Zach LaVine](/player/NBA/3064440) scored 37 for Sacramento, which is 0-3. Sabonis had 15 points and 15 rebounds.
