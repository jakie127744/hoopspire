The Atlanta Dream and the Golden State Valkyries both lead their best-of-five WNBA semifinals 2-0, and both can close them out on Friday night on the road. A win sends either team to the WNBA Finals. A loss sends the series to Game 4 on Sunday.

[Breanna Stewart](/player/WNBA/2998928) and the New York Liberty host Atlanta at Barclays Center in Brooklyn at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2. [A'ja Wilson](/player/WNBA/3149391) and the Las Vegas Aces host Golden State at Michelob Ultra Arena at 9:30 p.m. ET on Peacock and NBCSN.

## Dream v Liberty: two very different wins

Atlanta won Game 1 92-82 in the third quarter. They trailed 43-39 early in the second half, scored the next 13 points and won the quarter 32-15. [Jordin Canada](/player/WNBA/3142250) scored 25 that night.

Game 2 went to overtime. [Marine Fauthoux](/player/WNBA/4790260) tied it 88-88 with a three for New York with 10.3 seconds left in regulation, and the Liberty scored eight straight in overtime to lead 96-93. Then [Naz Hillmon](/player/WNBA/4398915)'s layup with 20.8 seconds left put Atlanta ahead for good, and the Dream won 101-98. Stewart scored 35 in the loss.

Atlanta won Game 2 without [Allisha Gray](/player/WNBA/3058901), their leading scorer this season, who hurt her left hand in Game 1. [Satou Sabally](/player/WNBA/4281929) has not played for New York in the series.

In Game 2 the Liberty shot 55% to Atlanta's 40%, but Atlanta took 84 shots to New York's 64. The Dream grabbed 11 offensive rebounds to five, and New York turned the ball over 17 times to Atlanta's nine.

## Valkyries v Aces: rebounds, then a block

Golden State won Game 1 71-60 on the glass, with 52 rebounds to 25 and 17 offensive rebounds to two. [Gabby Williams](/player/WNBA/3142328) had 25 points and 11 rebounds.

Game 2 was the opposite kind of game. The Valkyries led 64-47 late in the third quarter, and the Aces came back to lead 76-74 with 1:36 left, their only lead of the night. [Veronica Burton](/player/WNBA/4398935)'s 28-foot three put Golden State back in front, and [Kiah Stokes](/player/WNBA/2590093) blocked Chelsea Gray's layup with 3.0 seconds left, and Golden State won 83-81.

[Tiffany Hayes](/player/WNBA/1054) scored 21 off the bench in Game 2, and the Valkyries made 25 of 27 free throws and took 10 offensive rebounds to the Aces' six. Las Vegas won the fourth quarter 26-17.

Las Vegas won all three regular-season meetings. Wilson has scored 24 in both games of the series. [Jewell Loyd](/player/WNBA/2987869) left Game 1 with a leg injury and did not play in Game 2.

## What each Game 3 needs

For Atlanta, a third win in three tries against a Liberty team that outshot them in Game 2. For New York, a first win, on their own floor.

For Golden State, a third straight win over a Las Vegas team they had not beaten in the regular season. For the Aces, a home win to force Game 4.

## What is next

If New York win, Game 4 is at Barclays Center on Sunday, October 11, at 2:00 p.m. ET on ABC. If Las Vegas win, Game 4 is at Michelob Ultra Arena on Sunday, October 11, at a time not yet set. Any Game 5 would be on Wednesday, October 14, back in Atlanta or San Francisco.
