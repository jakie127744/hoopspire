The Atlanta Dream closed the 2026 regular season at 30-14, beating the New York Liberty 83-65 on the road on Wednesday night at Barclays Center.

Allisha Gray led Atlanta with 24 points on 8-of-17 shooting, adding five assists and three steals. Jordin Canada and Rhyne Howard each added six assists as the Dream shared the ball, and DeWanna Bonner pulled down eight rebounds off the bench.

Marine Fauthoux led the Liberty with 14 points, and Jonquel Jones added 10 points and 14 rebounds in 20 minutes. Sabrina Ionescu played 23 minutes and scored 7.

Atlanta finishes the season 30-14. New York finishes 26-18.
