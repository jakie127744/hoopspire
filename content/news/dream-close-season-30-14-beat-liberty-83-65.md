The Atlanta Dream closed the 2026 regular season at 30-14, beating the New York Liberty 83-65 on the road on Wednesday night at Barclays Center, and they did it in one quarter. New York led by a point after the first, 19-18. Atlanta outscored the Liberty 23-7 in the second, took the lead for good at 21-19 with 9:28 left in it, and led 41-26 at halftime. The crowd was 17,581.

[Allisha Gray](/player/WNBA/3058901) led Atlanta with 24 points on 8-of-17 shooting, with five assists and three steals. [Naz Hillmon](/player/WNBA/4398915) scored 16 and [Jordin Canada](/player/WNBA/3142250) 12. [Rhyne Howard](/player/WNBA/4398674) had six assists and Canada five, and [DeWanna Bonner](/player/WNBA/869) came off the bench for nine points and eight rebounds. [Angel Reese](/player/WNBA/4433402) played 17 minutes and had six points and six rebounds, four of them offensive.

## How the game turned

The first quarter was level, with three ties, and New York's biggest lead of the night was three, 17-14 with 2:29 to go. Then the Dream took over. They scored eight in a row in the second quarter, from 28-24 to 36-24 with 1:26 left before the break, and then nine more straight across the halftime break, from 36-26 to 45-26 early in the third. Atlanta's lead reached 20, at 50-30, with 6:39 left in the third quarter.

New York got no closer than seven in the fourth quarter, at 63-56 with 5:03 left. Atlanta closed the game with a 9-0 run, from 72-62 to 81-62 in under two minutes, and it was over. Gray hit a 25-foot three with 1:24 left, and Bonner hit another with 58.5 seconds to play.

## The numbers

Atlanta won most of the small fights. The Dream turned the ball over six times, and New York turned it over 18. Atlanta had 12 steals to the Liberty's three, and 12 fast-break points to New York's five. The Liberty shot 36.4% (20 of 55) and made 5 of 29 three-pointers. Atlanta shot 39.0% (30 of 77) and made 10 of 35 from three, and scored 36 points in the paint to 28. New York made 20 of 22 free throws to Atlanta's 13 of 15, and it out-rebounded the Dream 40-35.

[Marine Fauthoux](/player/WNBA/4790260) led the Liberty with 14 points in 17 minutes, making eight of nine free throws. [Han Xu](/player/WNBA/4337216) scored 11 in 20 minutes. [Jonquel Jones](/player/WNBA/2999101) added 10 points and 14 rebounds, 13 of them defensive, in 20 minutes. [Sabrina Ionescu](/player/WNBA/4066533) played 23 minutes, scored 7 on 2-of-7 shooting and had four turnovers. [Raquel Carrera](/player/WNBA/4790258) scored nine on 3-of-3 shooting.
