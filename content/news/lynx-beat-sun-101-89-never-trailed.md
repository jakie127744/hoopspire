The Minnesota Lynx beat the Connecticut Sun 101-89 at Mohegan Sun Arena on Sunday, and led from the opening basket to the last. Minnesota never trailed at any point in the game — not for one possession — despite Connecticut winning two of the four quarters.

That is not a contradiction so much as a description of how the game went. Minnesota opened on a 10-0 run inside the first five minutes, built a 20-point lead early in the second quarter (37-17, with 7:55 left), and had banked enough of a cushion by halftime that it could afford to lose the next two quarters and still win comfortably. Connecticut took the second quarter 31-28 and the third 24-21, cutting Minnesota's lead as low as seven — 71-64, with 2:28 left in the third — before the Lynx answered with an 8-0 run early in the fourth that put the game out of reach again, 86-74.

The result keeps Minnesota at 32-10, the WNBA's best record and its No. 1 playoff seed, one game ahead of the Golden State Valkyries (31-11) with the regular season nearly finished.

Minnesota's lead was down to 12 with two minutes left, 94-82, before the Lynx closed it out: a Kayla McBride three made it 97-82, and after Connecticut answered to within 12, two Gianna Kneepkens free throws and a Liatu King jumper pushed the margin back to 99-87 inside the final minute. The last exchange was the game's sharpest: Nia Brodie stole a Connecticut possession at 41 seconds, Connecticut's Charlisse Leger-Walker blocked a Minnesota drive on the possession that followed, and Minnesota scored anyway on the next trip to make it 101-87. Hailey Van Lith's jumper with 17 seconds left set the final margin.

Napheesa Collier led Minnesota with 23 points, and Olivia Miles added 21 with six assists. Natasha Howard started and had a double-double, 18 points and 11 rebounds.

**What is next.** Minnesota plays at the Indiana Fever on Tuesday, September 22, and hosts Indiana again on Thursday, September 24, to close the regular season. Connecticut hosts the Washington Mystics on Tuesday and the Toronto Tempo on Thursday.
