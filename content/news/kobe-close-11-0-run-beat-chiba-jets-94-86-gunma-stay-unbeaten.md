[Yuki Togashi](/player/BLeague/9055)'s three with 2:18 left put the Chiba Jets ahead 86-83 at Glion Arena on Thursday night. They did not score again. The Kobe Storks scored the last 11 points of the game and won 94-86 in front of 8,368 fans.

[Fardaws Aimaq](/player/BLeague/51000635) scored on two layups to put Kobe back in front at 87-86 with 1:25 left. [Yoeli Childs](/player/BLeague/51000317) hit a three with 40 seconds left, and [Jalen McDaniels](/player/BLeague/51000636) made four free throws in the last 25 seconds. Aimaq finished with 29 points and 16 rebounds.

It was one of five B.League Premier games on Thursday. Gunma Crane Thunders won 76-65 at the Ryukyu Golden Kings to stay unbeaten at 5-0.

## Kobe 94, Chiba Jets 86

Chiba led 51-43 at half-time and 69-61 after three quarters, and their biggest lead was 10, at 69-59 late in the third. Kobe opened the fourth with eight straight points: an Aimaq layup, a McDaniels three and an Aimaq three tied it 69-69 with 8:30 left. Chiba went back in front at 74-73 with 6:29 left and stayed there until Childs tied it 83-83 with 2:26 left.

Kobe won the fourth quarter 33-17 and took 17 offensive rebounds to Chiba's eight. [Shuto Terazono](/player/BLeague/18148) added 17 points, McDaniels 15 off the bench and Childs 14 with seven assists. [Nate Williams](/player/BLeague/51000641) led Chiba with 22, and Togashi scored 18 off the bench with five threes. Chiba have lost four in a row and are 1-4. Kobe are 2-3.

## Gunma 76, Ryukyu 65

The game was tied 31-31 at half-time at Okinawa Suntory Arena, in front of 8,601. Gunma went ahead 36-33 early in the third quarter and never trailed again. They won the quarter 22-12 and led by 16, at 49-33, with 4:32 left in it.

[Kerry Blackshear Jr.](/player/BLeague/51000145) scored 19 with nine rebounds, and [Yuma Fujii](/player/BLeague/8482) added 17 off the bench with four threes. Gunma made 20 of 26 free throws. [Jack Cooley](/player/BLeague/26914) led Ryukyu with 15, David Nwaba had 14, and [Kiefer Ravena](/player/BLeague/5100000062) scored 12 off the bench with four threes. Ryukyu are 2-3.

## Nagasaki 74, Shinshu 71

Shinshu led 46-32 with 33 seconds left in the first half and 56-50 after three quarters at Happiness Arena. Nagasaki won the fourth 24-15. [Marcus Lee](/player/BLeague/51000659)'s layup gave Shinshu a 71-70 lead with 1:24 left. [Yudai Baba](/player/BLeague/12577) answered with a layup with 25 seconds left and two free throws with 12 seconds left.

Baba scored 27 with five threes, and [Jarrell Brantley](/player/BLeague/51000304) added 19, also with five threes. [Kevon Harris](/player/BLeague/51000658) scored 28 for Shinshu. It was Nagasaki's first win of the season, and they are 1-4. Shinshu are 2-3. The crowd was 5,410.

## Kawasaki 87, Akita 81

Kawasaki scored the last seven points: a [Muhammad-Ali Abdur-Rahkman](/player/BLeague/51000710) free throw, two from [Alex Kirk](/player/BLeague/12582), an Abdur-Rahkman jumper and a [Jaedon LeDee](/player/BLeague/51000627) dunk with 20 seconds left.

Abdur-Rahkman scored 21, Matt Bonds 19 and LeDee 18 with nine rebounds. Smith had 26 points and eight assists for Akita, and [Yanni Wetzell](/player/BLeague/51000521) 23. Kawasaki are 2-3, Akita 1-4. The crowd was 4,492.
