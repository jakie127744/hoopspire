Real Madrid won the EuroLeague SuperCup in September. Three rounds into the regular season, they are one of two teams without a win. Madrid lost 78-77 at Dubai, 94-84 at Anadolu Efes and 102-98 to Hapoel Tel Aviv, in Sofia. All three games were on the road, and the three defeats add up to 15 points.

Théo Maledon has been Madrid's top scorer twice. He shared the team lead with Timothé Luwawu-Cabarrot, 11 points each, in the one-point loss in Dubai, and scored 26 in Sofia, where Luwawu-Cabarrot added 21. Hapoel answered with 22 each from Elijah Bryant and Vasilije Micić. In Istanbul, Facundo Campazzo led Madrid with 16.

On Thursday, October 8, Madrid play their first home game of the season, at the Movistar Arena. The visitors are Partizan Belgrade, one of four teams still unbeaten.

## The four at 3-0

Panathinaikos have the biggest margin of the four. They beat Paris 91-72, ASVEL 102-79 and Maccabi Tel Aviv 90-67, all at home in Athens, for a margin of 65 points over three games.

Fenerbahce also played all three games at home in Istanbul and beat Virtus Bologna 78-68, Bayern Munich 100-76 and Dubai 76-66.

Valencia won 96-94 at Besiktas, beat Baskonia 111-88 at the Roig Arena and won 105-92 at ASVEL.

Partizan beat Milan 92-76 in Belgrade, then won twice on the road: 92-79 at Paris and 86-84 at Bayern. In Munich, Tonye Jekiri scored 17 and Carlik Jones 16, and Partizan held on against 20 points from Duane Washington.

Round 4 pairs two of the four. Panathinaikos host Fenerbahce in Athens on Thursday, so at least one of them will lose for the first time this season. Valencia, meanwhile, host Hapoel Tel Aviv, who are 2-1.

## The other winless team

Maccabi Tel Aviv are also 0-3. They lost 84-74 at ASVEL, 109-93 to Besiktas and 90-67 at Panathinaikos, a point difference of minus 49, the worst in the competition. Maccabi's home game so far was played at the Aleksandar Nikolic Hall in Belgrade, and they host Milan there on Thursday. Milan are 1-2.

## The table after three rounds

Four teams are 3-0: Panathinaikos, Fenerbahce, Valencia and Partizan. Four are 2-1: Olympiacos, Barcelona, Hapoel Tel Aviv and Dubai. Dubai, who beat Madrid by a point in Round 1, host Crvena Zvezda on Thursday. Ten are 1-2, and two, Real Madrid and Maccabi, are 0-3.

## Round 4 fixtures

All times are Central European Summer Time.

- **Wednesday, October 7:** Paris v ASVEL, 20:45, Adidas Arena. Both are 1-2, and it is the first meeting of the season between the two French clubs.
- **Thursday, October 8:** Dubai v Crvena Zvezda, 18:00. Maccabi Tel Aviv v Milan, 18:00, in Belgrade. Bayern Munich v Virtus Bologna, 20:00. Panathinaikos v Fenerbahce, 20:15. Valencia v Hapoel Tel Aviv, 20:30. Real Madrid v Partizan, 20:45.
- **Friday, October 9:** Olympiacos v Anadolu Efes, 20:15. Barcelona v Zalgiris, 20:30. Baskonia v Besiktas, 20:30.

## What is next

Paris and ASVEL open Round 4 on Wednesday evening. Thursday decides how many unbeaten teams are left: Panathinaikos and Fenerbahce meet in Athens, Valencia host Hapoel, and Partizan visit Madrid, where the home side are still looking for a first win.
