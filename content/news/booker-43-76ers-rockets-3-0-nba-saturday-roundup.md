[Devin Booker](/player/NBA/3136193) scored 12 points in the last 2:55 of the third quarter, as the Phoenix Suns turned an 85-83 game into 102-92. Booker finished with 43 on 17 of 25 shooting, with seven rebounds and seven assists, and Phoenix beat the Golden State Warriors 131-122 on Saturday in its home opener.

Saturday had eight games. Philadelphia and Houston won to go to 3-0. Milwaukee, Miami and Golden State lost to fall to 0-3. Denver's 141-135 overtime win over New Orleans has [its own story](/story/nuggets-come-back-from-23-down-beat-pelicans-141-135-overtime). Here are the other seven.

## Suns 131, Warriors 122

The lead changed hands 14 times, the last with 5:32 left in the third quarter, when Phoenix went ahead 76-75. Golden State had scored 12 straight minutes earlier to turn 71-61 into 73-71.

Booker then took the game. He hit an 11-foot pull-up, scored on a drive and made the free throw, hit a 26-foot three and closed the quarter with a 27-foot pull-up three and another free throw. Phoenix scored 45 points in the third and led 102-92. The lead reached 16, at 118-102, with 6:07 left.

[Yaxel Lendeborg](/player/NBA/5175737) led the Warriors with 26 points and six threes, and [De'Anthony Melton](/player/NBA/4066436) scored 25. Phoenix is 1-1.

## 76ers 127, Bucks 116

[Joel Embiid](/player/NBA/3059318) had 28 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists, and made 12 of 14 free throws. [Jaylen Brown](/player/NBA/3917376) scored 27 and [Tyrese Maxey](/player/NBA/4431678) 26, 12 of them in the third quarter.

Milwaukee led 48-41 in the second quarter and was within 117-114 with 2:02 left. Then the 76ers scored eight straight. Brown hit a 27-foot three from Embiid. [Kentavious Caldwell-Pope](/player/NBA/2581018) hit a 25-footer from Brown. Embiid dunked at 47.4: 125-114.

[Jaime Jaquez Jr.](/player/NBA/4432848) led Milwaukee with 21 points, and [Kel'el Ware](/player/NBA/5105623) scored 19.

## Cavaliers 125, Nets 121

Cleveland led 104-80 with 9:29 left, on a 26-foot three by [Jaylon Tyson](/player/NBA/4683747). Brooklyn then scored 41 points in the fourth quarter.

[Egor Dëmin](/player/NBA/5175643) hit three threes in 47 seconds and scored 15 in the quarter. [Michael Porter Jr.](/player/NBA/4278104) hit a three to make it 116-106. Down 120-107 with 2:04 left, the Nets scored nine straight on free throws by Dëmin, a basket and two free throws by [Julius Randle](/player/NBA/3064514) and a three by Chaney Johnson. Porter's 23-foot three with 8.1 seconds left made it 123-121. [Sam Merrill](/player/NBA/4066757) made two free throws at 5.2 to end it.

[Donovan Mitchell](/player/NBA/3908809) scored 22, [Jarrett Allen](/player/NBA/4066328) 21 and [Evan Mobley](/player/NBA/4432158) 20 in Cleveland's home opener. Porter had 28 points for Brooklyn, Randle 23 and Dëmin 22, on 5 of 10 threes. The Cavaliers are 1-1, the Nets 0-2.

## Hornets 115, Heat 110

Charlotte led 96-73 late in the third quarter. Miami was still down 100-85 with 9:00 left, then scored 15 straight. [Davion Mitchell](/player/NBA/4278053) assisted on four of the baskets in the run and scored the last five points himself, the last two at 5:42 to tie it at 100.

It was tied twice more, the last at 104 on a dunk by [Bam Adebayo](/player/NBA/4066261) at 3:45. [Grayson Allen](/player/NBA/3135045) answered with a 26-foot three, was fouled and made the free throw: 108-104. [Dennis Schröder](/player/NBA/3032979) made two free throws for 110-104, and Miami got no closer than three after that.

[Brandon Miller](/player/NBA/4433287) scored 34 for Charlotte, with 11 of 12 free throws. Schröder scored 25 and Allen 20, nine of them in the fourth quarter. Mitchell finished with 22 points and 10 assists, 15 of his points in the fourth. Charlotte is 2-1.

## Wizards 124, Bulls 120

Washington won the third quarter 40-20 to turn a 62-57 halftime deficit into a 97-82 lead, and led 103-82 early in the fourth.

Chicago came back to within three, 118-115, on a running jumper by [Matas Buzelis](/player/NBA/4711294) with 23.3 seconds left. [Anthony Davis](/player/NBA/6583) and [Trae Young](/player/NBA/4277905) made six free throws in a row after that. [Norman Powell](/player/NBA/2595516) hit a three with 3.4 seconds left to make it 122-118, and Washington closed it out at the line.

Young scored 25, 10 in the fourth quarter, with nine assists and 9 of 9 free throws. [Kyshawn George](/player/NBA/5174563) scored 22 and Davis 19. [Caleb Wilson](/player/NBA/5095151) had 30 points and 14 rebounds for Chicago in its home opener, and Buzelis scored 21. Washington is 2-1.

## Spurs 122, Mavericks 106

There were 14 lead changes, and Dallas was within 92-91 early in the fourth quarter. San Antonio then scored 11 straight in 2:10: threes by [Devin Vassell](/player/NBA/4395630), [Julian Champagnie](/player/NBA/4592479) and [Dylan Harper](/player/NBA/5037871), and a lob to Tarris Reed Jr. Vassell set up three of the four baskets. A later 10-0 run made it 119-96.

[Victor Wembanyama](/player/NBA/5104157) had 23 points, seven rebounds and four blocks, and the Spurs outscored Dallas by 32 in his 29 minutes. Harper scored 22. It was San Antonio's first win. [Sergio de Larrea](/player/NBA/5279129) scored 21 for Dallas, which lost its home opener and is 1-2.

## Rockets 121, Hawks 114

Houston led by 20, at 85-65, in the third quarter. Atlanta cut it to 117-112 with 36.5 seconds left, but [Kevin Durant](/player/NBA/3202) made two free throws and [Tari Eason](/player/NBA/4433192) finished a running dunk at 8.7.

Durant scored 22, and [Reed Sheppard](/player/NBA/4711272) and Eason 19 each. [Jalen Johnson](/player/NBA/4701230) had 26 points, nine rebounds and 16 assists for Atlanta in its home opener. The Hawks are 2-1.
