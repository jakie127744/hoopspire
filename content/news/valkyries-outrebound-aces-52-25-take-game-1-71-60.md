The Golden State Valkyries took 17 offensive rebounds on Sunday. The Las Vegas Aces took two. Golden State beat Las Vegas 71-60 at Chase Center to open the WNBA semifinals, and leads the best-of-five series 1-0.

[Gabby Williams](/player/WNBA/3142328) led the Valkyries with 25 points, 11 rebounds and five assists. [A'ja Wilson](/player/WNBA/3149391) scored 24 for the Aces on 8-of-21 shooting. Las Vegas made 21 of 62 shots, 34%, and scored its fewest points of the playoffs.

The Aces won all three regular-season meetings, 91-81, 84-79 and 92-73.

## The glass

Golden State out-rebounded Las Vegas 52-25. [Kiah Stokes](/player/WNBA/2590093) did not score and took 14 rebounds, seven of them on the offensive end, with four blocks. [Kayla Thornton](/player/WNBA/2529622) had 11 rebounds, five offensive. The Valkyries outscored the Aces 34-18 in the paint.

The Aces turned the ball over six times to Golden State's 13 and had nine steals. Golden State still took 69 shots to Las Vegas' 62.

## How it went

[Chelsea Gray](/player/WNBA/2529122) opened the scoring with a three, and Las Vegas led by three, its biggest lead of the day. The game was tied three times in the first quarter. Golden State took the lead for good at 19-18 with 1:08 left in the quarter and scored 10 straight, from 17-18 down to 27-18 up early in the second.

The Valkyries led 34-23 after a [Veronica Burton](/player/WNBA/4398935) jumper at 4:22 of the second quarter. They did not score again in the half. Las Vegas scored the last eight points of the half, five of them from Wilson, and trailed 34-31 at halftime.

[Cecilia Zandalasini](/player/WNBA/4257500) opened the third with a jumper and a three, [Kaitlyn Chen](/player/WNBA/4899328) scored four, and Thornton's layup at 4:30 made it 45-37. [Aminata Gueye](/player/WNBA/5447776) scored seven points in the last four minutes of the quarter off the bench, two of her baskets set up by Williams, and Golden State led 56-48 after three.

[Jackie Young](/player/WNBA/4065870) hit a three at 8:25 of the fourth to cut it to 58-53. Williams answered with a step-back three at 7:21. Thornton, Williams and Chen scored on layups in the last four minutes, and Chen's at 2:00 made it 71-58, the biggest lead of the game. Neither team scored again until Kalani Brown's basket with 53 seconds left.

## The numbers

Williams made 9 of 18 shots and four of seven threes, and made all three free throws. Chen scored 11 off the bench and Gueye nine on 4-of-5 shooting. Zandalasini added seven.

Wilson made 8 of 11 free throws but missed both of her threes and did not have an assist. Gray scored 16 with three threes. Young scored 11 on 4-of-15 shooting and had three steals. Jewell Loyd started and did not score in 17 minutes.

Both teams made seven threes. Golden State made 12 of 16 free throws, Las Vegas 11 of 15. The crowd was 18,064.
