There were four-tenths of a second left in regulation at the Ynares Center in Antipolo on Sunday when Alec Stockton's basket tied Converge and TNT at 106-106. In overtime, with the game tied again at 112-112, Stockton scored with 11.3 seconds left. Converge defended Rondae Hollis-Jefferson on TNT's last possession, and the FiberXers won 114-112.

Converge had trailed by 15 in the second half. They finish the elimination round 8-4, third in Group A, and TNT finish fourth at 7-5.

"We just wanted to have the mentality of not giving up whether we're down or we're up," Stockton said.

## Converge 114, TNT 112 (OT)

TNT led after each of the first three quarters: 35-27, 56-55 at half-time and 85-74 going into the fourth. The lead reached 15 at 86-71.

Then Converge came back. Cameron Clark finished with 27 points, seven rebounds and six assists, and Juan Gomez de Liano had 22 points and eight assists. Stockton scored 19, and Justine Baltazar added 19 more.

Hollis-Jefferson scored 33 with 11 rebounds for TNT. Jordan Heading had 21, Calvin Oftana 14 and Roger Pogoy 12.

Both teams go to the best-of-five quarterfinals against Group B teams. Converge face the second-placed team from Group B and TNT the group winner.

## Ginebra 106, Phoenix 89

Barangay Ginebra scored the first 10 points on Sunday night and never trailed. They led 36-28 after one quarter, won the second 29-17 and were up 65-45 at half-time.

Scottie Thompson scored 29 and Jordan Murphy had 28 points and 13 rebounds. Japeth Aguilar scored 12, and Jeremiah Gray had 12 off the bench. Ginebra were coming off a loss to Magnolia.

"We came into this game with a sense of urgency," Thompson said.

For Phoenix, Marcellus Earlington scored 22. Ricci Rivero, who had 26 in Friday's [105-101 win over Meralco](/story/ricci-rivero-26-phoenix-rally-past-meralco-105-101-clinch-quarterfinal), had 10, and so did Kai Ballungay.

Ginebra are 4-5 and move into fourth place in Group B, ahead of Meralco at 4-6. A win over Blackwater on Friday, October 23, puts them in the quarterfinals. Phoenix, already through, are third at 5-4.

## Rain or Shine 125, Blackwater 123

On Saturday night, Rain or Shine clinched their quarterfinal place by two points. The Elasto Painters led by double figures late in the fourth quarter. Then Blackwater came back, and a Dalph Panopio three cut it to 124-123 late in the game.

Adrian Nocum went to the line and made one of two free throws for 125-123. Sedrick Barefield got the last shot for Blackwater, a three at the buzzer, and missed.

Aaron Fuller led Rain or Shine with 24 points, Caelan Tiongson scored 22 and Nocum 20. Kentrell Barkley scored 35 for Blackwater, Barefield scored 31 and RK Ilagan 24.

Rain or Shine are 5-3. Magnolia and Phoenix are the other Group B teams already through. Blackwater are last in the group at 3-6.

## Elsewhere

Terrafirma ended their season on Saturday with a 149-117 win over Titan Ultra. Neither team reached the quarterfinals, and Terrafirma finish the Governors' Cup at 5-7.
