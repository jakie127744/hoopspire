The Tokyo SunRockers led the Toyama Grouses 52-23 with 3:43 left in the second quarter at Toyota Arena Tokyo on Friday night. They still led 74-54 after three quarters. Toyama won 96-90 in overtime.

[Braian Angola](/player/BLeague/51000682) scored 41 points, 23 of them in the fourth quarter. His three with 42 seconds left in regulation put Toyama ahead 85-83. It was their first lead of the game. Rawle Alkins tied it with a layup 14 seconds later, and Toyama won overtime 11-5. The crowd was 6,751.

It was one of five B.League Premier games on Friday.

## Toyama 96, Tokyo SunRockers 90 (OT)

Tokyo scored first and led until the last 42 seconds of regulation. Toyama won the fourth quarter 31-11. Angola scored Toyama's first 12 points of the quarter, with two layups, two threes and two free throws, to cut it to 76-66 with 6:23 left. [Aamir Simms](/player/BLeague/51000681) scored twice inside to make it 76-70, and Angola's third three of the quarter made it 77-73.

Tokyo led 83-79 with 1:14 left after two Josh Hawkinson free throws. Angola made three free throws with 53 seconds left and then the go-ahead three. After [Rawle Alkins](/player/BLeague/51000515) tied it, the game went to overtime at 85-85.

In overtime, Simms made two free throws and later a three for a 95-88 lead with 1:34 left. Simms finished with 23 points and Darral Willis Jr. 12. [Donte Grantham](/player/BLeague/51000496) led Tokyo with 22 points off the bench and 10 rebounds, Hawkinson had 19 points and 13 rebounds, and Alkins 16. Toyama are 3-3 and Tokyo 4-2.

## Kyoto 74, Yokohama 73

Kyoto led 70-68 with 1:04 left at Nissin Electric Arena Kyoto. [Isaiah Livers](/player/BLeague/51000675) hit a three for a 71-70 Yokohama lead with 48 seconds left, then made two free throws for 73-70. [Charles Jackson](/player/BLeague/26922) answered with two free throws, and [Ryuichi Kishimoto](/player/BLeague/8655) made two more with 15 seconds left for the final score.

Jackson had 23 points and 11 rebounds, and Kishimoto 13 points and eight assists off the bench. Livers scored 20 for Yokohama. Kyoto have won four straight and are 5-1. Yokohama are 3-3.

Chiba scored the next eight points. Osaka got within four, at 94-90 with 1:02 left, and Sotto and Pardon scored the last three points of the game.

[D.J. Newbill](/player/BLeague/33085) scored 19 and [Dererk Pardon](/player/BLeague/51000321) 16 with 11 rebounds, eight of his points in the last 3:26. [Kai Sotto](/player/BLeague/51000259) had 18 points, 12 rebounds and five assists, and [Seiya Ando](/player/BLeague/8467) added 18 off the bench with four threes. [James Palmer Jr.](/player/BLeague/51000625) scored 25 for Osaka and Kyo-chang Song 20. Chiba are 2-4. Osaka have lost five in a row and are 1-5.
