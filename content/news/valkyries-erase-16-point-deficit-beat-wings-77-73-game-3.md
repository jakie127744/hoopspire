The Dallas Wings led by 16 points in the third quarter and had the Golden State Valkyries stuck without a point for 6:40. They lost anyway. The Valkyries outscored Dallas 40-20 from there and won Game 3 77-73 on Friday night at Chase Center, taking the first-round series 2-1, and [Gabby Williams](/player/WNBA/3142328) scored eight of Golden State's last 10 points. The crowd was 18,064. The second-seeded Valkyries now meet the third-seeded Las Vegas Aces in the semifinals, and Game 1 is Sunday at Chase Center.

## The Wings take the game away

Dallas started fast. [Arike Ogunbowale](/player/WNBA/3904577), who scored a playoff-record 45 in Game 2, scored six in the first quarter and Dallas led 19-14 after it. The second quarter was close, 25-23 Dallas, and when [Veronica Burton](/player/WNBA/4398935) hit a layup at 3:22 and [Tiffany Hayes](/player/WNBA/1054) made two free throws at 2:18, the Valkyries led 37-33.

That was Golden State's last lead until the fourth quarter. Dallas ran off 20 straight points, from 33 to 53, across halftime and into the third quarter. [Paige Bueckers](/player/WNBA/4433730) scored a floater and a free throw, Ogunbowale hit three jumpers in the last 1:23 of the half, Bueckers hit a 27-foot three at 6:47 of the third, and Ogunbowale's driving layup at 6:02 made it 53-37. The Valkyries did not score from 2:18 of the second quarter to 5:38 of the third.

## Burton and Hayes start the climb

The turn came on a step-back. Burton hit a 26-footer at 5:38 off a Kaila Charles pass, and Golden State outscored Dallas 17-6 the rest of the quarter. Hayes scored a layup off a Burton pass at 3:00, Williams buried a 27-foot three at 2:36, and Burton hit a turnaround at 1:57. Kiah Stokes tipped one in. Williams hit another three, from 28 feet, at 19.7 seconds, and the Valkyries trailed only 59-54 going into the fourth.

## The fourth quarter

The fourth was a scrap. [Jessica Shepard](/player/WNBA/3906949) scored twice for Dallas, Hayes hit a step-back three and Burton drove for a layup, and when Williams hit a three at 7:18 it was 63-62 Dallas. Charles tied it at 65 with a 25-foot three at 6:51, and Burton's running layup at 4:56 put Golden State ahead 67-65, its first lead since the second quarter.

Ogunbowale answered. She hit a 29-foot three at 4:33 for a 68-67 Dallas lead, made two free throws, and added a floater at 3:13 to make it 72-67. It was the last field goal the Wings scored all night.

Charles made a basket at 2:14 to cut it to 72-69, and Williams took over. She drove for a layup at 1:40 to make it 72-71, hit a 17-foot pull-up off a Burton pass at 1:17 for a 73-72 lead, and made two free throws at 9.6 seconds for 75-72. [Alysha Clark](/player/WNBA/924) made one of two free throws for Dallas at 7.7 seconds, and Williams made two more at 6.9 for the final score. The Wings scored one point in the last 3:13.

## The numbers

Golden State shot just 27 of 77 from the field (35%) and 9 of 29 from three, but it took 18 more shots than Dallas, which was 26 of 59 (44%) and 5 of 16 from three. The extra shots came from the offensive glass and the turnover column. The Valkyries had 14 offensive rebounds to six, 40 rebounds to 36, and six turnovers to Dallas's 10, and they won the fast-break points 10-2. Dallas made 16 of 19 free throws and Golden State 14 of 18. The game had five lead changes and three ties, and the Wings' largest lead was 16 to the Valkyries' four.

Williams finished with 19 points, 6 of 17 shooting, three threes and four of four at the line, and 11 of them came in the fourth quarter. Hayes scored 18 off the bench and was plus-15 in 27 minutes. Burton had 16 points, 10 rebounds, five of them offensive, and six assists. Charles scored 10. Stokes had 13 rebounds and three blocks.

For Dallas, Ogunbowale scored 30 on 11-of-21 shooting. Bueckers had 17 points and six assists, making all 10 of her free throws and three of her 14 shots. Shepard had 12 points and 12 rebounds.
