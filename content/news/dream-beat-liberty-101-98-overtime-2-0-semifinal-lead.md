[Naz Hillmon](/player/WNBA/4398915) took a pass from [Rhyne Howard](/player/WNBA/4398674) and laid it in with 20.8 seconds left in overtime. It put the Atlanta Dream ahead 99-98, and New York never got the lead back. [Angel Reese](/player/WNBA/4433402) made two free throws with 4.3 seconds left, [Breanna Stewart](/player/WNBA/2998928) missed a 32-foot three with 1.1 seconds left, and Atlanta beat the New York Liberty 101-98 in Game 2 of the WNBA semifinals on Wednesday night at State Farm Arena.

The Dream lead the best-of-five series 2-0 and are one win from the WNBA Finals. They won without [Allisha Gray](/player/WNBA/3058901), their leading scorer this season, who missed the game with the left-hand injury she suffered in Game 1.

## Fauthoux forces overtime

Atlanta went ahead 56-54 with 28.5 seconds left in the first half and stayed in front until the last 10.3 seconds of regulation. The lead was 86-80 after Howard's layup and free throw with 1:55 left. [Sabrina Ionescu](/player/WNBA/4066533) hit a three, and Stewart drove for a layup to make it 86-85 with 26.5 seconds left.

Reese made two free throws with 14.5 seconds left for an 88-85 lead. New York had one trip left. Stewart found [Marine Fauthoux](/player/WNBA/4790260), and Fauthoux made a 24-foot three with 10.3 seconds left to tie it 88-88.

It was one of four threes for Fauthoux, who scored 20 points off the bench on 8-of-10 shooting.

## Overtime: eight straight, then the answer

Howard and Reese put Atlanta up 93-88 with 3:37 left in overtime. New York scored the next eight points. Fauthoux hit a three, [Leonie Fiebich](/player/WNBA/4683006) drove for a layup, and [Jonquel Jones](/player/WNBA/2999101) made a three to put the Liberty ahead 96-93 with 2:01 left.

[Jordin Canada](/player/WNBA/3142250) answered with a short jumper. [DeWanna Bonner](/player/WNBA/869) stole a Fauthoux pass, and Reese scored to make it 97-96 Atlanta with 44.3 seconds left. Stewart laid one in at the other end for a 98-97 Liberty lead. Then came Hillmon's layup. On New York's next trip, Ionescu threw the ball out of bounds with 6.5 seconds left.

Reese scored six of Atlanta's 13 points in overtime. She finished with 23 points and seven rebounds and made all six of her free throws.

## Stewart's 35 was not enough

Stewart scored 35 points on 11-of-21 shooting, made 11 of 13 free throws, and had 11 rebounds and six assists. Jones added 21 points and eight rebounds. New York shot 55% from the field, Atlanta 40%.

Atlanta made up the gap with extra shots. The Dream took 84 shots to New York's 64. They grabbed 11 offensive rebounds to the Liberty's five, and New York committed 17 turnovers to Atlanta's nine. Atlanta also made 21 of its 23 free throws.

Canada scored 23 points with eight assists. Howard had 18 and played 44 minutes. So did Hillmon, who added 12 points, eight rebounds and five assists. [Satou Sabally](/player/WNBA/4281929) was out again for New York.

## What is next

Game 3 is on Friday, October 9, at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2. An Atlanta win sends the Dream to the WNBA Finals. If New York win, Game 4 is at Barclays Center on Sunday, October 11, at 2:00 p.m. ET on ABC. A Game 5 would be back in Atlanta on Wednesday, October 14.
