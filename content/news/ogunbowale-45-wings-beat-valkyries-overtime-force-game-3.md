[Arike Ogunbowale](/player/WNBA/3904577) scored 45 points on Wednesday night, a WNBA playoff record, and the Dallas Wings beat the Golden State Valkyries 108-100 in overtime at College Park Center to tie their first-round series at one game each. The old record was 42, shared by Napheesa Collier, Breanna Stewart and Angel McCoughtry. Ogunbowale passed all three, and 11 of her points came in the extra period. The crowd was 6,251.

It was a turnaround from Game 1, when the Valkyries won 104-80 and led by as many as 36. Ogunbowale scored 10 points that night, making 2 of her 7 shots. In Game 2 she took 42 minutes, made 12 of 23 shots and 5 of 8 threes, and went to the line 18 times, making 16.

## How the game went

[Gabby Williams](/player/WNBA/3142328) scored the first basket, and the Valkyries won the first quarter 26-23. Dallas had used an 8-0 run to come back from 13-21 down to 21-21, but Golden State got the last word of the period. Dallas then took the second quarter 27-19 to lead 50-45 at halftime. Golden State won the third quarter 29-22 and went into the fourth up 74-72.

The fourth quarter was a game of tie scores, and the lead changed hands 15 times over the night. The game was tied at 81 and again at 83, and with 1:26 left Williams hit a 14-foot jumper to put the Valkyries up 89-86. [Alysha Clark](/player/WNBA/924) cut it to 89-88 at the line, and Williams answered with two free throws for 91-88 at 1:02. Ogunbowale made a layup at 22.5 seconds to make it 91-90, and Williams made two more free throws to restore a three-point lead at 14.6 seconds, 93-90.

Dallas called timeout. Ogunbowale hit a 23-foot three, set up by [Paige Bueckers](/player/WNBA/4433730), with 4.1 seconds left to tie it at 93. Golden State challenged the call on the inbound pass, the call was overturned, a jump ball was ruled between Williams and [Jessica Shepard](/player/WNBA/3906949), Shepard gained possession, and the clock ran out.

## Overtime

Ogunbowale opened overtime with a 28-foot bank three, set up by Bueckers. [Aziaha James](/player/WNBA/4433807) added a basket, and Williams tied it at 98 with a three at 2:26. The same sequence brought flagrant fouls on Ogunbowale and Golden State's Tiffany Hayes, and each made two free throws for 100-100. Then Dallas scored the last eight points of the game. Ogunbowale hit a 24-foot step-back three at 1:46 for a 103-100 lead, Clark made two free throws, and Ogunbowale closed with free throws at 21.9 and 9.1 seconds.

Golden State scored seven points in overtime. Williams missed every shot she took in the last 1:31.

## The numbers

Dallas made 32 of 37 free throws to Golden State's 20 of 23. Both teams made 12 threes, Dallas on 23 attempts and Golden State on 27. The Wings shot 50% from the field (32 of 64) to the Valkyries' 47% (34 of 73), both teams grabbed 29 rebounds, and Dallas committed 13 turnovers to Golden State's 10. Golden State was called for 28 fouls and Dallas for 22.

Williams scored 36 points for Golden State, making 12 of 23 shots, 5 of 12 threes and all seven free throws, with seven assists and four steals. Kaila Charles scored 14 and Kayla Thornton 13. Cecilia Zandalasini, who scored 22 in Game 1, took only three shots and scored five.

Clark scored 21 off the bench on 6-of-7 shooting, and Dallas outscored Golden State by 15 in her 31 minutes. James had 13 on 4-of-5 shooting and was plus-19 in 18 minutes. Bueckers scored 10 on 3-of-13 shooting after 21 in Game 1, but she had four assists and the pass on the tying three. Shepard scored four points in 41 minutes with eight rebounds and six assists.
