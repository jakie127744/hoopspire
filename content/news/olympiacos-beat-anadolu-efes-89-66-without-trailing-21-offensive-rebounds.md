Olympiacos came into Round 4 at 2-1, one of the teams one step behind the unbeaten leaders. Against Anadolu Efes at home in Piraeus on Friday night, they never trailed. They won 89-66 in front of 5,935 and are 3-1. Efes, coached by Pablo Laso, fell to 1-3.

The game had no lead changes. Efes never led, and their only tie was at 9-9 with 6:20 left in the first quarter.

## A slow second quarter, then the third

Olympiacos won the first quarter 26-16. After the 9-9 tie they outscored Efes 17-7 over the last six minutes and 20 seconds of it. Then they scored only nine points in the second, and Efes won it 15-9 to trail 35-31 at half-time.

The third quarter settled it. From 44-36, Olympiacos scored 10 straight points for a 54-36 lead with 4:33 left in the quarter. Efes answered with eight straight to get within 58-49, but Olympiacos won the quarter 27-18 and led 62-49 going into the fourth.

## Montero closes it

Efes cut it to 73-62 with 3:29 left. [Tyrique Jones](/player/EuroLeague/rg-88753) made two free throws, and Jean Montero then scored seven straight Olympiacos points, two baskets and a three, for an 82-62 lead with 1:42 left. The lead was 23 at the end.

Montero finished with 16 points off the bench, with two steals and two blocks. Tyrique Jones scored 11 with six rebounds.

## The rebounds

Olympiacos took 49 rebounds to Efes's 31, and 21 of theirs came on the offensive glass, against Efes's eight. [Nikola Milutinov](/player/EuroLeague/rg-27707) had six offensive rebounds and nine in all in under 15 minutes. Mbaye Ndiaye had five offensive rebounds and nine points, and Tyrique Jones four.

Efes made more threes, 12 of 31 against eight of 29, but only 11 of 28 two-point shots. Olympiacos made 25 of 47. At the free throw line Olympiacos went 15 of 19 and Efes eight of 12. Cameron Payne led Efes with 12 points, PJ Dozier scored 11 and Jordan Loyd 10. Dario Šarić scored one point and missed all five of his three-point attempts.

[Sasha Vezenkov](/player/EuroLeague/rg-27768) scored nine, and Evan Fournier had seven points and six assists. Olympiacos had 23 assists to Efes's 16 and turned the ball over 13 times to Efes's 17.

## Where it leaves the table

Olympiacos join Fenerbahce and Partizan at 3-1, one game behind Panathinaikos and Valencia, who are 4-0. Efes have one win in four. It came in Round 2, a 94-84 home win over Real Madrid.
