[Yaxel Lendeborg](/player/NBA/5175737), the 11th pick in the 2026 draft, scored 17 points with nine rebounds as the Golden State Warriors beat the Los Angeles Lakers 124-98 in a preseason game at Chase Center on Tuesday night. Golden State never trailed. The game was never tied.

Both teams are 1-1 in the preseason. The Lakers played without [Luka Dončić](/player/NBA/3945274), [Austin Reaves](/player/NBA/4066457) and [Collin Sexton](/player/NBA/4277811), who did not play. The Warriors did not use [Jimmy Butler III](/player/NBA/6430), [Al Horford](/player/NBA/3213), [Kristaps Porziņģis](/player/NBA/3102531) or [Moses Moody](/player/NBA/4432171).

## Curry's 14 minutes

[Stephen Curry](/player/NBA/3975) started and played 14 minutes. He scored 16 points on 4-of-7 shooting, made five of six free throws and had five assists.

Twice he made a three and was fouled, and twice he made the free throw. The first, from 27 feet with 6:55 left in the first quarter, put Golden State ahead 27-13. The second, from 26 feet with 1:47 left in the half, made it 79-49.

[Brandin Podziemski](/player/NBA/4709138) opened the game with a three from Curry's pass and scored the first six points. Golden State were up 31-13 when Curry made two free throws with 5:57 left in the first quarter, and led 45-31 after one.

## Lendeborg's first half

Lendeborg scored seven points in the first quarter on two-point baskets and a free throw, then hit two threes in the first 2:08 of the second quarter. His basket with 4:40 left in the half, from Curry's assist, made it 68-46. He finished 7 of 11 from the field in 21 minutes.

The Warriors outscored the Lakers 36-22 in the second quarter and led 81-53 at half-time.

## The Lakers' second half

The Lakers won the third quarter 22-15. Golden State's lead reached 38 at 122-84 with 3:19 left, and the Lakers closed with the last 10 points of the game. [Cole Swider](/player/NBA/4397134) hit two threes and [Dalton Knecht](/player/NBA/4897943) made four free throws in the final 1:35.

[Sandro Mamukelashvili](/player/NBA/4278580) led Los Angeles with 15 points on 3-of-4 from three. [Jaden Hardy](/player/NBA/4868423) had 13 off the bench and [Quentin Grimes](/player/NBA/4397014) 12. [Bronny James](/player/NBA/4683774) started and had four points and five assists in 21 minutes.

## By the numbers

Golden State had 32 assists to the Lakers' 20 and outscored them 48-36 in the paint. Both teams were loose with the ball: 23 turnovers for Golden State, 22 for Los Angeles. The Warriors made 27 of 32 free throws, and [Draymond Green](/player/NBA/6589) had eight assists in 15 minutes.
