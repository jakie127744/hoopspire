The score was tied 92-92 with 52 seconds left after [Lee Yong-woo](/player/KBL/kbl-291355) hit a three for the Wonju DB Promy. [Yang Jun-seok](/player/KBL/kbl-291576) scored the last four points of the game. His basket with 32 seconds left and two free throws with six seconds left gave the Changwon LG Sakers a 96-92 win at Wonju Gymnasium on Thursday night, in front of 3,083 fans.

The game had 21 lead changes and 19 ties. LG are 2-1 and tied for third with Busan KCC. DB are 1-1.

## First quarter: nine lead changes

Neither side led by more than three in the first quarter, and the lead changed hands nine times. Yang Jun-seok's basket with three seconds left put LG ahead 23-21 at the end of the quarter.

## Second quarter: LG in front throughout

LG led for the whole of the second quarter. [Archie Goodwin III](/player/KBL/kbl-291882) scored the first five points of the quarter for a 27-21 lead, the biggest of the night for either team. DB kept it close through [Rayshaun Hammonds](/player/KBL/rg-104917) and [Henry Ellenson](/player/KBL/rg-54842), and [Ethan Alvano](/player/KBL/rg-80742)'s basket and free throw with six seconds left made it 45-43 LG at half-time.

## Third quarter: Goodwin's minute

Hammonds tied it on the first play of the half and scored 14 of DB's first 16 points in the quarter. The lead changed hands 10 times in the third. DB's biggest lead was four, at 55-51 with 5:27 left.

Ellenson's basket and free throw made it 64-63 DB with 3:26 left. Goodwin then scored six straight points in under a minute: two free throws, a dunk and a layup for a 69-64 LG lead. LG took a 73-70 lead into the fourth.

## Fourth quarter: seven ties

The fourth quarter was tied seven times. Lee's three put DB ahead 83-80 with 5:18 left, and [Carl Tamayo](/player/KBL/kbl-291699) answered with a three of his own. Alvano made two free throws for an 89-87 DB lead with 2:03 left.

[Yang Hong-seok](/player/KBL/kbl-291091) hit a three 15 seconds later to put LG ahead 90-89, the last lead change of the game. Tamayo scored for 92-89 with 1:16 left. Lee tied it again with 52 seconds left, and Yang Jun-seok finished it.

## Where it was won

LG made 12 of 26 threes, DB five of 22. That was a 21-point gap from beyond the arc. DB were better inside the arc, 30 of 47 to 24 of 43, and at the free throw line, 17 of 21 to 12 of 17, but that only made up 17 points of it.

## The imports and the rest

Hammonds led all scorers with 27 points off the bench, on 10-of-14 shooting in 23 minutes, with eight rebounds. Ellenson had 25 points and nine rebounds, and Alvano 19 points with six assists.

For LG, Goodwin scored 21 off the bench on 8-of-13 shooting. Yang Hong-seok had 20 with four threes, and Tamayo 14. [Assem Marei](/player/KBL/rg-28587) scored only nine on 3-of-13 shooting, but had 13 rebounds and nine assists. Yang Jun-seok finished with 11 points and six assists and made all four of his free throws.
