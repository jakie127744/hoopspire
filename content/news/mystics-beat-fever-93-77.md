The Washington Mystics beat the Indiana Fever 93-77 at Gainbridge Fieldhouse on Sunday afternoon, moving within a game of fifth place in the WNBA standings with two regular-season games left.

The Mystics are 26-16 and now trail the Fever, who are 27-15, by one game. Washington is level on record with the New York Liberty and the Dallas Wings. Indiana is fifth by winning percentage and one game behind the Atlanta Dream, who are 28-14. The standings reflect games completed through Sunday afternoon.

Washington took control early. It led 34-19 after the first quarter and 51-30 at halftime, and its largest lead was 26 points, at 51-25 in the closing seconds of the second quarter. Indiana never led. Neither team put together a long run: Washington's longest streak of unanswered points was seven and Indiana's was five. The Fever won the third quarter 31-26 and outscored Washington 47-42 in the second half, but after the first quarter they never got closer than 14 points, and they trailed 77-61 going into the fourth.

Indiana shot 26 of 71 from the field, 37 percent, and made 6 of 28 three-pointers. Washington shot 37 of 65, 57 percent, made 9 of 18 threes and had 27 assists to Indiana's 13. Indiana's bench scored eight points to Washington's 19.

Caitlin Clark scored 25 points for the Fever. She made all 10 of her free throws and shot 6 of 20 from the field, with five assists and five turnovers. Kelsey Mitchell added 22 points on 8-of-20 shooting, and the two guards were a combined 14 of 40 from the field. Aliyah Boston played 19 minutes, picked up five fouls and scored seven points.

For Washington, Kiki Iriafen had 21 points and 14 rebounds, Shakira Austin scored 20 with 10 rebounds, and Sonia Citron had 16 points and nine assists.

**What is next.** Washington hosts the Connecticut Sun (10-32) on Tuesday, September 22, and the Chicago Sky (15-27) on Thursday, September 24. Indiana hosts the Minnesota Lynx, who have the league's best record at 32-10, on Tuesday, September 22, and plays them again in Minnesota on Thursday, September 24.
