The Washington Mystics beat the Indiana Fever 93-77 at Gainbridge Fieldhouse on Sunday afternoon, never trailing and never allowing a tie, and moved within a game of fifth place in the WNBA standings with two regular-season games left. The crowd was 17,274.

The Mystics are 26-16 and now trail the Fever, who are 27-15, by one game. Washington is level on record with the New York Liberty and the Dallas Wings. Indiana is one game behind the Atlanta Dream, who are 28-14. The standings reflect games completed through Sunday afternoon.

## How the game went

[Georgia Amoore](/player/WNBA/4704180) scored the game's first points on a 26-foot three, set up by [Sonia Citron](/player/WNBA/4433524), and Washington was in front from there. The Mystics led 34-19 after the first quarter and 51-30 at halftime. Their biggest lead was 26 points, 51-25 with 43 seconds left in the second quarter, and the Fever made up only five of those points before the break.

Indiana won the third quarter 31-26 and the game's second half 47-42, but it was never a real threat. Washington led 77-61 going into the fourth quarter, and the closest the Fever got in the period was 14 points, at 77-63 with 9:41 to play. Washington had the last word too. With 1:52 left [Kelsey Mitchell](/player/WNBA/3142191) made a one-foot shot off an [Aliyah Boston](/player/WNBA/4432831) assist, but [Angela Dugalic](/player/WNBA/4433411) answered at 1:28 with a 10-foot jumper off a [Lauren Betts](/player/WNBA/5105737) pass, and the 16-point margin held.

## The numbers

Washington shot 57% (37 of 65), made 9 of 18 threes and had 27 assists to Indiana's 13. Indiana shot 37% (26 of 71) and made 6 of 28 threes, 21%. The Mystics scored 50 points in the paint to the Fever's 38, out-rebounded them 37-30 and turned the ball over four more times, 16 to 12. Indiana had the edge at the free-throw line, 19 of 21 to 10 of 15, and in fast-break points, 17-7. Indiana's bench scored eight points to Washington's 19.

[Caitlin Clark](/player/WNBA/4433403) scored 25 points for the Fever. She made all 10 of her free throws and shot 6 of 20 from the field and 3 of 10 from three-point range, with five assists and five turnovers, and Indiana was outscored by 14 in her 34 minutes. Mitchell added 22 points on 8-of-20 shooting, and the two guards were a combined 14 of 40 from the field. [Makayla Timpson](/player/WNBA/4433546) made all four of her shots and scored 13 with seven rebounds and three steals. Boston played 19 minutes, picked up five fouls and scored seven points.

For Washington, [Kiki Iriafen](/player/WNBA/4898384) had 21 points and 14 rebounds, six of them offensive, making 9 of 13 shots. [Shakira Austin](/player/WNBA/4398911) scored 20 on 7-of-13 shooting, making six of eight free throws, and had 10 rebounds and five assists. Citron finished with 16 points and 10 assists, and Amoore scored 11 on 4-of-6 shooting with three threes. Betts scored five points but had three steals and two blocks in 24 minutes. [Alicia Florez](/player/WNBA/5208985) came off the bench for six points and three assists, and Washington outscored Indiana by 16 in her 15 minutes.
