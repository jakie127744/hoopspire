Hiroshima Dragonflies had a two-point lead, the ball had just gone through the net on Nick Mayo's three, and there were 18 seconds left. They had won their first nine games of the season. Then Levanga Hokkaido took it away.

Keisei Tominaga's jumper was blocked by Ryo Yamazaki, but Marques Bolden grabbed the rebound, scored, was fouled, and made the free throw for a 94-93 lead with nine seconds left. Kofi Cockburn missed a layup at the other end, Gary Clark made two free throws with three seconds to go, and Christopher Smith's last three missed. Hokkaido won 96-93 on Saturday at FPCO Arena Fukuyama, and Hiroshima are no longer unbeaten.

This follows [Wednesday's roundup](/story/hiroshima-9-0-twelve-straight-in-third-beat-akita-98-84-hawkinson-31-sunrockers-come-back-at-shinshu-bleague-wednesday), when Hiroshima beat Akita to go 9-0.

This roundup covers Thursday and Saturday. The league had not posted box scores for Friday's three games or Sunday's two when this was published.

## Hokkaido 96, Hiroshima 93

**Hiroshima in front.** The Dragonflies won the first quarter 27-14, and the lead reached 20 at 36-16 early in the second.

**Hokkaido come back.** From 36-16, Hokkaido outscored Hiroshima 80-57. They won the second quarter 26-16 and the fourth 33-22. The lead changed hands 13 times and the game was tied 10 times.

**The finish.** Tominaga made two free throws to tie it at 87 and two more for a 91-90 lead with 38 seconds left. Mayo's three put Hiroshima back in front, and Bolden's three-point play answered it.

Clark finished with 24 points and 10 rebounds, Tominaga with 23 points and six assists, and John Harrar with 20 points and 16 rebounds off the bench. Dwight Ramos started and had 5 points and two assists. Cockburn led Hiroshima with 21 points and 14 rebounds, and Dwayne Evans II scored 18.

## Alvark Tokyo lose twice at home

**Thursday: Ibaraki 69, Alvark Tokyo 66.** The game was tied 13 times. Finn Delany's two free throws with eight seconds left made it 69-63, and D.J. Hogg's three at the buzzer only cut the margin. Vic Law had 29 points, 12 rebounds and seven assists for Ibaraki, and Delany had 13 points and 14 rebounds. Keyontae Johnson scored 17 for Alvark. Ray Parks Jr. played 16 minutes for Ibaraki and did not score.

**Saturday: Mikawa 91, Alvark Tokyo 82.** SeaHorses Mikawa led by 12 late in the third quarter and won the fourth 32-27. Davante Gardner scored 27, and Yudai Nishida had 25 with six threes in nine tries. Johnson and Dejon Jarreau scored 20 each for Alvark, who came into the week 8-1 and are now 8-3.

## Angola closes it for Toyama

Toyama Grouses led Shimane by 20 at 57-37 in the third quarter, and Shimane came all the way back to lead 77-74 with 2:19 left. From there Braian Angola scored all eight of Toyama's points, every one from the free-throw line. His first two cut it to 77-76, and after Yuta Okada's layup made it 79-76, two more made it 79-78 with 1:40 left. With 16 seconds left he made two for an 80-79 lead. Thirdy Ravena then blocked Okada's layup, and Angola made two more with six seconds left. Toyama won 82-79.

Angola finished with 27 points. Ravena had 7 points, five rebounds and two assists off the bench. Dexter Dennis scored 26 for Shimane.

## Kell finishes Shinshu

Utsunomiya Brex and Shinshu were tied at 78 with 1:45 left. Trey Kell III hit a three for 81-78, a jumper for 83-78 and two free throws for 85-78, and Utsunomiya won 87-80. Kell scored 25. Kevon Harris had 29 for Shinshu and Mike Daum 23.

## San-en hold on at Kobe

San-en NeoPhoenix led by 15 in the second quarter, and Kobe got within one twice in the last 24 seconds. Keigo Kimura's three made it 88-87, and Fardaws Aimaq's two free throws made it 90-89 with 10 seconds left. Jake Van Tubbergen made one of two at the other end, and Yoeli Childs missed a three with two seconds left. San-en won 91-89.

Childs scored 33 with 13 rebounds, and Aimaq had 21 points and 22 rebounds. Terence Davis scored 27 for San-en and Vitto Brown 23.

## The rest

**Sendai 91, Yokohama 72 (Thursday).** Sendai 89ERS led by 24 in the fourth quarter. Sergio El Darwich and Jake Layman scored 22 each, and Layman had 12 rebounds. Isaiah Livers scored 21 for Yokohama.

**Sendai 93, Kawasaki 82 (Saturday).** Sendai won again two days later. Layman had 23, and Jarrett Culver and El Darwich 20 each, with El Darwich adding nine assists. Jaedon LeDee scored 23 off the bench for Kawasaki.

**Akita 95, Yokohama 86 (Saturday).** Akita won the fourth quarter 30-22. Mark Smith scored 28, and Baiqing Zhao came off the bench to make all five of his threes for 18 points. James Bell scored 20 for Yokohama, who lost twice this week.

**Ibaraki 102, Altiri Chiba 87 (Saturday).** Altiri Chiba won the first quarter 36-26 and led by 14 in the second. Ibaraki won the rest of the game 76-51. Delany scored 20, and Parks had 9 points in 14 minutes off the bench. Kai Sotto did not play for Altiri Chiba.
