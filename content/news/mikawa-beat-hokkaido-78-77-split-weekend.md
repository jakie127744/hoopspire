With seven seconds left at Hokkai Kita-yale, Marques Bolden hit a three from Yuta Osaki's pass and cut Mikawa's lead to 78-77. It was as close as Levanga Hokkaido got. Mikawa turned the ball over, Hokkaido turned it straight back, and the SeaHorses held on to win 78-77 on Sunday in front of 6,158 fans. The two clubs split their B.League Premier weekend series, one game each.

Mikawa started fast. The visitors scored the first nine points and led 12-3 in the first quarter, their biggest lead of the day. Hokkaido won the second quarter 24-23 but still trailed 43-40 at halftime and 64-60 after three.

The fourth quarter was a slow climb for the home side. Davante Gardner finished a fast break to make it 68-60 with 8:49 left. Hokkaido came back with Bolden inside, an Osaki three and a Keisei Tominaga basket, and at 4:32 Tominaga made three free throws in a row to tie it 70-70.

Sean O'Mara and Yudai Nishida scored inside for a 74-70 Mikawa lead. Osaki answered with a jumper. With 45 seconds left, Gary Clark grabbed his own team's miss and scored to tie it at 74.

Mikawa went straight back inside. Yutaro Suda found O'Mara for a layup with 31 seconds left. Tominaga missed a three at the other end, Gardner took the rebound and was fouled, and he made both free throws for a 78-74 lead with nine seconds left. Bolden's three came two seconds later. Yoshiaki Kubota then lost the ball for Mikawa, but Clark gave it back with two seconds to go and Gardner came up with the steal. Nishida missed two free throws at the buzzer, and Clark took the rebound as time ran out.

The lead changed hands six times and the score was tied seven times.

Bolden led all scorers with 27 points and 13 rebounds. Tominaga added 23, making all seven of his free throws, and Clark had 12 points and 11 rebounds. For Mikawa, O'Mara had 17 points and 16 rebounds, Nishida scored 16 with four threes, and Gardner had 14.

Saturday belonged to Hokkaido. Tominaga scored 41 points in a 106-97 win, and Gardner led Mikawa with 27.

Mikawa grabbed 15 offensive rebounds on Sunday to Hokkaido's 12, and turned the ball over eight times to Hokkaido's 11.
