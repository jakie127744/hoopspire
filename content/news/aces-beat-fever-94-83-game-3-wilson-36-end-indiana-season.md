The Las Vegas Aces beat the Indiana Fever 94-83 on Thursday night at Michelob ULTRA Arena to win their first-round series 2-1 and end the Fever's season. [A'ja Wilson](/player/WNBA/3149391) scored 36 points, and the Aces made 28 of 35 free throws to Indiana's 14 of 20. The third-seeded Aces, the defending champions, move on to the best-of-five semifinals. The sixth-seeded Fever are out. The crowd was 10,388.

The series swung each way. Las Vegas won Game 1 102-85 and Indiana won Game 2 99-89. Game 3 was tied seven times and the lead changed hands seven times, all of it before halftime.

## How the game went

[Kelsey Mitchell](/player/WNBA/3142191) scored the game's first points with a 26-foot three, and the Fever led 10-2 with 7:50 left in the first quarter. The score was tied at 22 with 2:58 left, tied again at 29 with 1:19 left, and the quarter ended 31-31. Wilson scored 14 of Las Vegas's 31. [Caitlin Clark](/player/WNBA/4433403) scored 11 of Indiana's 31.

The second quarter went to the Aces, 23-17. Indiana's last lead was 34-33 with 7:51 left in the half. The last tie came at 42-42 with 3:54 left, and Las Vegas led for the rest of the night. It was up 54-48 at halftime. Indiana took the third quarter 17-16, but the Aces had led by as many as 12 in the period (67-55 with 4:32 left) and went into the fourth ahead 70-65.

## The fourth quarter

[Sophie Cunningham](/player/WNBA/3907781) hit a 27-foot three at 8:50 to cut it to 70-68. Las Vegas then scored 10 of the next 12 points. [Jackie Young](/player/WNBA/4065870) made a layup, Wilson a short shot, and [Chelsea Gray](/player/WNBA/2529122) a 13-footer that she turned into a three-point play. Wilson's free throw at 6:10 made it 80-70.

Indiana answered with a 10-2 stretch. [Aliyah Boston](/player/WNBA/4432831) hit a 26-foot three at 5:53, and Mitchell scored the next seven points, ending with a step-back three at 3:00 that made it 82-80.

It was the last time the margin was as small as two. Las Vegas outscored Indiana 12-3 from there to the end. Gray had six of the 12, including a 16-foot pull-up at 1:25 that made it 86-80. Clark answered with a seven-foot shot at 1:19, and then the game became a free throw contest. Wilson, Gray, Young and Wilson again went to the line in the last 46.8 seconds, and Las Vegas made all eight of its attempts. Las Vegas made 14 of 15 free throws in the quarter, and 14 of its 24 points in it came from the line.

## The numbers

Indiana shot 30 of 78 (38%) and made 9 of 32 threes. Las Vegas shot 29 of 64 (45%) and made 8 of 20. The Fever were called for 27 fouls and the Aces for 19. Both teams had 10 turnovers. Indiana won points in the paint 36-22 and fast-break points 11-6, and had 9 offensive rebounds to the Aces' two.

Wilson made 11 of 18 shots and 12 of 15 free throws and had 10 rebounds. Gray scored 23 in 40 minutes, including 11 in the fourth quarter, and made all six of her free throws. Young had 18 points, seven assists and eight rebounds, and [Jewell Loyd](/player/WNBA/2987869) scored 11. The Aces' starters scored 88 of the 94 points. Their bench scored six.

Mitchell led Indiana with 23 points, on 7-of-23 shooting and 3 of 11 from three. Boston scored 19 with 10 rebounds. Clark finished with 18 points on 6-of-18 shooting, 3 of 12 from three, with four assists, two rebounds, six turnovers, two steals and four blocks in 37 minutes. She scored 11 in the first quarter and seven over the last three. [Myisha Hines-Allen](/player/WNBA/3142055) came off the bench for 12 points and 12 rebounds.

## What is next

The Aces open the semifinals on Sunday, October 4, with Game 2 on Wednesday, October 7. Their opponent is the winner of the Dallas Wings and Golden State Valkyries series, which is tied 1-1 and is decided in Game 3 on Friday, October 2, at 9 p.m. ET at Chase Center in San Francisco. The Fever's season is over.
