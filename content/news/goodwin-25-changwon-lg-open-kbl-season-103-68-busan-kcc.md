Archie Goodwin settled the first night of the 2026-27 KBL season in one quarter. He scored 16 of Changwon LG's 28 points in the second, made five free throws in a row along the way, and by halftime LG led Busan KCC 53-30. It never got closer. LG won the season opener 103-68 on Saturday at Busan Sajik Gymnasium, and Goodwin finished with 25 points on 8-of-18 shooting, three threes and six made free throws in 25 minutes and 52 seconds.

## An even start

It did not look like a rout early. Choi Hyung-chan's three at 9:45 gave LG a 3-0 lead, and KCC answered with a basket from Heo Woong and a dunk from [Shawn Long](/player/KBL/rg-28798). The game was tied at 5, at 7, at 9 and at 11. Then LG scored six straight points, two baskets from Jeong In-deok and one from [Assem Marei](/player/KBL/rg-28587), to lead 15-11. LG led 25-20 after the first quarter. [Carl Tamayo](/player/KBL/rg-137851) hit a three at 2:22, and Yang Hong-seok scored a three-point play and two late free throws.

## The second quarter

Then the game turned. Jang Jae-seok scored for KCC at 7:28 of the second quarter to make it 32-24, and Busan did not score again for more than three minutes. LG ran off the next eight points while KCC missed its shots, and Goodwin did most of the damage with a basket and then five straight free throws, two at 5:05 and three at 4:28. Louis King's three at 4:12 ended the drought, but it was 40-27 by then, and LG kept going. Goodwin hit a three at 1:21 and scored the last four points of the half on two baskets, the second with one second left. LG won the quarter 28-10 and led by 23.

KCC never got inside 20 points after halftime. Yang Hong-seok scored eight in the third, Goodwin hit two threes, and LG led 75-52 after three.

## The fourth quarter

The fourth belonged to Yang Jun-seok. Marei made a free throw at 7:05, and then Yang scored LG's next eight points: a three at 6:43, a two at 6:30 and another three at 5:42 that made it 89-59. He scored 10 of his 15 points in the quarter. Tamayo scored nine of his 12 in the last 4:01 and Han Sang-hyuk added a three. LG's lead reached 36, at 101-65 with 56 seconds left. Yoon Ki-chan's three-point play at 0:42 made it 101-68, and Tamayo's basket at 0:28 set the final score.

## The numbers

LG shot 39 of 71 (55%) and made 11 of 27 threes. KCC shot 27 of 64 (42%) and made three of 18 threes, and Heo Woong missed all seven of his three-point tries. LG made 14 of 21 free throws to KCC's 11 of 18. The visitors won the rebounding 39-28 and the assists 27-15.

Goodwin led all scorers with 25. Yang Hong-seok had 22 points on 8-of-16 shooting and five of five at the line, with six rebounds and six assists. Marei had 14 points, 14 rebounds and eight assists. Yang Jun-seok made six of eight shots for 15. Tamayo had 12.

For KCC, King scored 18 with three threes and six rebounds. Long had 13 points and 15 rebounds, five of them offensive, and Jang Jae-seok scored 12. Heo Hun, who had seven assists, made two of 10 shots and scored four.

## Elsewhere on opening day

Goyang Sono beat Daegu Korea Gas Corporation 82-73 at home, and Anyang Jung Kwan Jang edged Ulsan Hyundai Mobis 88-85. That leaves Sono, LG and Jung Kwan Jang at 1-0.

## What is next

KCC plays at Ulsan Hyundai Mobis on Monday, October 5, at 2 p.m., and hosts Korea Gas Corporation on Wednesday, October 7. LG visits Suwon KT on Tuesday, October 6, at 7 p.m. (all times Korea time).
