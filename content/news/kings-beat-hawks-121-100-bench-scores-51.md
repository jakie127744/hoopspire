[Makuach Maluach](/player/NBL/4278699) came off the Sydney Kings' bench on Sunday and did not miss. He made six of six from the field, three of them threes, and all four of his free throws for 19 points in 18 minutes, and Sydney beat the Illawarra Hawks 121-100 to stay unbeaten in the NBL. The Kings were plus-21 with him on the floor, the best mark on either team.

## How the game went

Illawarra started quickly. [Kelan Martin](/player/NBL/5447958) scored the game's first basket on a 14-foot pull-up, set up by [Milton Doyle](/player/NBL/2992564), and the Hawks led by as many as five in the first quarter. They were still ahead 27-23 with 1:29 left in it. Then Sydney took over. The Kings scored the last eight points of the quarter to lead 31-27, took the lead for good at 28-27 with 1:03 to go, and kept running into the second quarter, ten straight in all, from 23-27 down to 33-27 up with 9:46 left in the half.

The Kings won the second quarter 34-18. A nine-point run, from 42-33 to 51-33, and another of eight to close the half, 57-45 to 65-45, left them up 20 at the break. The margin reached 29 points, 100-71, with 9:45 left in the game. Illawarra answered with eight straight, 100-79, and won the fourth quarter 29-23, but never got closer than 19, at 104-85 with 5:55 to play. Sydney scored the last of its points on a [Peitok Machar](/player/NBL/5447340) tip-in dunk at 1:16, 121-93, and Illawarra finished on a 7-0 run, including a 28-foot three and a layup from [Kyle Adnam](/player/NBL/5196708), to set the final margin.

## The numbers

Sydney shot 56% (42 of 75), made 10 of 25 threes and 27 of 32 free throws, and out-rebounded Illawarra 42-20, with 13 offensive rebounds to four. The Hawks shot 51% (37 of 72), made 13 of 35 threes and 13 of 17 free throws. The Kings got to the line 32 times to the Hawks' 17. Illawarra forced only 13 Sydney turnovers and had 12 of its own, and the teams each had 20 assists.

[Kendric Davis](/player/NBL/4397066) led Sydney with 31 points on 10-of-18 shooting, including 10 of 11 from the line, and had six assists. [Torrey Craig](/player/NBL/5336709) scored 20 on 8-of-11 shooting in 23 minutes. Rookie [Will Johnston](/player/NBL/5404874) added 14 off the bench on 5-of-6 shooting, with two threes and four assists. [Andrew Carr](/player/NBL/5404873) had 12 rebounds, four of them offensive, and two blocks. [Jason Spurgin](/player/NBL/4702155) scored five in nine minutes and Sydney outscored Illawarra by 17 in that time.

The Kings' bench scored 51 of their 121 points.

"The guys on the bench, the guy sitting next to me [Johnston], brought some energy and pickup and started getting stops," coach Brian Goorjian said.

[Sam Froling](/player/NBL/5091613) and Martin scored 21 each for Illawarra. Froling made nine of 13 shots. Adnam had 17 off the bench on 6-of-9 shooting, and [Tyler Harvey](/player/NBL/5090641) made four of five threes for 12 points. [Johny Narkle](/player/NBL/5161002) started and missed all three of his shots in 19 minutes. The loss leaves the Hawks without a win after three games.
