Makuach Maluach came off the Sydney Kings' bench on Sunday and did not miss. He made six of six from the field, three of them threes, and all four of his free throws for 19 points in 18 minutes, and Sydney beat the Illawarra Hawks 121-100 to stay unbeaten in the NBL.

Illawarra led for most of the first quarter, by as many as five points, and was ahead 27-26 with 1:29 left in it. Sydney scored the last five points of the quarter and never trailed again. The Kings won the second quarter 34-18 to lead 65-45 at halftime, and the margin reached 29 points, at 100-71, early in the fourth. Illawarra won the fourth quarter 29-23.

Kendric Davis led Sydney with 31 points, 10 of them from 11 free throws, and six assists. Torrey Craig scored 20 on 8-of-11 shooting. Rookie Will Johnston added 14 off the bench on 5-of-6 shooting, and Andrew Carr had 12 rebounds.

The Kings' bench scored 51 of their 121 points. Sydney outrebounded Illawarra 42-20 and made 27 of 32 free throws to the Hawks' 13 of 17.

"The guys on the bench, the guy sitting next to me [Johnston], brought some energy and pickup and started getting stops," coach Brian Goorjian said, according to [the NBL's report](https://www.nbl.com.au/news/kings-bench-depth-maluach-johnston-nbl27).

Sam Froling and Kelan Martin scored 21 each for Illawarra. Kyle Adnam had 17 off the bench, and Tyler Harvey made four of five threes for 12 points. The loss leaves the Hawks without a win after three games.
