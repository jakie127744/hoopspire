The lead changed hands five times in the last three minutes at Anyang Gymnasium on Tuesday night. The last change belonged to John Mooney. His three with 1:19 left put Anyang Jung Kwan Jang ahead 83-81, his two free throws with 39 seconds left made it 85-81, and Jung Kwan Jang beat Goyang Sono by that score.

Mooney finished with 25 points on 10-of-14 shooting and 15 rebounds. Jung Kwan Jang are 4-1, second in the KBL behind unbeaten Seoul Samsung (3-0). Sono fell to 1-4 with a fourth straight defeat.

On Monday, Ulsan Hyundai Mobis beat Wonju DB 99-89 for their first win of the season.

## Jung Kwan Jang 85, Sono 81

**First quarter.** Sono led 17-16 after a basket by Johnny O'Bryant III with 3:22 left. Jung Kwan Jang then scored the last 15 points of the quarter, a run that included threes from Byun Jun-hyung, Moon Yu-hyeon and Rhenz Abando, and led 31-17.

**Second quarter.** Sono won it 26-14. David James Jr. scored 10 of his 17 points in the quarter, and Lee Jung-hyun's three tied the game at 39-39. Jung Kwan Jang led 45-43 at half-time.

**Third quarter.** The lead changed hands four times, and the teams split the quarter 20-20. Jung Kwan Jang led 65-63.

**Fourth quarter.** It was tied at 76-76 with 3:47 left. Abando made two free throws for 78-76, Lee answered with a three for 79-78, and Mooney scored inside for 80-79. Lee made two more free throws to put Sono ahead 81-80 with 2:06 left. Mooney's three and his free throws ended it.

Lee Jung-hyun led Sono with 20 points, three threes and three steals. O'Bryant had 17 points, seven rebounds and six assists, but made 1 of 9 threes. James added 10 rebounds. Abando scored 17 for Jung Kwan Jang on 6-of-9 shooting. The crowd was 1,987.

## Hyundai Mobis 99, DB 89

Mobis led from the second minute at Dongcheon Gymnasium. The only tie was 2-2, DB never led, and Mobis were ahead by as many as 13 at 63-50 in the third quarter.

Gaige Prim scored 33 points on 14-of-20 shooting with eight rebounds. Darius Bazley had 22 points and 14 rebounds in 21 minutes, and Lee Woo-suk 15 points, nine rebounds and six assists. Henry Ellenson led DB with 28 points and 10 rebounds, and Ethan Alvano had 21 points and 10 assists. DB are 2-2. Mobis are 1-3. The crowd was 1,018.
