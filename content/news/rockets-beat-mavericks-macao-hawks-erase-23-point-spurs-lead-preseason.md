The Houston Rockets beat the Dallas Mavericks 135-117 in a preseason game at the Venetian Arena in Macao on Friday, the first of two games the teams play there as the NBA China Games 2026. Houston scored the first nine points and never trailed. The crowd was 11,564.

Six more preseason games were played in the United States on Thursday night. These are exhibition games, and every team used at least 13 players. The results do not count.

## Rockets 135, Mavericks 117

Houston led 65-45 at half-time and by as many as 28, at 111-83, early in the fourth quarter. [Tari Eason](/player/NBA/4433192) scored 20 points off the bench. [Fred VanVleet](/player/NBA/2991230) had 17, Alperen Şengün and [Reed Sheppard](/player/NBA/4711272) 16 each, and [Kevin Durant](/player/NBA/3202) 15. [Cooper Flagg](/player/NBA/5041939) led Dallas with 19 and [Naji Marshall](/player/NBA/4278594) added 16.

The teams meet again at the Venetian Arena on Sunday, October 11.

[Asa Newell](/player/NBA/4873201) scored 16 and [Kingston Flemings](/player/NBA/5149077) 15 off the bench. [Dylan Harper](/player/NBA/5037871) led the Spurs with 16.
