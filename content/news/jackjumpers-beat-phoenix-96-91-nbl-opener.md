The Tasmania JackJumpers beat the South East Melbourne Phoenix 96-91 at MyState Bank Arena on Monday night to win their opening game of the 2026-27 NBL season, coming back from a deficit that the league's match report put at 16 points.

South East Melbourne led 52-38 at halftime. Tasmania scored 36 points in the third quarter, shooting 14 of 19, according to the NBL, and the score was 74-74 going into the fourth. The JackJumpers won the second half 58-39. The quarter scores, from the box score, were Tasmania 15, 23, 36, 22 and South East Melbourne 24, 28, 22, 17.

David Johnson led Tasmania with 26 points, six rebounds and six assists on 10-of-15 shooting. Taran Armstrong scored 19 points, Will Magnay had 14 points and six assists, Dakota Mathias added 14 points and six rebounds, and Nick Marshall scored 10.

Angus Glover scored 21 points for the Phoenix and made five of his 11 three-point attempts. The NBL said 13 of his points came in a two-and-a-half-minute stretch of the second quarter. Skylar Mays had 18 points and six rebounds, D'Shawn Schwartz scored 12, Dash Daniels had 11 points and four assists, and Jordan Hunter scored nine.

Tasmania played without Josh Bannan, Ben Ayre, Anthony Drmic and Bryce Hamilton, the league's report said. South East Melbourne was without Nathan Sobey. The Phoenix had won 100-79 at Perth on Saturday.

**What is next.** The JackJumpers play the Cairns Taipans in Cairns on Wednesday, September 23. South East Melbourne plays Melbourne United in Melbourne on Thursday, September 24.

Source: the [NBL's match report](https://www.nbl.com.au/news/jackjumpers-dig-deep-to-beat-phoenix), published September 21, and the ESPN box score.
