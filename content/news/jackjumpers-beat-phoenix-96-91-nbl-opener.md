The Tasmania JackJumpers beat the South East Melbourne Phoenix 96-91 at MyState Bank Arena on Monday night to win their opening game of the 2026-27 NBL season, after trailing by 14 at halftime.

South East Melbourne led 52-38 at halftime. Tasmania scored 36 points in the third quarter and the score was 74-74 going into the fourth. The JackJumpers won the second half 58-39. By quarter, it was Tasmania 15, 23, 36, 22 and South East Melbourne 24, 28, 22, 17.

David Johnson led Tasmania with 26 points, six rebounds and six assists on 10-of-15 shooting. Taran Armstrong scored 19 points, Will Magnay had 14 points and six assists, Dakota Mathias added 14 points and six rebounds, and Nick Marshall scored 10.

Angus Glover scored 21 points for the Phoenix and made five of his 11 three-point attempts. Skylar Mays had 18 points and six rebounds, D'Shawn Schwartz scored 12, Dash Daniels had 11 points and four assists, and Jordan Hunter scored nine.

The Phoenix had won 100-79 at Perth on Saturday.

**What is next.** The JackJumpers play the Cairns Taipans in Cairns on Wednesday, September 23. South East Melbourne plays Melbourne United in Melbourne on Thursday, September 24.
