Justin Robinson's three went in as the fourth quarter ended at the Palau Blaugrana on Friday, and Barcelona had tied Olympiacos at 92-92. Tyrese Martin's three opened overtime with a 95-92 lead. It was Barcelona's last lead. Olympiacos scored the next 11 points, and won 105-100 to close Round 6 of the EuroLeague regular season.

Ten games were played on Thursday and Friday. Maccabi Tel Aviv won 91-88 at Valencia, Besiktas beat Bayern Munich 95-87 after leading by 28, and Hapoel Tel Aviv won 91-81 at Panathinaikos in front of 17,015, the largest crowd of the round.

## Olympiacos 105, Barcelona 100 (OT)

Barcelona led by eight late in the first quarter, at 30-22. Olympiacos answered with an 11-0 run across the break between quarters, won the second quarter 31-17 and led 57-47 at half-time. The lead was 14 at 74-60 late in the third.

Barcelona came back with a 9-0 run in the fourth, from 84-72 to 84-81. Codi Miller-McIntyre's three made it 90-83 with 1:42 left. Robinson hit a three for 90-88, Josh Nebo made a free throw, Sasha Vezenkov made two free throws for 92-89 with eight seconds left, and Robinson tied it from deep at the buzzer.

In overtime, Mbaye Ndiaye scored six straight points, a three, a free throw and a basket, and Jean Montero added a three and a basket for 103-95 with 42 seconds left. Barcelona's next points came with 36 seconds left.

Vezenkov finished with 25 points and made all 10 of his free throws. Miller-McIntyre and Ndiaye scored 17 each, Ndiaye off the bench. Martin and Robinson had 17 each for Barcelona, who made 16 of 41 threes. It was Barcelona's second home loss in three rounds, after [Zalgiris won 88-81 at the Palau in Round 4](/story/zalgiris-win-88-81-at-barcelona-33-16-fourth-quarter-besiktas-win-at-baskonia).

## Maccabi 91, Valencia 88

The lead changed hands 19 times in Valencia on Thursday. Maccabi led by 12 in the second quarter before Valencia answered with 10 straight points. With 2:37 left, Gonzalo Corbalán put Valencia ahead 85-83.

Valencia did not score again until 20 seconds remained. Bonzie Colson's three made it 86-85, Jimmy Clark III hit a three for 89-85 with 1:01 left and scored again for 91-85. Armoni Brooks' late three made it 91-88. Clark led Maccabi with 17 and Roman Sorkin had 16. The crowd was 10,361.

## Besiktas 95, Bayern 87

Besiktas led Bayern by 28 at 67-39 midway through the third quarter in Istanbul on Friday. Bayern then scored 19 straight, from 69-41 to 69-60 early in the fourth. Besiktas held on. Scottie Wilbekin made all five of his free throws and scored 20, and Jaylen Nowell had 21. Vladimir Lučić scored 17 for Bayern.

## Hapoel Tel Aviv 91, Panathinaikos 81

Hapoel led 48-46 at half-time in Athens and won the second half 43-35. Zach LeDay scored 17, Elijah Bryant 14 and Dan Oturu 13. Sylvain Francisco scored 22 for Panathinaikos.

## The rest of the round

**Anadolu Efes 85, Zalgiris 70.** Efes never trailed in Kaunas in front of 15,133 and led by 20 in the fourth. Matthew Strazel scored 19.

**Fenerbahce 74, Partizan 65.** Partizan led by 11 in the first quarter and 28-25 at half-time. Fenerbahce won the third quarter 34-16. Shavon Shields scored 17.

**Dubai 101, Milan 97.** Dubai led 83-68 after three quarters, and Milan won the fourth 29-18. Dwayne Bacon scored 29 and Mfiondu Kabengele 18.

**Real Madrid 111, ASVEL 92.** Andrés Feliz scored 26, and Madrid won the fourth quarter 34-21.

**Crvena Zvezda 90, Paris 81.** Chima Moneke scored 23 in front of 16,124 in Belgrade. Tyson Etienne had 24 for Paris.

**Virtus 86, Baskonia 78.** Virtus led 24-12 after the first quarter, and Marcus Carr scored 20.
