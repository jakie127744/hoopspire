Soles de Mexicali lead the LNBP's 2026 Varonil season at 17-4, 2.5 games clear of second-place Astros (15-7), with the regular season a little over two weeks from its close. Soles are outscoring opponents by 224 points this season; Astros, next-best in the league, are at plus-151. The next team after that, Panteras, is at plus-76. Eight games separate Soles from ninth place, Gambusinos (9-12) — the first team outside the eight-team playoff line.

The scoring margin says Astros are the closest thing Soles have to an equal, and the results back it up. The two met three times in eight days this month. Astros won the first meeting 82-78 on September 5, Soles won the rematch 90-76 on September 11, and Astros took the third meeting 81-74 on September 12 — Astros ahead 2-1 in a stretch against the team sitting 2.5 games above them in the standings. No other club has beaten Soles more than once this month.

Astros are not the only team to have beaten Soles twice this season. Fuerza Regia did it first, winning both meetings in the season's opening week, 94-91 on July 25 and 95-89 the following night. Fuerza Regia sit fourth now at 14-7, three games behind Soles — a fast start in July against a team that has lost only three times since. Correcaminos also beat Soles once, on August 25, and have not been within reach of them in the standings since; they sit last in the league at 4-17.

Soles have not lost since the Astros series ended on September 12. They beat Freseros twice on the road the following week, 85-73 and 92-63, then closed out last week with a 77-67 win over Panteras at home — three straight, at Auditorio PSF, the 4,779-seat home arena Soles have played out of all season under head coach Pablo García Fernández.

Third-place Dorados (14-8) are 3.5 games back and have not played Soles at all this season, so where they would actually stand head-to-head is untested. Fuerza Regia, in fourth, are the only team with a winning record against Soles this year — from a series played before either team had found its form, against a Soles team that now has the league's best record by a comfortable margin.

The LNBP's regular season runs from July 23 to October 7, with the top eight of the league's fourteen teams advancing to the playoffs: a best-of-three play-in from October 11 to 15, best-of-five zone semifinals and zone finals running through November 9, and a best-of-seven grand final from November 12 to 23. Soles, at 17-4, are already through that line with more than two weeks of the regular season left to play. The question the rest of the season has to answer is not whether Soles make the playoffs — that was settled weeks ago — but which team, if any, is built to match them once it starts. On the evidence of three games in September, Astros are still the only one that has managed it even once.

**What is next.** Soles play at Dorados on September 25 and 26, then host Diablos Rojos on October 1 and 2 and Fuerza Regia on October 6 to close the regular season.
