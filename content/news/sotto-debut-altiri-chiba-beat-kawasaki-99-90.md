The first basket of [Kai Sotto](/player/BLeague/rg-133946)'s Altiri Chiba career was a dunk. His last rebound came with 43 seconds left, in a game his new team had dragged out of a 14-point hole. Chiba beat the Kawasaki Brave Thunders 99-90 on Saturday at Chibagin Arena in front of 4,531 fans, and the 24-year-old center, whose signing the club announced on September 24, was in the middle of it. He scored 13 points, took 11 rebounds, gave out four assists and made six of his seven shots from inside the arc in 25 minutes and eight seconds.

## Sotto starts with a dunk

Chiba had lost its previous game 76-74 at Gunma on September 24, the day the club announced Sotto's signing. This was his first game in the Chiba jersey, and it began fast. [Kotetsu Kurokawa](/player/BLeague/rg-219375) scored 13 of Chiba's 23 first-quarter points, and at 4:23 Sotto threw down his first dunk to make it 17-9. Chiba led by as many as eight in the quarter, then watched Kawasaki trim it to 23-22 by the end of it.

The second quarter went the other way. Kawasaki outscored Chiba 30-20 and pulled away until it led 45-31 with 4:13 left in the half, the biggest lead of the night. [Matt Bonds](/player/BLeague/rg-70510), Muhammad-Ali Abdur-Rahkman and [Shun Matsuyama](/player/BLeague/rg-148841) scored 26 of Kawasaki's 30 points in the quarter. Chiba went to the break trailing 52-43.

## The turn in the third

Chiba came out and clawed. It tied the game at 53 with 6:04 left in the third, and Sotto spent the rest of the quarter in the paint: an assist at 4:14, an offensive rebound and a putback at 3:32, a free throw, and a dunk at 0:23 that cut it to 69-73. He picked off a steal in the last seconds. Alex Kirk's free throw and an inside basket in the last minute had put Kawasaki up 73-67 before Sotto's dunk, so the visitors still led 73-69 going into the fourth, but Chiba had won the quarter 26-21.

## A 30-17 fourth

Then came the quarter that decided it. Takaaki Kida hit a three to start it, and [D.J. Newbill](/player/BLeague/rg-22883) followed with another at 9:06 to put Chiba up 75-73, its first lead since the end of the first quarter. Kirk answered with a dunk and a layup, and Jaedon LeDee's free throw put Kawasaki back up 78-75 with 7:39 left.

Chiba answered. Sotto hit a jumper off a turnover at 7:05, [Seiya Ando](/player/BLeague/rg-81768) scored twice in 28 seconds to make it 81-78, and Sotto finished a second-chance dunk at 5:26 for an 83-78 lead. Abdur-Rahkman hit a three to cut it to 83-81, Newbill answered with a layup, and Matsuyama's jumper made it 85-83. Ando scored again, and Newbill hit a three at 3:29 to make it 90-83. Bonds answered with a three, Dererk Pardon made two free throws, and Kurokawa buried a three with 2:16 left for a 95-86 lead. Matsuyama scored again to make it 97-90 with 13 seconds left, and Kurokawa's two free throws at 0:06 closed it out. His jumper at 0:21 had made it 97-88.

Sotto grabbed five of his 11 rebounds in the fourth, three on the defensive end in the last 2:32.

## The numbers

Kurokawa finished with 30 points on 12-of-23 shooting, four of 10 from three, with seven assists in 27 minutes. [D.J. Newbill](/player/BLeague/rg-22883) had 18 points, eight rebounds and eight assists. Pardon had 15 points, 10 rebounds and seven assists. For Kawasaki, Bonds scored 24, Abdur-Rahkman 22 and Matsuyama 15.

Chiba shot 36 of 67 (54%) to Kawasaki's 35 of 71 (49%), made 10 threes to nine, and went to the line 23 times, making 17, to Kawasaki's 11 of 17. It won the rebounding 39-33 and the assist count 31-26 and committed 11 turnovers to Kawasaki's 12. Kawasaki had the edge in fast-break points, 20-10, and Chiba in the paint, 48-44. The game had six lead changes and four ties.
