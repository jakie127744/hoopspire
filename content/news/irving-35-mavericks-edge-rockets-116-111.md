[Kyrie Irving](/player/NBA/6442) scored 35 points, and the Dallas Mavericks beat the Houston Rockets 116-111 at American Airlines Center on Friday night. Irving made 12 of 19 shots and had nine assists. [Cooper Flagg](/player/NBA/5041939) scored 33 with nine rebounds and six assists, and Dallas made 42 of 81 field goals, which is 51.9 percent.

## Houston leads at halftime

Dallas led 27-20 after the first quarter. Houston led 51-44 at halftime after a second quarter in which it scored 31 to Dallas's 17.

The Rockets' biggest lead came with 33.7 seconds left in the third quarter, when they led 84-73, a margin of 11. The third quarter ended 84-76, with Dallas scoring three in the final seconds.

## Dallas comes back

Dallas scored 40 points in the fourth quarter to Houston's 27, and that quarter decided the game.

Irving made two free throws with 6.2 seconds left, and Dallas led 113-110. [Amen Thompson](/player/NBA/4684740) missed the second of his two free throws with 3.6 seconds left, and Flagg made his one free throw to make it 114-111. Irving then made both of his free throws with 3.1 seconds left, and the final score was 116-111.

Irving made five of eight threes. He had four rebounds and nine assists. Flagg made 13 of 19 shots, and he made no threes in four attempts.

[Kevin Durant](/player/NBA/3202) scored 28 for Houston on nine-of-20 shooting. [Jabari Smith Jr.](/player/NBA/4432639) scored 22 and had three rebounds in 35 minutes. [Fred VanVleet](/player/NBA/2991230) scored 16 and made three of his 10 threes. [Amen Thompson](/player/NBA/4684740) had 15 points and 39 minutes.

[Tari Eason](/player/NBA/4433192) scored 11 and grabbed 13 rebounds for Houston, and [Marcus Smart](/player/NBA/2990992) scored eight.
