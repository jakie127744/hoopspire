The Las Vegas Aces beat the Indiana Fever 102-85 on Sunday in Game 1 of their best-of-three first-round series. [A'ja Wilson](/player/WNBA/3149391) scored 38 points with 16 rebounds at Michelob ULTRA Arena, and the Aces were plus-29 with her on the floor. Las Vegas leads the series 1-0. The crowd was 10,207.

Wilson made 15 of 19 shots from the field, both of her threes and six of seven free throws in 35 minutes. She also had four blocks, two steals and four offensive rebounds. [Jackie Young](/player/WNBA/4065870) added 21 points and 10 assists, making 8 of 14 shots, and [Chelsea Gray](/player/WNBA/2529122) had 14 points and eight assists. Both guards were plus-27 in 34 minutes.

## How the game went

It was a game for about a quarter and a half. The score was tied six times in the first half, the last at 36-36 with 7:20 left in the second quarter, and Indiana's largest lead was two points, at the opening tip. The Fever led 34-33 early in the second quarter, and the last lead change was Las Vegas going up 35-34 with 7:32 left. From the 36-36 tie the Aces outscored Indiana 26-11 to lead 62-47 at halftime.

The Aces had three separate bursts that decided it. They ran off 10 straight in the first quarter, from 14-16 to 24-16, nine in a row in the second quarter, from 40-38 to 49-38, and nine more early in the third, from 62-50 to 71-50. The lead reached 27 points, 85-58, with 1:32 left in the third quarter. Indiana then scored nine straight, from 86-62 to 86-71 early in the fourth quarter, and pulled within 15 at 8:25. It got no closer, and Indiana won the fourth quarter 20-16 only because the game was already settled.

## The numbers

The Aces shot 59% from the field (38 of 64) and 9 of 19 from three. The Fever shot 45% (30 of 67) and made 12 of 31 threes. Both teams committed 16 turnovers. Las Vegas won the rebounding 31-24 and blocked six shots to Indiana's none. The Aces scored 34 points in the paint to the Fever's 36, and Indiana had the edge in fast-break points, 15-14, but it made 13 of 14 free throws to little effect.

[Caitlin Clark](/player/WNBA/4433403), listed as probable with a back injury before the game, started and played 34 minutes. She finished with 15 points and 10 assists, shot 6 of 17 from the field and 3 of 10 from three, and had six turnovers. Indiana was outscored by 18 points while she was on the floor. [Aliyah Boston](/player/WNBA/4432831), listed as questionable with a right lower leg injury, started and led Indiana with 18 points and eight rebounds in 25 minutes, making seven of 12 shots, though she finished with five fouls. [Kelsey Mitchell](/player/WNBA/3142191) scored 12 on 4-of-16 shooting. [Raven Johnson](/player/WNBA/4433797) gave Indiana 14 points on 6-of-7 shooting off the bench, and [Sophie Cunningham](/player/WNBA/3907781) scored 13, making all three of her threes.

Las Vegas got 10 points from [Jewell Loyd](/player/WNBA/2987869), who made two of three threes, and seven on perfect shooting from [Cheyenne Parker-Tyus](/player/WNBA/2529458) in 15 minutes.

The Fever played without guard Ty Harris, who broke her left fibula in the regular-season finale and will miss the rest of the season.
