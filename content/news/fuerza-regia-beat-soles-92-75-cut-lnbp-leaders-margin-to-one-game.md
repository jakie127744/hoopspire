One night after losing 88-86 at Auditorio PSF in Mexicali, Fuerza Regia came back to the same building and beat Soles 92-75 on Wednesday. The 17-point win split the two-game series and cut the gap at the top of the LNBP table to one game.

Soles are 21-7. Astros beat Gambusinos 86-71 and are 20-8, one game back. Fuerza Regia and Panteras are tied for third at 19-9.

All 14 teams played on Wednesday, in the second game of the same seven pairings as Tuesday. Every club has now played 28 games.

Soles still have the best points difference in the league, plus 292. Astros are next at plus 227, then Fuerza Regia at plus 136.

## The leaders lose at home

Soles had won 88-86 against the same opponent on Tuesday. On Wednesday Fuerza Regia held them to 75 points, 13 fewer than on Tuesday, and won by 17. It was the biggest margin of the night but one.

Astros did their part at Arena Astros. They beat Gambusinos for the second night in a row, 86-71 after 96-89 on Tuesday, and moved within a game of first place.

## Panteras by one, again

Panteras beat Diablos Rojos 77-76 on Tuesday at Auditorio Hermanos Carreón in Aguascalientes. On Wednesday, in the same building, they won 93-92. Two wins by a combined two points kept Panteras level with Fuerza Regia in third.

## Lobos and Dorados win again

Lobos split their two games in Cancún. After losing 77-68 to El Calor at Poliforum Cancún on Tuesday, they won 90-77 on Wednesday and are fifth at 18-10.

Dorados won at Inforum Irapuato for the second straight night. They beat Freseros 105-77, a 28-point margin, the biggest of the night, after a 105-85 win on Tuesday. Dorados are sixth at 17-11. Freseros are 9-19.

## Santos split in Zacatecas

Mineros won Tuesday's game 96-75 at Gimnasio Marcelino González. Santos won Wednesday's, 93-82. Mineros stay seventh at 15-13. Santos are 11-17.

## Abejas move clear in eighth

Abejas won 95-89 at Correcaminos in Ciudad Victoria, their second win there in two nights. With Diablos Rojos and Gambusinos both losing, Abejas are alone in eighth at 12-16, one game ahead of a three-way tie at 11-17: Santos, Diablos Rojos and Gambusinos. Correcaminos are 4-24.
