The Oklahoma City Thunder scored three points in the last 6:45 on Wednesday night. They had 93 when [Ajay Mitchell](/player/NBA/4900671) made two free throws at 6:45 and 96 when the game ended. The Dallas Mavericks beat them 120-96 at American Airlines Center and ended the Thunder's 4-0 start.

Dallas led 108-93 at 6:45 and outscored Oklahoma City 12-3 the rest of the way, a 12-0 run that took the lead from 15 to 27. [Cooper Flagg](/player/NBA/5041939) scored 26 points and had eight assists and no turnovers. The Mavericks are 3-2. The Thunder are 4-1.

## Dallas never trails again

Oklahoma City led 28-25 with 1:44 left in the first quarter. Dallas scored eight straight points to go ahead 33-28, and it led 33-30 at the end of the quarter. The Mavericks did not trail again after 28-28 with 1:03 left in the first.

They won the second quarter 32-29 and led 65-59 at halftime.

Then came the third quarter. Oklahoma City cut it to 65-61 at 10:19. Dallas answered with 11 straight points, and it was 76-61 at 7:50, a 15-point lead. Oklahoma City answered with an 8-0 run that made it 78-71 at 5:56. Dallas won the quarter 29-22 and led 94-81.

## The fourth quarter

Mitchell kept Oklahoma City in it early in the quarter. He scored on a 13-footer at 11:44, a 14-footer at 10:30, two floaters at 7:53 and 7:14, and two free throws at 6:45. Dallas led 108-93 at that point.

The Mavericks then scored 12 in a row between 5:26 and 2:11 to lead 120-93. Oklahoma City's only points after 6:45 were [Brooks Barnhizer](/player/NBA/4684208)'s free throw at 1:53 and a short jumper at 1:11. The Thunder were 0 for 4 from three in the fourth quarter and scored 15 in it. Dallas scored 26.
