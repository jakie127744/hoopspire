Real Madrid beat Olympiacos 93-89 at Etihad Arena in Abu Dhabi on Saturday to win the first EuroLeague SuperCup, according to the club's match report and the tournament coverage of Eurohoops and The National.

Madrid's Edy Tavares was named MVP after scoring six points with seven rebounds in the final. Sasha Vezenkov scored 22 points for Olympiacos, and Evan Fournier added 14. For Madrid, Timothé Luwawu-Cabarrot scored 12, and Jaime Pradilla and Mikael Jantunen each scored 11, Eurohoops reported.

Madrid led 24-15 after the first quarter and 47-43 at halftime, the club said. Olympiacos took its first lead of the game early in the third quarter and reached its biggest lead, 61-56, before Madrid went into the fourth quarter ahead 75-72. Madrid pushed the lead back to 10 points in the fourth. Fournier hit a three-pointer with about a minute left to make it 91-89, and Madrid held on in the final seconds.

The final was a rematch of the EuroLeague final in May, which Olympiacos won, The National noted. It was also the first trophy for Madrid under coach Pedro Martínez, Eurohoops reported.

"Congratulations to my players," Martínez said, according to The National. "They believed. We had a horrible moment in the third quarter when Olympiacos played incredible."

The SuperCup used a four-team format in Abu Dhabi. Madrid reached the final by beating Dubai Basketball 98-95 in overtime in Friday's semifinal, The National reported. Tavares scored 20 points in that game, according to the same report. Fenerbahce took third place with an 85-77 win over Dubai Basketball.

The EuroLeague regular season opens on Thursday, Sept. 24. Madrid's first game is away to Dubai Basketball at Coca-Cola Arena, the club said.
