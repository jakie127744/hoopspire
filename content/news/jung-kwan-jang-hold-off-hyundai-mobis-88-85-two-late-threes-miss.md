With three seconds left in Anyang and Jung Kwan Jang up 88-85, Darius Bazley rose for a three that would have tied it. It missed. Lee Woo-suk grabbed the offensive rebound for Ulsan Hyundai Mobis and fired a three of his own with one second left. That missed too. Anyang Jung Kwan Jang won their KBL season opener 88-85 on Saturday, after a fourth quarter in which a 16-point lead nearly disappeared.

John Mooney led Jung Kwan Jang with 25 points, 10 rebounds and three steals. Rashawn Thomas scored 20 in 20 minutes on 9-of-13 shooting, and Jung Kwan Jang were plus-18 with him on the floor. Gaige Prim scored 33 for Mobis.

## Mobis start fast

Mobis scored 14 of the first 16 points. Lee Woo-suk hit a three, Cho Han-jin another, and Prim scored inside and at the line for a 14-2 lead with 5:36 left in the first quarter. Jung Kwan Jang came back with threes from Lee Dae-heon and a late basket and free throw from Thomas, and trailed only 26-23 after one.

## The second quarter swings

Thomas put Jung Kwan Jang in front for the first time with a three at 8:28 of the second quarter, 28-26, and dunked 24 seconds later. The lead changed hands again and again. The teams were tied at 30, 35 and 37, and Bazley's three made it 40-37 Mobis at 4:27.

[Byun Jun-hyung](/player/KBL/rg-52293) answered. He hit a three at 2:42 and a jumper at 2:02, Thomas scored at 1:26 for a 46-45 lead, and Jung Kwan Jang never trailed again. Byun made two free throws and [Moon Yu-hyeon](/player/KBL/rg-202850) hit a three with 29 seconds left for a 51-45 halftime lead.

## A 15-2 run in the third

Jung Kwan Jang pulled away early in the third. From 51-48 at 8:44, they scored 15 of the next 17 points. Mooney made two free throws, a jumper, a three and another jumper. Thomas scored twice and Moon once. It was 66-50 at 4:33. Mobis stayed in it at the free-throw line, where Prim made eight free throws in the last 4:11 of the quarter, and Bazley's three at 0:17 made it 74-65 after three.

## The fourth

[Park Ji-hoon](/player/KBL/rg-121129) opened the fourth with a jumper and a three, and Mooney dunked at 8:41 for an 81-65 lead.

Then Mobis came. [Park Mu-bin](/player/KBL/rg-133883) scored seven straight, a jumper, a three and another jumper, for 81-72 at 6:42. Prim hit a three at 4:56 for 83-77, and Park another at 3:21 for 85-80. Mooney's basket at 4:32 was Jung Kwan Jang's last field goal. From there they scored three points, all free throws: two from [Han Seung-hee](/player/KBL/rg-119766) at 2:01 and one from Moon at 0:13.

Bazley scored at 0:31 for 87-83. Byun lost the ball at 0:23, and [Cho Han-jin](/player/KBL/rg-250916) stole it and scored to make it 87-85. Moon was fouled and made one of two free throws, and Mobis had the last two shots.

## The numbers

Jung Kwan Jang had 26 assists to Mobis' 16 and turned the ball over six times to Mobis' 16. Mobis won the boards 40-28 and went to the line 26 times, making 19, to Jung Kwan Jang's 10 of 13. Both teams made eight threes.

Byun had 14 points and 10 assists, and Moon eight points and eight assists. Park Ji-hoon scored nine, five of them to open the fourth quarter. [Rhenz Abando](/player/KBL/rg-200301) went 0 for 9 in 21 minutes.

Prim's 33 came on 10-of-20 shooting and 12 of 14 free throws, with seven rebounds. Bazley had 20 points and 12 rebounds but five turnovers. Park Mu-bin scored 12, 10 of them in the fourth, with five assists. Lee Woo-suk had nine points and nine rebounds.
