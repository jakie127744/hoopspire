Dorados needed one point to finish their Play-In series on Monday night at the Gimnasio Manuel Bernardo Aguirre in Chihuahua. They beat Gambusinos 88-87 and won the best-of-three series 2-0. Lobos did the same at the Gimnasio Miguel Hidalgo in Puebla, beating Freseros 75-63 for a 2-0 sweep.

The other two series are level. Abejas beat Santos 84-74 at the Domo de la Feria in León and Mineros beat Diablos Rojos 97-86 at the Gimnasio Marcelino González in Zacatecas, both a night after losing Game 1 at home. Both series go to a third game on Thursday, October 15.

## How the Play-In works

The LNBP's Play-In takes the teams that finished fifth to 12th in the regular season and pairs them in four best-of-three series: fifth against 12th, sixth against 11th, seventh against 10th and eighth against ninth. The higher seed played Games 1 and 2 at home on Sunday and Monday. The four winners join the top four, Soles, Astros, Fuerza Regia and Panteras, in the zone semifinals.

## The four series

**Lobos (5th) 2, Freseros (12th) 0.** Lobos won Game 1 105-86 on Sunday and Game 2 75-63 on Monday. Over the two games they outscored Freseros by 31 points. Lobos finished the regular season 18-10, nine games ahead of 9-19 Freseros. The sweep reversed the regular season, when Freseros won both meetings in Puebla, 82-71 and 90-86 on back-to-back nights in September.

**Dorados (6th) 2, Gambusinos (11th) 0.** Dorados won Game 1 by 15, 98-83, then held on by a single point in Game 2. Dorados were 17-11 in the regular season and Gambusinos 11-17, and Dorados had already beaten them twice in Chihuahua in August, 89-81 and 91-77.

**Mineros (7th) 1, Diablos Rojos (10th) 1.** Diablos Rojos took Game 1 in Zacatecas 98-92. Mineros answered with a 97-86 win in Game 2. Game 3 is in Mexico City at the Gimnasio Olímpico Juan de la Barrera. Mineros were 15-13 in the regular season, four games ahead of Diablos Rojos at 11-17. Their two regular-season games in Zacatecas were also split: Diablos Rojos won 77-67 and Mineros won 98-85 the next night.

**Abejas (8th) 1, Santos (9th) 1.** Santos won Game 1 in León 106-82, a 24-point road win. Abejas came back in Game 2 to win 84-74. Game 3 is at the Auditorio Miguel Barragán in San Luis Potosí. The two teams were a game apart in the regular season: Abejas 12-16, Santos 11-17. They split their two September games in León as well, Santos winning 87-86 and Abejas 92-78.
