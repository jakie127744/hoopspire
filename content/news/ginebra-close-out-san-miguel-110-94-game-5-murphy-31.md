Barangay Ginebra had two chances to end the series and missed both. On Tuesday night it took the third one. The Gin Kings beat San Miguel 110-94 in Game 5 of their Governors' Cup quarterfinal at the Ynares Center in Antipolo, won the best-of-five series 3-2, and moved into the semifinals against TNT.

San Miguel led 57-49 at half-time and 80-76 after three quarters. Then Ginebra outscored the Beermen 34-14 in the fourth, and the game was gone.

## How the series got here

Ginebra, coached by Tim Cone, won the first two games. It took Game 1 on October 27, 104-95, and Game 2 on October 30 by more than 25 points. That put the Gin Kings 2-0 up with two chances to finish the series.

San Miguel won both. It took Game 3 on November 3, and Game 4 on November 6, 108-102, and the series went to a deciding fifth game on Tuesday.

## Quarter by quarter

Ginebra led after the first quarter, 26-20. San Miguel then won the second quarter 37-23 and went to the break up eight, 57-49.

The Beermen built the lead to 80-72 late in the third quarter, and then June Mar Fajardo was hurt around his left eye. Ginebra scored the last four points of the quarter, which finished 80-76, and the third quarter went to Ginebra 27-23.

The fourth quarter belonged to Ginebra. George King, San Miguel's import, made a jumper and then fouled out with 5:13 left. He finished with 31 points. San Miguel scored 14 points in the quarter, and Fajardo finished with 20.

## Who scored for Ginebra

Jordan Murphy led Ginebra with 31 points and 11 rebounds. Scottie Thompson had 29 points, eight rebounds, two assists and five steals. RJ Abarrientos scored 19, and John Pinto, off the bench, added nine.

"For us, so much at stake in this game and it's the perfect time to bounce back sa last two losses namin," Thompson said.

Murphy, Thompson and Abarrientos scored 79 of Ginebra's 110 points, and nine Ginebra players scored in all. The fourth quarter turned a four-point deficit after three quarters into a 16-point win, a swing of 20 points.

CJ Perez scored 15 for San Miguel, Don Trollano 10 and Jerrick Ahanmisi nine. King and Fajardo accounted for 51 of the Beermen's 94 points. Eight San Miguel players scored.

## The next round

Ginebra now plays TNT. The Tropang 5G are the defending Governors' Cup champions and beat Ginebra in the finals of the last two Governors' Cups. Ginebra won the Commissioner's Cup earlier this season. The semifinals are a best-of-five series, and they begin on December 4.
