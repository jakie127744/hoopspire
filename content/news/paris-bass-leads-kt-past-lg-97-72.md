[Paris Bass](/player/KBL/kbl-291671) had 23 points, 14 rebounds and six assists, and the Suwon KT Sonicboom beat the Changwon LG Sakers 97-72 at Suwon KT Sonicboom Arena on Tuesday. KT led by as many as 27 points. Both teams are now 1-1.

KT had lost their opener at home to Seoul Samsung on Sunday. This was their first win of the season.

## First quarter: close

[Moon Jeong-hyeon](/player/KBL/kbl-291667) opened the scoring with a three, and KT led 22-16 with 3:48 left in the first quarter after a three from [Miguel Oczon](/player/KBL/kbl-291692). LG answered with eight straight points. [Yun Won-sang](/player/KBL/kbl-291363) and [Archie Goodwin III](/player/KBL/kbl-291882) hit threes, and two free throws from [Yang Hong-seok](/player/KBL/kbl-291091) at 1:34 put LG ahead 24-22, their biggest lead of the night.

The lead changed hands four times in the last 1:34 of the quarter. [Jung Chang-young](/player/KBL/kbl-290454) made two free throws with 26 seconds left, and KT led 27-26 after one. KT did not trail again.

## Second quarter: KT pull away

KT won the second quarter 26-13. [Trey Porter](/player/KBL/kbl-291885), off the bench, and Bass scored inside, and [Han Hee-won](/player/KBL/kbl-290583) added seven points in the quarter. Porter's basket with 38 seconds left made it 53-39 at half-time.

## Third quarter: LG get within eight

LG won the third quarter 20-16. [Assem Marei](/player/KBL/rg-28587) cut it to 62-54 with 2:29 left, and the margin was eight three times in the quarter. Porter made two free throws with two seconds left for a 69-59 lead after three.

## Fourth quarter: Oczon and an 11-0 run

Oczon made two threes in 22 seconds early in the fourth, the second for 77-61 at 7:05. From 82-66 with 4:16 left, KT scored 11 straight points, and the lead reached 93-66 at 2:21. KT won the quarter 28-13.

## The lines

Bass played 35 minutes and made two of four threes. Porter had 17 points and 10 rebounds in 22 minutes. Moon added 14 points and 10 rebounds, and Oczon scored 15, all but three of them from four threes.

Goodwin scored 20 off the bench for LG, with nine rebounds and five assists, but made two of his nine threes. [Jung In-duk](/player/KBL/kbl-290996) made five of eight threes for 15 points, and Yang Hong-seok made four of seven for 16. Marei had 10 points and 13 rebounds.

The attendance was 1,011.
