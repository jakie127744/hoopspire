Osaka Evessa scored six points in the first quarter on Sunday. By the middle of the fourth, SeaHorses Mikawa led them by 46. It was the widest margin of a busy B.League Premier Sunday, which also had a 26-point Shiga win a day after a home loss, a 34-point lead in Toyama and a 37-point night from Keyontae Johnson in Alvark Tokyo's win over the Chiba Jets, which we covered separately along with Kai Sotto and Altiri Chiba's loss to Kawasaki. Here is everything else.

## Sweeps

**Mikawa 101, Osaka 57.** Mikawa led 17-6 after one quarter and never let go, building the lead to 84-38 with 6:39 left. Sean O'Mara scored 26 on 10-of-12 twos with nine rebounds. Mikawa won 76-57 on Saturday, so Osaka scored 57 both days.

**Kyoto 90, Nagasaki 65.** Nagasaki scored two points in the first quarter and never led. Kyoto went up 27 in the third. Angelo Caloiaro scored 23 in 21 minutes without missing a free throw, seven of seven. Michael Phillips added 12 points and six rebounds off the bench. Kyoto also won Saturday, 84-76.

**Nagoya 90, Shimane 80.** Nagoya never trailed. They scored 26 in the first quarter and led 47-25 just before halftime, Shimane won the third quarter 30-23. Aaron Henry made nine of 11 twos for 25 points. Quentin Millora-Brown played seven minutes and scored three. Nagoya won 85-73 on Saturday.

**Hokkaido 100, Sendai 89.** Sendai led 49-39 late in the second quarter. Hokkaido won the second half 58-38 and the last quarter 31-21. Keisei Tominaga scored 22 with four threes and six of six at the line, and Dwight Ramos added 15, making three of four threes. Jarrett Culver and Jake Layman scored 28 each for Sendai. Hokkaido won 108-105 on Saturday.

**SunRockers 86, Akita 76.** Akita led 45-37 early in the third. The SunRockers scored 32 in the fourth quarter. Rawle Alkins had 30 points on 12-of-17 twos and made all six of his free throws. Mark Smith scored 24 for Akita. The SunRockers won 90-63 on Saturday.

**San-en 93, Saga 89.** Saga led 88-81 with 5:02 left. San-en scored the next 10 points, took the lead at 89-88 on a Jake Van Tubbergen basket with 1:12 to go, and Saga managed one free throw the rest of the way. Terence Davis made six of 10 threes for 27 points, and Vitto Brown scored 27 without missing a three, four of four. Damyean Dotson scored 30 and Nassir Little 28 for Saga. San-en won 97-61 on Saturday.

**Gunma 86, Ibaraki 77.** Trey Jones scored 36, 14 of them at the free-throw line on 16 attempts. A.J. Edu had eight points, nine rebounds and four assists for Gunma. Bobby Ray Parks Jr. scored five for Ibaraki. Gunma won Saturday's game 72-71.

## Splits

**Shiga 98, Ryukyu 72.** A day after losing 94-87 at home, Shiga led by as many as 31, at 66-35 in the third quarter. Zach Auguste scored 22 and Ryan Kriener 21. Jack Cooley took 17 rebounds for Ryukyu. Kiefer Ravena scored eight off the bench and made one of eight threes.

**Utsunomiya 99, Toyama 77.** Toyama led 22-14 late in the first quarter. Utsunomiya scored 37 in the third and led 97-63 with 4:40 left. Isaac Fotu scored 17, and Makoto Hiejima made all three of his threes. Thirdy Ravena scored 11 for Toyama on 5-of-6 twos, a day after scoring 19 in Toyama's 77-67 win.

**Shinshu 89, Yokohama 84.** Yokohama scored 28 in the first quarter and led by 10 in the second. Shinshu led 89-81 with two seconds left. Mike Daum scored 21 with 11 rebounds and five threes, and Marcus Lee had 20 points and 12 rebounds. Gakuto Arai scored 25 for Yokohama, which won 85-78 on Saturday.

## One game

**Hiroshima 83, Kobe 72.** Hiroshima led 61-37 in the third quarter. Kobe won the fourth 32-22 but never got close enough. Dwayne Evans II had 30 points, 11 rebounds and seven assists and made 13 of 16 free throws. Kofi Cockburn added 15 points and 14 rebounds. Yoeli Childs scored 20 for Kobe.
