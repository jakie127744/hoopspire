The Indiana Fever open the WNBA playoffs at the Las Vegas Aces on Sunday at 4 p.m. ET, without guard Ty Harris and with [Caitlin Clark](/player/WNBA/4433403) listed as probable. Game 1 of the best-of-three first-round series is at Michelob ULTRA Arena.

## The injuries

Harris will miss the rest of the season. She broke her left fibula in the third quarter of Thursday's 86-66 loss at Minnesota, the last regular-season game for both teams, and the Fever announced the news on Saturday. She is a real loss for a team that leaned on her in this matchup. Harris started the first meeting with the Aces in July and handed out five assists, and in the second she came off the bench for 10 points on 3-of-5 shooting, with Indiana outscoring Las Vegas by 35 points in her 23 minutes.

The team's injury report listed Clark as probable with a back injury and [Aliyah Boston](/player/WNBA/4432831) as questionable with a right lower leg injury. Boston played 13 minutes in the finale and Clark played 18.

## The matchup

Las Vegas is the third seed at 31-13 and won its last seven regular-season games. Indiana is the sixth seed at 28-16. The league seeds its top eight teams by record, regardless of conference.

Indiana won the season series 2-1, and both wins came in Las Vegas. The Fever won 84-68 on July 5, leading by as many as 18, and 109-75 on July 12, when they led by 34 and shot 56% while holding the Aces to 39%. Neither Clark nor A'ja Wilson played in the July 5 game. [Kelsey Mitchell](/player/WNBA/3142191) scored 27 in each of those two wins, and Boston had 18 points and 10 rebounds in the first and 19 points and 11 rebounds in the second. [Sophie Cunningham](/player/WNBA/3907781) made six of seven threes for 20 points in the July 12 game.

The Aces won the only meeting in Indianapolis, 86-84 in overtime on August 6, after a game with seven ties and 12 lead changes. The Fever had led by 20 in the second quarter, 42-22. [A'ja Wilson](/player/WNBA/3149391) had 26 points and 13 rebounds that night, on 10-of-25 shooting. Clark scored 20 points with eight assists and eight turnovers, and Indiana committed 21 turnovers to the Aces' 16. Mitchell scored 28 and Boston had 21 points and 12 rebounds. [Jackie Young](/player/WNBA/4065870) had 22 points and nine assists for Las Vegas.

## The players

Wilson averaged 26.2 points and 9.4 rebounds in 41 regular-season games. For Indiana, Mitchell averaged 24.7 points in 44 games. Clark averaged 22.3 points and 8.3 assists in 40 games. Boston averaged 16.1 points and 8.0 rebounds in 40 games.

The simplest way to read the series is that Wilson has been the Aces' whole offense when the shots fall, and that in the two Indiana wins the Aces shot under 41% from the field and made four of 19 and four of 17 from three. In the Aces' one win, the margin was the Fever's 21 turnovers and Wilson's 13 rebounds.

**What is next.** Game 2 is in Indianapolis on Tuesday, September 29, at 6:30 p.m. ET at Gainbridge Fieldhouse. If a third game is needed, it is in Las Vegas on Thursday, October 1, with the time to be announced. The winner moves on to the best-of-five semifinals, which begin on October 4.
