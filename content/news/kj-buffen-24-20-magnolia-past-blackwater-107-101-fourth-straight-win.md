Blackwater had cut a 13-point Magnolia lead to 100-97 with about three minutes left at Ninoy Aquino Stadium on Friday when KJ Buffen took over. The Magnolia import made two free throws, then hit a three off a Jerom Lastimosa pass, and the Hotshots led 105-97 with 2:10 left. They held on to win 107-101 in the PBA Governors' Cup.

Buffen finished with 24 points and 20 rebounds, a 20-20 night. Lastimosa also scored 24 and had seven assists. It was Magnolia's fourth win in a row, and it lifted them to 5-3 after a 1-3 start to the conference. Blackwater fell to 3-4.

## The first half: close all the way

Blackwater led 25-23 after the first quarter. Magnolia took the second 30-25 and went to half-time ahead 53-50.

## The third quarter: Magnolia pull away

The Hotshots won the third quarter 31-25 and led 84-75 going into the fourth. Their biggest lead was 13, at 93-80.

## The finish

Magnolia led 100-97 with about three minutes left. Buffen answered with his five straight points. Kentrell Barkley and Barefield then made free throws to bring Blackwater within 105-101 with 39 seconds left.

Magnolia's next defensive trip ended with a Chris Koon steal, and Lastimosa made two free throws with 3.6 seconds left for the final score.

## The glass

Magnolia won the rebounding battle by at least 18. The two reports of the game have it at 55-36 and 55-37, and Buffen alone took 20.

The scoring was concentrated on both sides. Buffen and Lastimosa combined for 48 of Magnolia's 107 points, and Barefield and Barkley for 44 of Blackwater's 101. Blackwater won the first quarter 25-23 and the fourth 26-23, and lost the two in between by 11 combined.

## The rest of the lines

Zav Lucero scored 17 for Magnolia, and Koon and Yukien Andrada had nine each. Barefield led Blackwater with 24 points. Barkley had 20 points, 13 rebounds and eight assists, and Mike DiGregorio added 15.

Blackwater had beaten Magnolia 89-81 when the teams first met in this conference on July 17. That was Magnolia's second loss in a 1-3 start. The four-game run began with an 88-73 win over Barangay Ginebra on August 2.

Magnolia are 5-3. Rain or Shine and Phoenix are 4-3. Blackwater and Barangay Ginebra are 3-4, after Ginebra beat Rain or Shine 129-111 later on Friday. Meralco are 3-5.
