The score was 112-112 with a minute left in overtime at Paycom Center on Tuesday night. [Zhaire Smith](/player/NBA/4277923) pulled up from 19 feet and hit it with 45.6 seconds to go, and the Oklahoma City Thunder held on to beat the Indiana Pacers 114-112 in a preseason game. A minute earlier his 26-foot three had made it 112-109. He scored the last five Oklahoma City points and played five minutes all night.

It was one of five preseason games on Tuesday. Toronto beat New York 148-98, Orlando beat Cleveland 132-116, Golden State beat the Lakers 101-89 at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas, and Portland won 110-98 at Sacramento. These games do not count in the standings, and several teams rested regulars.

## Thunder 114, Pacers 112 (overtime)

Indiana scored 33 points in the first quarter and led 52-32 when [Kelly Oubre Jr.](/player/NBA/3133603) hit a three with 7:14 left in the second. The Thunder answered with 13 straight points from five different scorers and trailed only 61-59 at half-time.

Oklahoma City went back in front at 70-69 on two [Aday Mara](/player/NBA/5174983) free throws with 7:07 left in the third. [Jared McCain](/player/NBA/4683778) then scored five points in 28 seconds, and the Thunder led 80-73. There were 14 lead changes and 10 ties in all.

The Pacers went on an 8-0 run late in the fourth to turn a 94-87 deficit into a 95-94 lead. Brooks Barnhizer's hook made it 100-98 with 35.9 seconds left, and [Kobe Brown](/player/NBA/4431752) tied it at 100 with 5.6 seconds left on a pass from Braden Smith. In overtime Rienk Mast's three tied it 112-112 before Zhaire Smith's jumper. Braden Smith missed a three and then an eight-footer with 3.5 seconds left.

McCain led Oklahoma City with 20 points, all six of his baskets from three. [Jalen Williams](/player/NBA/4593803) had 16 and [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](/player/NBA/4278073) 15 on 5-of-6 shooting with six assists in 23 minutes. [Tyrese Haliburton](/player/NBA/4396993) scored 24 for Indiana on 9-of-14 in 18 minutes, and [Ivica Zubac](/player/NBA/4017837) had 13 points and 13 rebounds in 18. The Pacers turned the ball over 27 times, and the Thunder had 14 steals.

## Raptors 148, Knicks 98

Toronto led 42-22 after one quarter and 86-47 at half-time, and the lead reached 53 at 146-93 with 2:09 left. [Kawhi Leonard](/player/NBA/6450) scored 21 in 17 minutes with four threes, [Scottie Barnes](/player/NBA/4433134) had 19 on 8-of-10, and [Collin Murray-Boyles](/player/NBA/5093267) scored 19 off the bench on 7-of-7 shooting from the field. New York made 7 of 41 threes. Jalen Brunson, Karl-Anthony Towns, OG Anunoby, Mikal Bridges and Josh Hart did not appear in the box score. [James Wiseman](/player/NBA/4432808) led the Knicks with 18 points and 10 rebounds. The crowd at Scotiabank Arena was 17,884.
