With 1:41 left in overtime at the Brisbane Entertainment Centre, [Arnas Velička](/player/NBL/5447896) buried a 25-foot three and the Bullets and the Tasmania JackJumpers were level at 99. Brisbane did not score again. [Will Magnay](/player/NBL/4066480) answered with a hook, [Taran Armstrong](/player/NBL/5447897) scored five points in the last 45 seconds, and Magnay hit a three from Armstrong with two seconds left. Tasmania won 109-99 on Saturday night, its second win in three days.

Armstrong finished with 29 points. The JackJumpers are 3-1, still without [Majok Deng](/story/majok-deng-fractured-fibula-jackjumpers-out-four-weeks), Bryce Hamilton, Anthony Drmic and Ben Ayre. Brisbane, beaten by the Sydney Kings earlier in the round for its first loss of the season, is 2-2.

## Brisbane's first half

The Bullets owned the first half. They led 23-17 after one quarter and pushed it to 47-33 with 1:34 left in the second, a 14-point lead. Tasmania got back to 47-40 by the break.

## The 13-0 third quarter

Then the game flipped. Brisbane led 59-56 with 5:34 left in the third quarter, and Tasmania scored the next 13 points, to 69-59 at 2:42. The JackJumpers won the quarter 34-18 and led 74-65 after three. A basket early in the fourth made it 76-65 at 9:50, Tasmania's biggest lead.

## Brisbane's 11-0 answer

The Bullets were down nine, 89-80, with 4:42 to play, and Tasmania did not score again in regulation until the last 22 seconds. Brisbane scored 11 straight. [Jaylin Williams](/player/NBL/5447895) dunked on a feed from [Tyrell Harrison](/player/NBL/4278785) to tie it 89-89 at 1:13. Harrison put Brisbane ahead 91-89 with a hook from Velička at 0:26.

Four seconds later [Josh Bannan](/player/NBL/4702058) took a pass from Armstrong and laid it in. Tied at 91, and overtime.

## Overtime

Brisbane led 96-95 with three minutes left in the extra period, and Tasmania went back in front at 97-96 with 2:51 to go. Brisbane never led again. Velička had missed all 11 of his shots in regulation. He made two in overtime, the 25-footer that tied it at 99 among them, and finished with 13 assists.

Then the JackJumpers closed it. Magnay's nine-foot hook at 1:24 made it 101-99. Armstrong scored and drew the foul at 0:45, made both free throws at 0:16, and found Magnay for the last three. Tasmania won overtime 18-8.

"I say we're way ahead of schedule," Brisbane coach Will Weaver said.

## The numbers

Armstrong made nine of 17 shots, four of seven threes and all seven of his free throws, and had four assists. Magnay scored 22 on 10-of-14 shooting with nine rebounds, six assists and four blocks. Bannan had 22 points, 11 rebounds and five assists, and [Dakota Mathias](/player/NBL/3136991) 19 points and three steals. Tasmania was plus-24 with Mathias on the floor, the best mark in the game.

Harrison led Brisbane with 24 points and 12 rebounds. [Nate Hinton](/player/NBL/5447894) and [Max Mackinnon](/player/NBL/5447892) scored 21 each, Mackinnon with five of eight threes.

Brisbane won the boards 41-32 and had 27 assists to Tasmania's 22. Tasmania turned it over 10 times to Brisbane's 16 and went to the line 27 times to Brisbane's 14.

Chris Fagan, the Tasmanian who has just coached the Brisbane Lions to a third straight AFL premiership, sat courtside in a JackJumpers jersey. "His record is undefeated, every time he seems to show up we end up winning," Tasmania coach Scott Roth said.
