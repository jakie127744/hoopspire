With 14 seconds left in Honolulu, [Keaton Wagler](/player/NBA/5254165) stepped back from 19 feet and put the Los Angeles Clippers ahead 102-101. The 19-year-old rookie, the fifth pick in the 2026 draft, had made two of his first 14 shots. The Clippers held on and beat the Golden State Warriors 104-101 on Sunday at the Stan Sheriff Center in the preseason opener for both teams.

[Rui Hachimura](/player/NBA/4066648) led the Clippers with 21 points in 15 minutes. [Stephen Curry](/player/NBA/3975) scored 10 in 13 minutes for Golden State and did not play in the second half.

## The last 80 seconds

Malevy Leons tied it at 97 with a layup with 1:19 left. Then it turned on almost every possession.

- Miles Kelly drove for a layup: Warriors 99-97 with 59.9 seconds left.
- [Blake Wesley](/player/NBA/4683935) hit a pull-up and the free throw: Clippers 100-99 at 0:45.7.
- [Graham Ike](/player/NBA/4703396) tipped in a miss: Warriors 101-100 at 0:27.5.
- Wagler's step-back: Clippers 102-101 at 0:14.0.

Golden State called timeout. Kelly drove again, and Wesley blocked his layup with 3.9 seconds left. [Cam Christie](/player/NBA/4845363) took the rebound, was fouled and made both free throws for 104-101. Ike's 35-foot three at the buzzer missed.

## How it got there

Curry set up [Yaxel Lendeborg](/player/NBA/5175737), Golden State's own 2026 rookie and the 11th pick, for a three and the game's first basket. The Clippers led 55-43 at halftime and by 13, 60-47, early in the third quarter. Hachimura made eight of 11 shots and four of five threes.

Golden State won the third quarter 30-22 and opened the fourth with nine straight points to lead 82-77. The Warriors' biggest lead was six, 88-82, with 7:20 left. The teams were tied four times in the last four minutes, and the lead changed hands 17 times in the game.

## The numbers

Golden State scored 62 points in the paint to the Clippers' 34 and had 15 steals. The Clippers turned the ball over 21 times. They made it up at the line and from three: 29 of 38 free throws to the Warriors' 21 of 31, and 11 of 36 threes to six of 33.

[Darius Garland](/player/NBA/4396907) scored 15 without missing, four of four from the field, three of three from three and four of five free throws, with six assists. Wagler finished with 15 points on 3-of-15 shooting, one of 11 from three, and made eight of nine free throws.

Charles Bassey led Golden State with 12 points and eight rebounds. Ike and Brandon Williams scored 11 each. Lendeborg had four points, nine rebounds and three assists. Jimmy Butler III and Kristaps Porziņģis did not play.

The crowd was 10,112.
