The last four of the FIBA U18 Women's Asia Cup 2026 Division A, the top division of the continent's under-18 women's championship, were settled on Thursday at the Karisma Arena in Seremban, Malaysia. Korea beat Chinese Taipei 67-64 and China beat the Philippines 112-49 in the qualification to the semi-finals.

They join the two group winners, Australia and Japan, who both went 3-0. The four semi-finalists are the only countries ever to have won this tournament.

## Korea 67, Chinese Taipei 64

Korea scored 24 points in the first quarter to Chinese Taipei's 10 and never trailed. They led for 39 minutes and 33 seconds, the game had no lead changes, and Korea's biggest lead was 18.

Chinese Taipei won the second half 39-28 and came close at the end. Korea led 65-56 with 3:32 left after the second three of the game from Lim Yeonseo. Lee Sohee's jumper made it 67-62 with about two minutes left. They were Korea's last points. Huang I-chen scored from close range with 1:48 left to cut it to 67-64, but Chinese Taipei did not score again.

Lim finished with 23 points, nine rebounds and five steals. Cho Heewon scored 17 and made all eight of her free throws. Kim Damhee had nine points and 18 rebounds. Lin Nian-zhen led Chinese Taipei with 14, Huang had 13 points and 10 rebounds, and Wu Hsuan-ling 11 points and seven rebounds.

## China 112, Philippines 49

China, champions 16 times, opened on a 10-0 run and led from start to finish. A 24-0 run across the end of the third quarter and the start of the fourth made it 87-39. Their biggest lead was 63.

China shot 66.2% from the field, 45 of 68, made 14 threes and had 30 assists. Li Yuanshan scored 21 on 8-of-11 shooting, with five threes. Sun Hanyun added 19, Hu Ruiyan 16 with six assists, and Yao Zixuan 14 with seven rebounds and five assists. Grace Curley led the Philippines with 17 points and five rebounds.

## Malaysia go down

Earlier on Thursday, New Zealand beat Malaysia 78-40 in the game for seventh place. New Zealand keep their place in Division A, and Malaysia, the hosts, drop to Division B. Madelin Eliadis scored 13 for New Zealand.

## What is next

Friday, October 9, local time in Seremban (GMT+8):

- **14:00, fifth place:** Philippines v Chinese Taipei. The winner goes to the FIBA U19 Women's Basketball World Cup 2027 with the four semi-finalists. China qualify as hosts of that World Cup.
- **16:30, semi-final:** Japan v Korea.
- **19:00, semi-final:** Australia v China, a rematch of the last two finals, both won by Australia.

The tournament ends on Saturday, October 10.
