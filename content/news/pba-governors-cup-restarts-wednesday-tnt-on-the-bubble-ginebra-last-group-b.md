Almost eight weeks of waiting end on Wednesday. The PBA Governors' Cup comes back on October 7 with the defending champion, TNT, holding the last playoff place in Group A by a single game, and the Commissioner's Cup champions, Barangay Ginebra San Miguel, at the bottom of Group B at 2-4. NLEX and San Miguel lead Group A at 7-2.

TNT's first game back is against the team chasing it.

The conference was paused from August 15 to October 6 to make room for the preparations for the FIBA World Cup Asian Qualifiers and the Asian Games. The last games before the break, on August 11 to 14, included NLEX's 118-111 overtime win over TNT and Converge's 112-110 win over NLEX.

## How the playoffs work

The 13 teams are split into two groups. Group A has seven teams, including the guest team Macau, which plays as the Giant Pandas, and each Group A team plays 12 elimination games. Group B has six teams, and each plays 10. The top four in each group go through to the quarterfinals, which are crossover best-of-five series: first in Group A plays fourth in Group B, second plays third, and so on. The semifinals are best-of-five and the finals are best-of-seven. The elimination round runs through the second half of October.

NLEX and San Miguel each have a two-game lead over Converge. TNT plays Terrafirma on Wednesday in the second game of the restart, at 7:30 p.m. at Ninoy Aquino Stadium. Terrafirma is a game behind TNT in the standings and has played the same number of games, so that game is a direct fight for fourth place.

TNT has four games left, more than any of the three teams above it, and it trails Converge by one win with a game in hand. Titan Ultra at 2-7 can finish no better than 5-7.

NLEX opens the restart against Macau at 5:15 p.m. on October 7. NLEX is under new head coach Jimmy Alapag, who replaced Jong Uichico this season.

Group B is the more crowded race. Rain or Shine leads at 4-2, and Magnolia and Phoenix are both 4-3. Blackwater is 3-3. Meralco is 3-5 with only two games left, so it can finish no better than 5-5. Ginebra, which beat TNT in seven games to win the Commissioner's Cup in June, is a game behind Blackwater with the same number of games played and four left.

Ginebra has four games left, the same as Rain or Shine and Blackwater.

## What is next

Wednesday, October 7, at Ninoy Aquino Stadium: NLEX v Macau at 5:15 p.m., then Terrafirma v TNT at 7:30 p.m.

Friday, October 9: Blackwater v Magnolia and Ginebra v Rain or Shine, Ginebra's first game back.

Saturday, October 10: Macau v Terrafirma, then TNT v San Miguel, TNT's second game in four days.
