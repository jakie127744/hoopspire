Kang Sang-jae hit a three with 51 seconds left in the third quarter in Wonju to tie the game at 71. Twenty-seven seconds later he hit another. Wonju DB never trailed again and beat Goyang Sono 95-86 on Sunday, a day after Sono had opened its season by beating Daegu Korea Gas 82-73.

Five games made up the KBL's first weekend. We covered three of them separately: [Changwon LG's 103-68 rout of Busan KCC](/story/goodwin-25-changwon-lg-open-kbl-season-103-68-busan-kcc), [Anyang Jung Kwan Jang's 88-85 win over Ulsan Hyundai Mobis](/story/jung-kwan-jang-hold-off-hyundai-mobis-88-85-two-late-threes-miss) and [Seoul Samsung's 81-76 win at Suwon KT](/story/kanter-30-samsung-win-at-kt-81-76-despite-bass-35). Here are the other two.

## DB 95, Sono 86 (Sunday, Wonju)

Sono pulled ahead in the second quarter and went into the break up 54-45, after [Lim Dong-seop](/player/KBL/rg-44552) hit two threes in the last 1:14 and [Lee Jung-hyun](/player/KBL/rg-108030) another at the buzzer. Lim made five of seven threes for 19 points.

David James Jr. made two free throws at 4:37 of the third for a 66-56 Sono lead, its biggest of the game. DB scored 18 of the last 23 points of the quarter. [Kang Sang-jae](/player/KBL/rg-52290) started it with a three at 4:18, [Ethan Alvano](/player/KBL/rg-80742) and [Rayshaun Hammonds](/player/KBL/rg-104917) scored inside, [Lee Yong-woo](/player/KBL/rg-175452) hit a three, and Kang's two threes at 0:51 and 0:24 made it 74-71 DB after three.

DB opened the fourth with threes from Lee Yong-woo and [Lee Yu-jin](/player/KBL/rg-199649) and led 85-74 by 6:12. Hammonds scored five straight, and Kang's fifth three at 2:36 made it 95-79.

Kang finished with 19 points on five of six threes. [Henry Ellenson](/player/KBL/rg-54842) also scored 19, 10 of them in the first quarter, and Alvano had 15 points, 11 rebounds and nine assists. DB made 15 of 38 threes and took 40 rebounds to Sono's 38.

Johnny O'Bryant III scored 26 for Sono on 11-of-23 shooting with three threes. Kevin Quiambao, the Filipino forward, scored seven and went 0 for 5 from three.

## Sono 82, Korea Gas 73 (Saturday, Goyang)

Korea Gas led by four early, 11-7, their biggest lead, and the game was 22-22 after one quarter. Sono led 47-43 at halftime and 64-60 after three, then pulled away. With 5:56 left Sono led 73-62, its biggest lead.

[Johnny O'Bryant III](/player/KBL/rg-24170) scored 26, making four of eight threes. [Kevin Quiambao](/player/KBL/rg-200302) had 16 points and six assists, and David James Jr. 13 points and eight rebounds. Sono had 25 assists to Korea Gas' 18.

[Ra Gun-ah](/player/KBL/rg-23297) led Korea Gas with 21 points on 7-of-9 shooting and 12 rebounds, and made all six of his free throws. Keve Aluma scored 16. [SJ Belangel](/player/KBL/rg-182444), the Filipino guard, had two points and six assists.
