The Stockton Kings traded their first-round pick in the 2026 NBA G League Player Draft to the Laketown Squadron on Tuesday for the returning player rights to guard Trhae Mitchell. The draft is on Saturday, October 24, at noon Central time.

Stockton are the G League affiliate of the Sacramento Kings. The Squadron are the affiliate of the New Orleans Pelicans. The pick Laketown receive started out as Cleveland's and reached Stockton through Delaware and Coachella Valley.

## Who Stockton get

Mitchell is a 6-foot-6 guard with six seasons in the G League, for the Rio Grande Valley Vipers and the Squadron. He has played 243 games, counting the Tip-Off Tournament and the regular season, and started 137 of them.

He won the 2021-22 G League championship with Rio Grande Valley and was named to the 2023-24 All-Defensive Team. Across 168 regular-season games he has averaged 7.1 points, 4.3 rebounds, 1.6 assists and 1.2 steals in 27.5 minutes, shooting 47.8% from the field and 35.7% from three.

His last two seasons were with the Squadron: 98 games and 39 starts. In 2025-26 he averaged 5.2 points, 2.0 rebounds and 1.3 assists in 23.4 minutes a game, across the Tip-Off Tournament and the regular season.

Before turning professional, Mitchell played three seasons at South Alabama. In 2018-19 he was named third-team All-Sun Belt after averaging 13.6 points, 7.6 rebounds and 3.6 assists, and led the Jaguars in rebounds, assists, steals and blocks.

## Stockton's busy autumn

This is Stockton's third trade since the middle of September.

- **September 16:** a three-team trade with the Capital City Go-Go and the Sioux Falls Skyforce. Stockton received the returning player rights to forward Rechon "Leaky" Black and guard Damari Monsanto from Capital City and forward Alex Fudge from Sioux Falls. They sent Sioux Falls the rights to center Mo Bamba and guard Dexter Dennis, plus a 2027 first-round pick.
- **October 1:** another three-team trade, with the Long Island Nets and the Rio Grande Valley Vipers. Stockton sent out Fudge's rights and received the rights to center David Muoka from Long Island and forward Kacper Klaczek from Rio Grande Valley. Muoka, who is 6-foot-10, has played 131 G League games, 49 of them as a starter, across three seasons.
- **October 6:** the Mitchell trade.

## Laketown's moves

The Squadron have been busy too. On September 7 they acquired the returning player rights to forward Trevon Scott and a 2027 first-round pick from the Long Island Nets. Scott averaged 12.0 points and 4.7 rebounds in 47 games for Long Island last season.

On September 10 they sent a 2027 first-round pick to Raptors 905 for the rights to Aziz Bandaogo, a 7-foot center from Senegal whom Raptors 905 took second overall in the 2026 G League International Draft in July. He played college basketball at Cincinnati until 2025 and spent his first professional season with a club overseas.

The Mitchell trade gives the Squadron a first-round pick in this month's draft.

## What is next

The 2026 NBA G League Player Draft is on Saturday, October 24, at noon Central time. Laketown hold the former Stockton pick. Stockton head into the draft with Mitchell's rights and the four players they collected in September and October.
