The Indiana Fever beat the Minnesota Lynx 96-77 on Tuesday night at Gainbridge Fieldhouse, and four of Minnesota's five starters did not play after halftime. The Lynx clinched the No. 1 seed in the WNBA playoffs later that night, when Portland beat Golden State 90-82 in overtime. The crowd was 16,521.

[Caitlin Clark](/player/WNBA/4433403) scored 27 points with nine assists, seven rebounds and four steals for Indiana, shooting 9 of 18 from the field and 6 of 6 from the free-throw line, in 33 minutes. [Kelsey Mitchell](/player/WNBA/3142191) scored 20 points, and [Aliyah Boston](/player/WNBA/4432831) had 17 points on 7-of-9 shooting and was plus-23 in 30 minutes.

## A game that was decided early

Minnesota scored first, and Indiana took the lead for good at 9-7 with 6:52 left in the first quarter. The Fever ran off eight straight points there, and eight more at the end of the quarter, from 23-16 to 31-16. They led 31-16 after the first quarter and 57-34 at halftime. A 10-0 run in the second, from 45-26 to 55-26, pushed the lead to 29 points with 1:44 left in the quarter.

The four starters who sat were [Napheesa Collier](/player/WNBA/3917450), [Kayla McBride](/player/WNBA/2529205), [Courtney Williams](/player/WNBA/2987891) and [Natasha Howard](/player/WNBA/2529130). They each played between nine and 13 minutes, and none of them played after halftime. Collier scored 9 points in 13 minutes, Howard had six and Williams two, and McBride missed all four of her shots. Rookie guard Olivia Miles was out with a calf injury, and [Maya Caldwell](/player/WNBA/4280850), who also started, played 19 minutes.

Coach Cheryl Reeve explained the decision after the game. "I'm old enough to know that chasing wins is a really bad idea when you play every other day and start in playoffs on Sunday," Reeve said.

## The second half

Without those four, Minnesota won the third quarter 30-14, after a 17-0 run that started with 1:03 left in the second quarter and ran from 57-28 to 57-45 early in the third. The Lynx cut the deficit to seven, 71-64, in the final seconds of the period, and to eight, 74-66, with 8:15 left in the fourth. Indiana answered with an 8-0 run, from 74-66 to 82-66, and outscored the Lynx 25-13 in the fourth.

[Chloe Bibby](/player/WNBA/4280877) led Minnesota with 15 points, making five of nine three-pointers in 26 minutes. [Liatu King](/player/WNBA/4596309) scored 12 and had three steals, and [Aari McDonald](/player/WNBA/4066553) had 10 points and five assists. [Dorka Juhász](/player/WNBA/4398938) had 12 rebounds and six assists in 32 minutes.

## The numbers

Indiana shot 51% (32 of 63) and made 9 of 26 threes, with 27 assists and 11 steals. Minnesota shot 38% (27 of 72), made 11 of 33 threes and had 23 assists and five steals. The Fever made 23 of 28 free throws to Minnesota's 12 of 14 and out-rebounded the Lynx 39-32. [Sophie Cunningham](/player/WNBA/3907781) came off the bench for 11 points in 29 minutes, making three of six threes. [Monique Billings](/player/WNBA/3142255) scored six with five rebounds, three of them offensive, and Indiana outscored the Lynx by 19 in her 15 minutes.

## What is next

Indiana improved to 28-15, one game behind Atlanta (29-14) for the fourth seed and one game ahead of Washington (27-16). Minnesota fell to 32-11.

Atlanta plays at New York on Wednesday, September 23. Indiana plays at Minnesota on Thursday, September 24, at 8 p.m. ET at Target Center, the last regular-season game for both teams.
