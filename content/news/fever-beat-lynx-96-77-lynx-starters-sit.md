The Indiana Fever beat the Minnesota Lynx 96-77 on Tuesday night at Gainbridge Fieldhouse, and four of Minnesota's five starters did not play after halftime. The Lynx clinched the No. 1 seed in the WNBA playoffs later that night, when Portland beat Golden State 90-82 in overtime, [ESPN reported](https://www.espn.com/wnba/story/_/id/50010938/stumbling-lynx-pull-starters-vs-fever-top-seed-line).

Caitlin Clark scored 27 points with nine assists and seven rebounds for Indiana, shooting 9 of 18 from the field and 6 of 6 from the free-throw line. Kelsey Mitchell scored 20 points, and Aliyah Boston had 17 points on 7-of-9 shooting.

Indiana led 31-16 after the first quarter and 57-34 at halftime. The lead reached 29 points, 55-26, with 1:44 left in the second quarter.

Napheesa Collier, Kayla McBride, Courtney Williams and Natasha Howard each played between nine and 13 minutes, and none of them appears in the play-by-play after halftime. Collier scored 9 points in 13 minutes. Rookie guard Olivia Miles was out with a calf injury, [FOX 9 reported](https://www.fox9.com/sports/minnesota-lynx-clinch-no-1-seed-wnba-playoffs-2026), and Maya Caldwell, who also started, played 19 minutes.

Coach Cheryl Reeve explained the decision after the game. "I'm old enough to know that chasing wins is a really bad idea when you play every other day and start in playoffs on Sunday," Reeve said, according to [FOX 9](https://www.fox9.com/sports/minnesota-lynx-clinch-no-1-seed-wnba-playoffs-2026).

Without those four, Minnesota won the third quarter 30-14 and cut the deficit to seven, 71-64, in the final seconds of the period. Indiana outscored the Lynx 25-13 in the fourth. Chloe Bibby led Minnesota with 15 points, Liatu King scored 12, and Aari McDonald had 10.

Indiana improved to 28-15, one game behind Atlanta (29-14) for the fourth seed and one game ahead of Washington (27-16). Minnesota fell to 32-11.

**What is next.** Atlanta plays at New York on Wednesday, September 23. Indiana plays at Minnesota on Thursday, September 24, at 8 p.m. ET at Target Center, the last regular-season game for both teams.
