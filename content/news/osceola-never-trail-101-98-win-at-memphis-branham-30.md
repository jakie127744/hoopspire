The Osceola Magic led the Memphis Hustle by 19 points in the third quarter on Tuesday night, and by one possession at the end. Memphis needed a three to tie it. [Jahmai Mashack](/player/GLeague/4683934) took a 26-footer with the clock at 0:00, missed it, and Osceola won 101-98 at the Landers Center in front of 771 fans.

Osceola never trailed. Memphis tied the game twice in the third quarter, at 72-72 and at 74-74, and never got ahead.

Osceola is 2-0. Memphis is 0-1.

## A 19-point lead

Osceola scored 30 points in the first quarter and 31 in the second, and it led 61-43 at half-time. The lead reached 19 early in the third quarter, 63-44 with 10:53 left.

[Malaki Branham](/player/GLeague/4565201) carried the scoring. He had 30 points on 9-of-18 shooting and made 5 of 6 free throws. [Ricky Council IV](/player/GLeague/4701234) scored 24, made four of his 10 threes, and had 12 rebounds. [JD Davison](/player/GLeague/4576085) added 16 points and six assists, and [Mike Sharavjamts](/player/GLeague/5105875) had 12 off the bench on 4-of-7 shooting.

## Memphis comes back in the third

Memphis scored 31 points in the third quarter to Osceola's 17. [Eric Dixon](/player/GLeague/4431718) made a running pull-up jumper at 5:08 for 67-58. [Kira Lewis Jr.](/player/GLeague/4395698) added a jumper at 3:11, and [Karim López](/player/GLeague/5231919) made a three at 2:45 for 72-66. [Yuri Collins](/player/GLeague/4432957) made a layup at 2:12, 72-70, and Mashack made a free throw at 1:11 to tie it 72-72.

Osceola got two points back from a Davison free throw, 74-72, at 0:55. Mashack tied it again at the line with 0:34 left. [Branham](/player/GLeague/4565201) then made one free throw at 0:16 and another at 0:05, and Osceola went into the fourth quarter ahead 78-74.

## The fourth quarter

The fourth quarter was close the whole way. Osceola led 86-78 with 7:59 left. [Brendan Hausen](/player/GLeague/5105589) made a three for 86-81 and Memphis was within one, 86-85, at 6:08 after a turnaround jumper by Dixon. Council answered with two baskets for 90-85.

[Zach Cleveland](/player/GLeague/5107069) made the Hustle's last three baskets: a driving layup at 2:17 for 97-93, a jumper at 1:05 for 100-96, and a layup at 0:36 for 100-98. Davison had hit a step-back three at 1:53 for 100-93. Council made a free throw at 0:20 for the final score.

Memphis had the ball with a chance to tie. [Ace Baldwin Jr.](/player/GLeague/4433182) threw the ball out of bounds with two seconds left, and the Hustle got it back. Mashack's three was the last shot of the game.

## The numbers behind it

Osceola shot 31 of 70 and 11 of 31 from three. Memphis shot 32 of 84 and 13 of 38. Osceola made 15 of 20 free throws and Memphis made 11 of 16, and Memphis was called for 32 fouls to Osceola's 25.

Both teams turned the ball over a lot, Osceola 25 times and Memphis 21, and each side scored 27 points off the other's turnovers. Collins had eight assists and eight turnovers, with four steals and three blocks, and scored five points on 2-of-11 shooting.

Dixon led Memphis with 20 points and four threes. López had 16 points and nine rebounds, Hausen had 15 on four threes, and Cleveland had 13 points, 10 rebounds and four assists.
