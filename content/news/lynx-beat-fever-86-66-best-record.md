The Minnesota Lynx beat the Indiana Fever 86-66 on Thursday night at Target Center in the last regular-season game for both teams. Minnesota finished 33-11, the best record in the league. Indiana finished 28-16.

Minnesota led 32-13 after the first quarter and 62-32 at halftime. An 18-0 run in the first quarter turned a 7-7 game into 25-7. The lead reached 36 points, 68-32, with 8:06 left in the third quarter. Indiana outscored the Lynx 25-10 in the fourth.

Kayla McBride scored 22 points for Minnesota, making 6 of 10 three-point attempts. Natasha Howard had 21 points on 9-of-11 shooting and eight rebounds. Courtney Williams had 10 assists.

Caitlin Clark scored two points in 18 minutes, making 1 of 12 shots from the field and 0 of 7 from three-point range. She had three assists and four rebounds. Kelsey Mitchell led Indiana's starters with 10 points in 22 minutes. No Indiana starter played more than 22 minutes, and Monique Billings had 10 rebounds off the bench. Grace VanSlooten scored nine points, all in the fourth quarter.

The result reversed Tuesday's game in Indianapolis, when the Fever beat the Lynx 96-77 and four Minnesota starters did not play after halftime. On Thursday all five Minnesota starters played at least 20 minutes. Rookie guard Olivia Miles did not play, listed in the box score with a strained left calf.

Indiana finished level with Washington at 28-16. Atlanta finished 30-14.

**What is next.** The playoffs open on Sunday, September 27. Indiana plays at the Las Vegas Aces in Game 1 of the first round at 4 p.m. ET. Minnesota hosts the New York Liberty at 2 p.m. ET. Washington plays at Atlanta, and Dallas plays at Golden State.
