The Minnesota Lynx beat the Indiana Fever 86-66 on Thursday night at Target Center in the last regular-season game for both teams. Minnesota finished 33-11, the best record in the league. Indiana finished 28-16. The Fever never led and never tied: Minnesota scored the first basket and was ahead the rest of the night. The crowd was 15,221.

## How the game went

Minnesota led 32-13 after the first quarter and 62-32 at halftime. An 18-0 run in the first quarter, from 7-4 to 25-4, turned a three-point game into a 21-point one. [Napheesa Collier](/player/WNBA/3917450) hit two threes in it, [Kayla McBride](/player/WNBA/2529205) scored five, and the Fever did not score for more than four minutes. Later the Lynx scored 13 straight, late in the second quarter and early in the third, from 55-32 to 68-32, and the lead reached 36 points with 8:06 left in the third quarter.

Indiana fought back late. Trailing 76-40, the Fever scored 11 straight points, starting with 43.8 seconds left in the third quarter and ending at 76-51 early in the fourth, and they outscored the Lynx 25-10 in the period. They got no closer than 20, at 84-64 with 43.9 seconds to play.

## The numbers

Minnesota shot 44% (33 of 75) and made 15 of 40 three-pointers (38%), with 28 assists. Indiana shot 34% (25 of 73) and made 5 of 32 threes (16%). The Fever had the rebounding edge on the offensive glass, 16 to 10, and made 11 of 20 free throws to the Lynx's 5 of 6, but none of it was enough.

McBride scored 22 points for Minnesota in 24 minutes, making 6 of 10 three-point attempts. [Natasha Howard](/player/WNBA/2529130) had 21 points on 9-of-11 shooting, with eight rebounds, three blocks and four offensive rebounds in 20 minutes. [Courtney Williams](/player/WNBA/2987891) had 10 assists, nine points and seven rebounds.

[Caitlin Clark](/player/WNBA/4433403) scored two points in 18 minutes, making 1 of 12 shots from the field and 0 of 7 from three-point range. She had three assists and four rebounds, and Indiana was outscored by 27 points while she was on the floor. [Kelsey Mitchell](/player/WNBA/3142191) led Indiana's starters with 10 points in 22 minutes on 3-of-12 shooting. The five starters shot 9 of 37 between them and scored 23 points, and no Indiana starter played more than 22 minutes. [Monique Billings](/player/WNBA/3142255) had 10 rebounds off the bench, six of them offensive. [Grace VanSlooten](/player/WNBA/5105731) scored nine points, all in the fourth quarter.

The result reversed Tuesday's game in Indianapolis, when the Fever beat the Lynx 96-77 and four Minnesota starters did not play after halftime. On Thursday all five Minnesota starters played at least 20 minutes. Rookie guard Olivia Miles did not play, listed in the box score with a strained left calf.

## What is next

Indiana finished level with Washington at 28-16. Atlanta finished 30-14. The playoffs open on Sunday, September 27. Indiana plays at the Las Vegas Aces in Game 1 of the first round at 4 p.m. ET. Minnesota hosts the New York Liberty at 2 p.m. ET. Washington plays at Atlanta, and Dallas plays at Golden State.
