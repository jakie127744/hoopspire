Two teams are unbeaten after three rounds of the CBA season. The Beijing Ducks beat Shenzhen 120-62 at home on Thursday, and on Friday the Liaoning Flying Leopards beat Sichuan 104-93 behind 40 points and 17 assists from Sharife Cooper. Both are 3-0.

Round 3 was played over the two days, five games each night. The champions, Shanghai, lost 103-92 at Shanxi on Thursday and are 2-1.

## Beijing Ducks 120, Shenzhen 62

Beijing led 32-18 after the first quarter and 62-29 at halftime. They won every quarter, and the margin was 58 points.

Lindy Waters III scored 21 in 16 minutes and made seven of eight threes. Zeng Fanbo scored 19, Barry Brown Jr. 15 and JaKarr Sampson 13. Beijing shot 45 of 82 and had 27 assists. All 12 Beijing players who played scored at least two points.

Shenzhen made 18 of 59 shots and turned the ball over 18 times. Terquavion Smith scored 19 for them, and Ibou Badji blocked six shots.

## Shanxi 103, Shanghai 92

Shanghai led 32-24 after the first quarter. Shanxi won the second 30-14 to lead 54-46 at halftime, and the third 26-17. Shanghai won the fourth 29-23.

Lindell Wigginton scored 26 with eight assists for Shanxi, and Hamidou Diallo had 21 points and 10 rebounds. For Shanghai, Brandon Goodwin scored 20 with seven assists, Zhang Zhenlin 19, and D'Angelo Russell 18 with 10 rebounds.

## Liaoning 104, Sichuan 93

Cooper played 34 minutes and finished with 40 points, five rebounds and 17 assists. Kyler Edwards scored 17 and Marquese Chriss 11. Liaoning led 59-52 at halftime and won each of the last two quarters.

## The rest of Round 3

On Thursday, Guangdong beat the Zhejiang Lions 96-78 for their first win. Guangdong won the third quarter 31-17. Kyle Fogg and Cui Yongxi scored 16 each, and Fogg had nine assists. Rayjon Tucker scored 34 for the Lions.

Fujian beat Ningbo 95-76 and won the fourth quarter 29-17. Daryl Macon scored 20. Bennie Boatwright, [who took his oath as a Filipino citizen on Wednesday](/story/bennie-boatwright-takes-oath-gilas-pilipinas-naturalization), scored six in 15 minutes. Rolands Šmits had 22 points and nine rebounds for Ningbo.

Jilin beat Xinjiang 89-83 after leading 78-59 going into the fourth quarter. Xinjiang won the fourth 24-11. DeVaughn Akoon-Purcell scored 19 with eight assists for Jilin, and Zavier Simpson had 15 points and 15 assists for Xinjiang.

On Friday, the Beijing Royal Fighters beat Jiangsu 93-82. Jiangsu won the third quarter 32-18 to get within one, and Beijing won the fourth 24-14. Liao Sanning scored 17. Shandong won 106-97 at Nanjing, with 27 from Wang Lanqin and 24 for Nanjing from Guo Haowen. Tianjin beat Guangzhou 102-86 and won the fourth quarter 32-21. He Siyu scored 20 for Tianjin, and Chen Guohao 16 for Guangzhou.

The Zhejiang Golden Bulls beat Qingdao 88-80. Qingdao led 68-61 after three quarters, and the Golden Bulls won the fourth 27-12. Gabe York scored 28 for Qingdao, and Wang Yibo had 15 points and 10 assists.

## The records

3-0: Beijing Ducks, Liaoning. 2-1: Shanghai, Shanxi, Fujian, Zhejiang Lions, Xinjiang, Sichuan, Beijing Royal Fighters, Guangzhou, Zhejiang Golden Bulls. 1-2: Guangdong, Shenzhen, Jilin, Nanjing, Shandong, Tianjin. 0-3: Ningbo, Jiangsu, Qingdao.
