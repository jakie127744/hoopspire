Shavon Shields made a three with eight seconds left in overtime to tie it 109-109, and Paris called a timeout. [Nadir Hifi](/player/EuroLeague/rg-171599) then made a two-pointer with the clock at 0:00, and Paris Basketball beat Fenerbahce Tarfin Istanbul 111-109 on Wednesday at the Accor Arena, in front of 11,774 fans. The official play-by-play ends with that basket, the end of the period and the end of the game.

Paris is 5-6 and 12th in the EuroLeague table. Fenerbahce is 7-4 and seventh.

## Paris builds a lead, Fenerbahce catches up

Paris led 23-14 after the first quarter and by 15, 50-35, with 1:51 left in the second. It went to half-time ahead 50-43. Fenerbahce scored 29 in the third quarter, to 22 for Paris, and the score was 72-72 going into the fourth.

The fourth quarter was level at 20-20, but it did not feel that way at the end. Paris led 92-84 after two free throws from [Allan Dokossi](/player/EuroLeague/rg-127348) with 2:10 left. Fenerbahce scored the last eight points of regulation. Shields made a three at 1:59, Talen Horton-Tucker made a two-pointer at 0:53, Marcus Bingham made a free throw at 0:18, and Wade Baldwin IV tied it at 92-92 with a basket at 0:08 after a steal.

[Frank Ntilikina](/player/EuroLeague/rg-71575) turned the ball over at 0:14, which gave Baldwin the steal. In the last seven seconds of regulation Paris took three shots, a three by Hifi, then a two by [Sebastian Herrera](/player/EuroLeague/rg-51983) and a two by Hifi, each after an offensive rebound, and missed all three.

Paris answered with Herrera's three at 2:04, 100-102. Baldwin made one of two free throws, 100-103, and Herrera made another three at 1:35 to tie it 103-103.

Horton-Tucker made one of two free throws at 1:04 for 103-104. Ntilikina then made a three at 0:51, 106-104, and Shields made two free throws at 0:50, 106-106. Ntilikina made another three at 0:08, 109-106, and Shields answered at once with his three, 109-109, with an assist from Baldwin. Paris took a timeout with six seconds left, and Hifi's two-pointer is logged at 0:00, 111-109. From 97-102 at 2:17, Paris outscored Fenerbahce 14-7.

## The numbers behind it

Horton-Tucker scored 32 points, with 10 rebounds and 12 of 14 on two-point shots, but he had seven turnovers. Baldwin scored 22 and Bingham 14. Fenerbahce had 43 rebounds to 34, with 17 on the offensive glass, but it turned the ball over 20 times to Paris's 14.

Ntilikina led Paris with 22 points on 4-of-8 three-point shooting and 6 of 6 free throws. Warren scored 19 and [Jared Rhoden](/player/EuroLeague/rg-132192) 13. Herrera had 12 points and six assists, and Hifi had 11 points on 4 of 18 shooting, but made the last basket.

Paris made 14 of 37 threes, 42 of its 111 points, and Fenerbahce made 9 of 26. At the free-throw line Paris was 19 of 25 and Fenerbahce 18 of 23. Paris had 12 steals and Fenerbahce 11.
