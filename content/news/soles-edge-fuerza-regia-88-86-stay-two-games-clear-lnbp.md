The top of the LNBP table met at Auditorio PSF in Mexicali on Tuesday night, and the leaders held on. Soles beat Fuerza Regia 88-86, the closest of the night's seven games but one, and moved to 21-6. Astros also won, so the lead over second place is still two games. Fuerza Regia had started the night level with Astros at 18-8 and fell to 18-9, a game behind them.

Every team in the league played on Tuesday. All 14 clubs have now played 27 games.

Soles have the league's best points difference, plus 309. Astros are next at plus 212. Behind them the order on the table and the order by points difference do not match: Fuerza Regia are plus 119, Lobos plus 106 and Panteras plus 96, so Lobos have the better difference while sitting a game behind Panteras.

## Panteras by one

The tightest game of the night was in Aguascalientes. Panteras beat Diablos Rojos 77-76 at Auditorio Hermanos Carreón and moved to 18-9, level with Fuerza Regia. The two clubs share third place, one game behind Astros.

That win also moved Panteras clear of Lobos. The two had started the night tied at 17-9. Lobos lost 77-68 at El Calor in Cancún and dropped to fifth at 17-10. It was El Calor's ninth win.

## Astros keep pace

Astros beat Gambusinos 96-89 at Arena Astros to go to 19-8 and stay two games behind Soles. Gambusinos fell to 11-16.

## Three wins by 20 or more

The other three games were not close.

- **Abejas 98, Correcaminos 76** in Ciudad Victoria. Abejas won by 22 and moved to 11-16. Correcaminos are 4-23, five games behind the next team.
- **Mineros 96, Santos 75** at Gimnasio Marcelino González in Zacatecas. Mineros won by 21 to reach 15-12, seventh. Santos fell to 10-17.
- **Dorados 105, Freseros 85** at Inforum Irapuato. Dorados won by 20 on the road and are sixth at 16-11. Freseros are 9-18.

## A crowd in eighth place

Abejas' win, with losses for Gambusinos and Diablos Rojos, leaves three clubs at 11-16. The league ranks them Gambusinos, Diablos Rojos, Abejas. Santos are one game back at 10-17. Freseros and El Calor are two back at 9-18.

The LNBP does not publish box scores, so there are no individual scoring lines for these games.

## What is next

The same seven pairs meet again on Wednesday, October 7, in the same arenas. Soles host Fuerza Regia at 8 p.m. Mexico City time, Panteras host Diablos Rojos and Astros host Gambusinos at the same hour. Mineros host Santos and Freseros host Dorados at 8 p.m. as well.
