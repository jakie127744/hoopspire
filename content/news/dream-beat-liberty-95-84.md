The Atlanta Dream beat the New York Liberty 95-84 on September 21 at Barclays Center, improving to 29-14 and opening a game and a half cushion over Indiana (27-15) for the fourth seed in the WNBA playoffs with one regular-season game left for each team. The crowd was 16,458.

[Jordin Canada](/player/WNBA/3142250) led Atlanta with 19 points and nine assists, making all eight of her free throws. [Angel Reese](/player/WNBA/4433402) scored 18 and had 10 rebounds. [Breanna Stewart](/player/WNBA/2998928) led New York with 27 points and 14 rebounds, and [Leonie Fiebich](/player/WNBA/4683006) had six assists.

## How the game went

It was close for more than half of it. New York led 24-23 after the first quarter, the score was tied five times in the first half, and the Liberty's biggest lead was six, 31-25 with 6:59 left in the second quarter. Atlanta scored 18 to New York's 14 in the second and took the lead for good with a basket at 3.1 seconds, 40-38, before the break. It led 41-38 at halftime.

Atlanta's 8-0 run began with 1:11 left in the second quarter, with the Dream trailing 38-35, and ended at 43-38 early in the third. New York answered with a run of its own, eight straight points, from 55-45 down to 55-53 by the 4:21 mark, and the Liberty were within two. Atlanta pulled away after that, outscoring New York 32-24 in the third quarter to lead 73-62 entering the fourth. The lead reached 13, 75-62, with 9:32 left. New York got no closer than eight in the fourth, at 79-71 with 6:38 to play. Both teams scored 22 in the fourth, so the 11-point margin held to the final buzzer.

Atlanta scored its last seven points, all of them free throws, in the final 1:55, including two by Canada with 50 seconds left that made it 95-84.

## The numbers

Atlanta won with cleaner basketball. The Dream turned the ball over four times, and New York turned it over 11. Atlanta shot 48% (32 of 67) to New York's 42% (30 of 71), and made 23 of 30 free throws to the Liberty's 17 of 23. The Liberty out-rebounded the Dream 40-32 and had 23 assists to Atlanta's 17, but they made only 7 of 29 threes (24%) to the Dream's 8 of 28 (29%). Both teams scored 44 points in the paint.

[DeWanna Bonner](/player/WNBA/869) came off the bench for 17 points on 5-of-6 shooting, with six rebounds, in 29 minutes. [Rhyne Howard](/player/WNBA/4398674) scored 16 and played 38 minutes, and [Allisha Gray](/player/WNBA/3058901) scored 14 on 5-of-8 shooting.

For New York, Stewart made 11 of 19 shots in 37 minutes and had four turnovers. [Jonquel Jones](/player/WNBA/2999101) scored 18 with 12 rebounds. [Sabrina Ionescu](/player/WNBA/4066533) made all seven of her free throws but shot 3 of 12 from the field. [Pauline Astier](/player/WNBA/5345320) scored 15 on 6-of-9 shooting. Fiebich scored one point but handed out six assists.

## What is next

New York falls to 26-17, tied with Washington (26-16) and Dallas (26-17) in the crowded group fighting for the sixth, seventh and eighth seeds. The loss leaves the Liberty needing help to climb out of that group with one game to play.

Atlanta and New York play again on Wednesday, September 23, at Barclays Center, the final game of the regular season for both teams. Indiana closes its schedule with two games against Minnesota, already locked into the No. 1 seed race with Golden State. The WNBA playoffs' best-of-three first round is set to begin September 27.
