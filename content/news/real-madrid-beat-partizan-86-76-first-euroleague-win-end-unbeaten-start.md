Real Madrid came into Round 4 as one of two EuroLeague teams without a win, three games into the season and all three on the road. In their first home game, at the Movistar Arena on Thursday night, they beat Partizan Belgrade 86-76 and handed the Serbian side their first defeat.

Madrid are now 1-3. Partizan, who had won in Belgrade, Paris and Munich, are 3-1.

## The run that built the lead

Partizan led 16-15 with 3:20 left in the first quarter. Madrid went ahead 17-16 seven seconds later and never trailed again.

The game turned on a 14-0 run across the end of the first quarter and the start of the second. From 21-19, Madrid scored 14 straight points to lead 35-19 with 8:46 left in the half. They won the first quarter 27-19 and led 53-37 at half-time.

Partizan answered with nine straight points either side of the break, from 53-35 to 53-44 early in the third quarter. They won the third quarter 20-18, and Madrid still led 71-57 going into the fourth.

## Twenty up, then ten

[Théo Maledon](/player/EuroLeague/rg-105847) scored a basket and a free throw to make it 76-61 with 8:28 left. [Facundo Campazzo](/player/EuroLeague/rg-40886) hit a three, and [Andrés Feliz](/player/EuroLeague/rg-72668) made it 83-63 with 6:21 left, Madrid's biggest lead.

Partizan scored the next 10 points. [Tonye Jekiri](/player/EuroLeague/rg-31237) scored five of them and [Arijan Lakić](/player/EuroLeague/rg-116903) four, and an Ethan Thompson free throw made it 83-73 with 3:35 left. Maledon ended the run with another basket and free throw nine seconds later, and Partizan got no closer than 10.

## Where it was won

Partizan made 25 of 43 two-point shots, more than Madrid's 21 of 46, but only three of their 20 three-pointers. Madrid made nine of 27 threes.

At the free throw line Madrid were perfect, 17 of 17. Partizan made 17 of 27. Maledon was 7 of 7 and scored 13 points, the most for Madrid, with four assists. Campazzo added 12 off the bench, four of them from the line. [Edy Tavares](/player/EuroLeague/rg-39662) had eight points and eight rebounds. Madrid also took 13 offensive rebounds to Partizan's 10.

Coach Pedro Martínez used 11 players, and every one of them scored. Nobody played more than 22 minutes.

[Carlik Jones](/player/EuroLeague/rg-107107) led Partizan with 19 points on 7-of-10 two-point shooting, with five assists. Thompson added 14 points and eight rebounds, and Jekiri 11. The attendance was 8,579.

## The rest of Round 4 so far

- **Wednesday:** ASVEL won 98-96 at Paris.
- **Thursday:** Crvena Zvezda won 77-58 at Dubai. Maccabi Tel Aviv beat Milan 97-79 at the Aleksandar Nikolić Hall in Belgrade for their first win. Virtus Bologna won 94-87 at Bayern Munich. Panathinaikos beat Fenerbahce 92-74 in Athens. Valencia beat Hapoel Tel Aviv 93-82.

Panathinaikos and Valencia are 4-0, the only unbeaten teams left. With Madrid and Maccabi both winning, no team is still without a win.
