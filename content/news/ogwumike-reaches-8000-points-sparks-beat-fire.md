[Nneka Ogwumike](/player/WNBA/1068) of the Los Angeles Sparks became the fourth player in WNBA history to score 8,000 career points on Sunday night, in a 105-84 win over the Portland Fire at Crypto.com Arena. She needed seven points to get there. She scored 20.

Ogwumike finished with 20 points on 9-of-19 shooting, 12 rebounds, five of them offensive, and five assists in 27 minutes. The Sparks outscored Portland by 20 points while she was on the floor. Her career total is 8,013, behind Diana Taurasi (10,646 points), Tina Charles (8,396) and DeWanna Bonner (8,217). The crowd was 17,703.

Ogwumike will retire at the end of the 2026 season. On August 19 WNBA commissioner Cathy Engelbert marked her 15 seasons in the league. Ogwumike was the league's MVP in 2016.

## How the game went

Los Angeles took the lead for good late in the first quarter, at 21-19 with 2:03 left, and led 28-26 when it ended. The game was settled in the second quarter, which the Sparks won 32-15. They scored 12 straight points, from 33-31 to 45-31, and then 10 more in a row, from 52-41 to 62-41 early in the third quarter. They led by 19 at halftime, 60-41, and by 20 after three quarters, 85-65. Portland got no closer than 18 in the fourth.

The Sparks won it from the three-point line. They made 14 of 29 threes (48%), and the Fire made 6 of 27 (22%), a difference of 24 points on those shots alone. Los Angeles shot 33 of 62 from the field (53%) to Portland's 28 of 64 (44%). The Fire scored 42 points in the paint to the Sparks' 28 and had 10 steals to Los Angeles' six, but it turned the ball over 15 times, and it made 22 of 31 free throws to Los Angeles' 25 of 29. The Sparks had 24 assists to Portland's 17, and their reserves outscored the Fire's bench 32-25.
