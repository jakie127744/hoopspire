Nneka Ogwumike of the Los Angeles Sparks became the fourth player in WNBA history to score 8,000 career points on Sunday night, in a 105-84 win over the Portland Fire at Crypto.com Arena, according to the Associated Press. Field Level Media also reported the milestone.

Ogwumike finished with 20 points, 12 rebounds and five assists, and the AP put her career total at 8,013. She joined Diana Taurasi (10,646 points), Tina Charles (8,396) and DeWanna Bonner (8,217) in the group, the AP said.

Ogwumike has announced that she will retire at the end of the 2026 season. In a statement on August 19, WNBA commissioner Cathy Engelbert said she will retire at the end of this season and described her career as 15 seasons. Ogwumike was the league's MVP in 2016, the AP said.

Los Angeles led from late in the first quarter and outscored Portland 32-15 in the second to lead 60-41 at halftime, the AP reported. Rae Burrell also scored 20 for the Sparks, Ariel Atkins had 14 and Chance Gray had 11. The Sparks shot 33 of 62 from the field. For the Fire, Carla Leite scored 23 points and Charisma Osborne had a career-high 13.

The Sparks are 16-26 and the Fire are 16-27. Lisa Leslie was honored with the unveiling of her statue outside Crypto.com Arena and a halftime ceremony, the AP said.

**What is next.** The Sparks play at the Las Vegas Aces on Tuesday, September 22. The Fire finish their regular season at home against the Golden State Valkyries on Tuesday.
