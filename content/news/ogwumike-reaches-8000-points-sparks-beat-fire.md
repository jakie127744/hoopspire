Nneka Ogwumike of the Los Angeles Sparks became the fourth player in WNBA history to score 8,000 career points on Sunday night, in a 105-84 win over the Portland Fire at Crypto.com Arena, according to [the Associated Press](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/09/20/ogwumike-becomes-4th-player-in-wnba-history-with-8000-points-as-the-sparks-beat-the-fire-105-84/).

Ogwumike finished with 20 points, 12 rebounds and five assists. The AP put her career total at 8,013, behind Diana Taurasi (10,646 points), Tina Charles (8,396) and DeWanna Bonner (8,217).

Ogwumike will retire at the end of the 2026 season. In a [statement on August 19](https://pr.nba.com/cathy-engelbert-statement-nneka-ogwumike/), WNBA commissioner Cathy Engelbert marked her 15 seasons in the league. Ogwumike was the league's MVP in 2016.

Los Angeles led from late in the first quarter and outscored Portland 32-15 in the second to lead 60-41 at halftime. Rae Burrell also scored 20 for the Sparks, Ariel Atkins had 14 and Chance Gray had 11. The Sparks shot 33 of 62 from the field. For the Fire, Carla Leite scored 23 points and Charisma Osborne had a career-high 13.

The Sparks are 16-26 and the Fire are 16-27.

**What is next.** The Sparks play at the Las Vegas Aces on Tuesday, September 22. The Fire finish their regular season at home against the Golden State Valkyries on Tuesday.
