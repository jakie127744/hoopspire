[De'Anthony Melton](/player/NBA/4066436) hit a 23-foot three with 3:47 left, and the Golden State Warriors led the Memphis Grizzlies 106-94. They did not score again until 0.5 seconds were left. Memphis scored the next 17 points and won 111-108 on Friday at Chase Center.

Friday had 12 games. [Jayson Tatum's 36 in Boston's win over the Knicks](/story/tatum-36-celtics-hold-off-knicks-107-103) has its own story. Here are the other 11. Eight of Friday's winners are 2-0: Memphis, Houston, the Lakers, Portland, Toronto, Detroit, Minnesota and Atlanta.

## Grizzlies 111, Warriors 108

Jaylen Wells started the run with a three and a free throw. [Cameron Boozer](/player/NBA/5041935) made four free throws, two of them after [Zach Edey](/player/NBA/4600663) stole the ball from Brandin Podziemski. [Ty Jerome](/player/NBA/4065733) hit a 23-foot three from Edey at 1:24, and it was 106-105.

Edey blocked a 28-foot three by [Stephen Curry](/player/NBA/3975). Wells tied it at the line at 54.7, and Curry and Podziemski missed threes. Boozer hit a nine-foot turnaround with 20.1 seconds left for Memphis's first lead since the third quarter. Draymond Green was then called for an offensive foul, and a technical foul followed. Jerome made the technical free throw and a driving layup at 10.2: 111-106.

Jerome scored 28, 14 in the third quarter, and Boozer 25. Curry led Golden State with 24.

## Jazz 120, Pelicans 119

[Dejounte Murray](/player/NBA/3907497) scored with 1:01 left to put New Orleans ahead 119-118. At 50.5, Trey Murphy III fouled [Darryn Peterson](/player/NBA/5041955), who made both free throws. [Zion Williamson](/player/NBA/4395628) was called for an offensive foul at 35.9, and a Pelicans challenge did not change it. [Jaren Jackson Jr.](/player/NBA/4277961) missed at 14.3, and Murray missed a 19-foot pull-up with 1.4 seconds left.

Jackson scored 24 for Utah, nine in the fourth quarter. Williamson had 23 points and [Saddiq Bey](/player/NBA/4397136) 20. The Pelicans had trailed 67-52 early in the third quarter and taken the lead with a 12-0 run. Utah is 1-1, New Orleans 0-2.

## Rockets 114, Spurs 110

The lead changed hands 14 times at Moody Center in Austin, the last on a basket by [Alperen Şengün](/player/NBA/4871144) with 3:49 left: 105-104. Şengün then scored, was fouled and made the free throw at 2:42.

San Antonio got within 109-108 on an alley-oop from [De'Aaron Fox](/player/NBA/4066259) to [Victor Wembanyama](/player/NBA/5104157). [Kevin Durant](/player/NBA/3202) answered with a 25-foot three from Şengün at 1:34. [Dylan Harper](/player/NBA/5037871) dunked at 46.1 to make it 112-110, but the Spurs did not score again. Fred VanVleet made two free throws with 0.6 seconds left, and Amen Thompson blocked Wembanyama's layup at 0.1.

Şengün scored 29, 14 in the fourth quarter. Wembanyama had 25 for San Antonio and Harper 20, 11 in the fourth. Houston is 2-0, San Antonio 0-2.

The full game story: [Amen Thompson blocks Wembanyama, Rockets beat Spurs 114-110](/story/amen-thompson-blocks-wembanyama-rockets-beat-spurs-114-110).

## Bulls 132, Bucks 125

Milwaukee led 26-11 in the first quarter. Chicago scored 44 points in the second, with a 16-0 run, and the game was tied six times.

The Bucks went ahead 123-122 on a basket by Myles Turner with 2:51 left. Then the Bulls got to the line. [Caleb Wilson](/player/NBA/5095151) made two at 1:16 and two more at 35.5, [Norman Powell](/player/NBA/2595516) made two, and Tre Jones made four. Chicago made all 10 of its free throws in the last 1:16 and won the afternoon game at Fiserv Forum.

Powell scored 25, Wilson 21 and Leonard Miller 19. [Tyler Herro](/player/NBA/4395725) scored 34 for Milwaukee, 13 in the fourth quarter.

## Lakers 125, Clippers 117

The Lakers took the lead for good early in the third quarter and scored 12 straight to make it 89-76. [Austin Reaves](/player/NBA/4066457) scored 13 in the quarter and finished with 28. [Luka Dončić](/player/NBA/3945274) had 23 and [Walker Kessler](/player/NBA/4433136) 22.

The Clippers cut a 15-point lead to 119-113 with 3:21 left but got no closer. [Darius Garland](/player/NBA/4396907) scored 26 for the Clippers, 13 in the fourth quarter, and Brook Lopez 22. The Lakers are 2-0.

## Trail Blazers 128, Kings 120

Sacramento led 21-9 in the first quarter and 64-59 early in the third. Portland then scored 15 straight and led the rest of the way. [Deni Avdija](/player/NBA/4683021) scored 26, 11 in the third quarter, and [Damian Lillard](/player/NBA/6606) and Toumani Camara 19 each. The Kings got within 118-115 with 4:51 left.

[Darius Acuff Jr.](/player/NBA/5142620) scored 33 for Sacramento, 22 of them after halftime, and Domantas Sabonis 27. Portland is 2-0, Sacramento 0-2.

## Raptors 112, Wizards 102

Toronto led from its first basket to the end. Washington got within four early in the fourth quarter, at 83-79. [Scottie Barnes](/player/NBA/4433134) scored 28, 11 in the fourth, and [RJ Barrett](/player/NBA/4395625) 23. [Trae Young](/player/NBA/4277905) scored 22 for the Wizards. Toronto is 2-0.

## Pistons 128, Heat 111

Detroit scored the first 11 points, scored 41 in the first quarter and never trailed. John Collins scored 23 and Paul Reed 19. [Giannis Antetokounmpo](/player/NBA/3032977) scored 36 for Miami, 16 of them in the third quarter. Detroit is 2-0, Miami 0-2.

## Timberwolves 115, Magic 93

Orlando never led. Minnesota led by 26 late in the third quarter, and nine Timberwolves scored at least nine points, led by Jaden McDaniels and [Anthony Edwards](/player/NBA/4594268) with 15 each. Wendell Carter Jr. scored 20 for Orlando, 15 in the fourth quarter. Minnesota is 2-0, Orlando 0-2.

## Mavericks 121, Pacers 105

Dallas scored 44 points in the first quarter and led by 37, at 77-40, late in the first half. Indiana never led. [Kyrie Irving](/player/NBA/6442) scored 24 and P.J. Washington 20. [Pascal Siakam](/player/NBA/3149673) scored 23 for the Pacers, and [Tyrese Haliburton](/player/NBA/4396993) 18. Both teams are 1-1.

## Hawks 128, Hornets 81

Charlotte led 2-0. Atlanta led for good from 8:27 of the first quarter, scored 18 straight across halftime to go from 56-43 to 74-43, and led by 49, at 128-79, with 58.2 seconds left. [Jalen Johnson](/player/NBA/4701230) and Aaron Wiggins scored 19 each. Naz Reid scored 17 for the Hornets. Atlanta is 2-0, Charlotte 1-1.
