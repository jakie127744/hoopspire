Down 12 late in the third quarter at John Cain Arena, the Cairns Taipans scored the first 11 points of the fourth, won the quarter 35-14 and beat Melbourne United 107-95 on Saturday night. [Cole Anthony](/player/NBL/5443472) scored 40 for Melbourne. It was not close to enough.

The win was the Taipans' first on the road this season. They had lost at the Sydney Kings, the Adelaide 36ers and the New Zealand Breakers by a combined 58 points. Cairns is now 2-3. Melbourne, beaten by Tasmania on Thursday, falls to 1-3.

## Anthony's third quarter

For half the night this was a tight game. Cairns led 18-14 after one quarter and 47-46 at the half, with the score tied five times in the first half. Then came Anthony. He scored 16 of his 40 in the third quarter, Melbourne put up 35 in it, and when Kyle Bowen scored from three feet with 2:31 left the home side led 79-67, its biggest lead of the night.

[Malith Machar](/player/NBL/5090900) helped build it. He scored 14 points in 14 minutes and made three of his four threes.

Melbourne still led 81-72 after three.

## The turn

[Keanu Pinder](/player/NBL/5402797) scored on a layup from [Jaylon Brown](/player/NBL/5447257) at 9:25. [Reyne Smith](/player/NBL/4896994) buried a three from 31 feet, set up by Pinder, at 8:57. [Jack McVeigh](/player/NBL/3911893) hit a turnaround jumper and the free throw that came with it at 8:26, and it was 81-80. By 7:44 Cairns led 83-81, and Melbourne had not scored in the quarter.

Cairns coach Adam Forde said his team "started the fourth quarter with six defensive stops in a row."

Anthony tied it at 83 with a 20-foot pull-up at 7:03. United never led again.

"We lost the game in five minutes," Melbourne coach Jacob Chance said.

## Brown's ten minutes

Brown had six points on one-of-nine shooting through three quarters. He scored 14 in the fourth, with two threes. A 27-foot step-back three at 5:20 made it 92-87. Two free throws at 3:28 made it 95-89, and an 18-foot step-back at 2:16 made it 99-91. He finished with 20 points, nine of 10 at the line, four rebounds and four assists.

Pinder closed it out. He scored on a turnaround at 3:05, hit two free throws at 0:39 and laid in the last basket with 17 seconds left for the final 12-point margin. He led Cairns with 27 points on 10-of-14 shooting.

"This is not a team that will operate through fear and then we find ourselves down 12, the emotions are heightened and this is where you respond," Forde said.

## The numbers

Four Taipans scored 20 or more: Pinder 27, Smith 21, McVeigh 20 and Brown 20. Smith made five of 10 threes. Cairns made 15 of 39 from three to Melbourne's five of 24, and had 12 steals to four. Melbourne won the boards 42-32, with 10 offensive rebounds to Cairns' four. Both teams made 26 of 30 free throws.

Anthony scored his 40 on 11-of-26 shooting and 16 of 17 at the line. [Josh Oduro](/player/NBL/5402939) had 11 points and 11 rebounds, and [Luke Travers](/player/NBL/5402940) 10 points and eight rebounds. [Joe Ingles](/player/NBL/5402938) scored eight in 25 minutes.

[Jonah Bolden](/player/NBL/3155533) had nine points and 10 rebounds off the Cairns bench, and the Taipans were plus-13 with him on the floor.
