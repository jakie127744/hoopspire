Eleven games opened the season for 22 teams on Wednesday night. We wrote up three of them on their own: [the Hawks' 132-125 win at Orlando](/story/hawks-beat-magic-132-125-22-lead-changes), [the Pacers' 123-120 escape in New Orleans](/story/pacers-hold-off-pelicans-123-120-siakam-30) and [Anthony Edwards' 45 in Minnesota's win at Miami](/story/edwards-45-timberwolves-beat-heat-131-106). Here are the other eight.

## Grizzlies 123, Jazz 103

[Ty Jerome](/player/NBA/4065733) scored 45 points on 17-of-25 shooting at FedExForum, with five threes, seven rebounds and six assists. Utah led 31-22 late in the first quarter. Memphis scored the next 13 points across the quarter break, led 64-51 at halftime and by 27 in the third. Keyonte George scored 22 for the Jazz and Jaren Jackson Jr. 15.

## Lakers 127, Warriors 105

Walker Kessler made all 12 of his shots and scored 26 with nine rebounds. Luka Dončić had 28 points, eight rebounds and seven assists with five threes, and Austin Reaves scored 23. Golden State led 13-10 early, the first quarter ended 34-33, and the Lakers led 66-52 at the break. They won the third quarter 36-16 and led 102-68 going into the fourth. Stephen Curry scored 23.

## Rockets 128, Mavericks 110

Kevin Durant and Jabari Smith Jr. scored 30 each, and Alperen Şengün had 23 points and 10 rebounds. Houston won the second quarter 38-12, led 70-38 at halftime and by 37 in the third. Cooper Flagg scored 32 for Dallas on 10-of-17 shooting.

## Wizards 128, Bucks 116

Milwaukee led 93-90 after three quarters in Washington. The Wizards scored 14 straight early in the fourth, from 97-98 to 111-98, and won the quarter 38-23. Anthony Davis had 34 points and 12 rebounds. Trae Young scored 20 with 13 assists, though he missed all six of his threes. Tyler Herro scored 24 with nine assists for Milwaukee, and Jaime Jaquez Jr. 22.

## Hornets 110, Nets 103

The game was tied four times in less than two minutes of the third quarter in Brooklyn, the last at 72. Charlotte then scored eight straight and led 86-81 after three. Grayson Allen scored 21 with five threes, Naz Reid had 20 points, 11 rebounds and seven assists, and Dennis Schröder scored 19. Julius Randle scored 33 for Brooklyn and Michael Porter Jr. 26.

## Raptors 135, Bulls 92

Toronto led 43-23 after one quarter and did not trail. The lead reached 48 in the fourth. Kawhi Leonard, Scottie Barnes and Immanuel Quickley scored 23 each, and RJ Barrett added 20. Matas Buzelis led Chicago with 17.

## Trail Blazers 124, Suns 83

Portland led 43-12 after the first quarter and 72-39 at halftime. Damian Lillard scored 25 with six threes at the Moda Center, Toumani Camara and Deni Avdija scored 18 each, and Avdija had 10 rebounds. Devin Booker scored 23 for Phoenix.

## Clippers 121, Kings 96

Sacramento never led at the Intuit Dome. The Clippers won the first quarter 41-29 and scored 10 straight early in the third to lead 71-56. Darius Garland scored 30, Brook Lopez had 17 points and 11 rebounds, and [Keaton Wagler](/player/NBA/5254165) scored 15 with three threes, the rookie [our Free Minutes desk flagged](/story/add-keaton-wagler-clippers-open-without-ingram-strus-beal) before the opener. Domantas Sabonis had 14 points, 18 rebounds and eight assists for Sacramento, and Zach LaVine scored 28.
