Narito Namizato pulled up from outside the paint with 23 seconds left and gave the Yokohama B-Corsairs their first lead since early in the third quarter. He then made two free throws with five seconds to go, and Yokohama beat the Utsunomiya Brex 74-71 on Sunday at Nikkan Arena Tochigi to split their opening series.

Utsunomiya led 70-63 with 3:39 left after two Atsuya Ogawa free throws. Yokohama scored 11 of the last 12 points. Kyle Richardson made a layup and the free throw after the foul, Namizato hit a jumper, and Takuma Sato tied it 70-70 with a layup with 49 seconds left. Gavin Edwards missed the first of two free throws and made the second to put the Brex back in front 71-70. Namizato answered on the next possession.

Yokohama had started fast, leading 24-14 after the first quarter. Utsunomiya won the second quarter 23-12 to lead 37-36 at halftime, and was ahead 56-49 after three.

## The glass and the line

Yokohama took 46 rebounds to the Brex's 32 and had 16 offensive rebounds to Utsunomiya's seven, which turned into a 17-2 edge in second-chance points. The Corsairs also scored 14 fast-break points to eight. Utsunomiya made seven of 27 threes and Yokohama five of 25, and the Brex were the better team at the free-throw line by volume, making 22 of 29 to Yokohama's 19 of 21. Yokohama committed 15 turnovers to the Brex's 12, each team had seven steals, and the Brex had four blocks to one. The Brex were called for 24 fouls and Yokohama 26.

Neither team shot well. Yokohama made 25 of 66 from the field, 38%, and Utsunomiya 21 of 56, 38%, so the game came down to the last 40 seconds. Edwards made one of two free throws for a 71-70 lead at 36 seconds, Namizato's jumper at 23 seconds put Yokohama in front, and the Brex never scored again. Kell missed a three with 13 seconds left, Namizato took the defensive rebound and was fouled with five seconds to go, and after his two free throws Kell missed one more three with three seconds left.

Isaiah Livers led Yokohama with 16 points and nine rebounds, making five of eight two-point shots but two of eight threes, and he had four turnovers in 33 minutes. Kameron McGusty scored 15 in 17 minutes, making four of six two-point shots and four of five free throws. Namizato had 12 points with four assists and made all six of his free throws. Takuma Sato made five of six free throws for 10 points, and Yokohama was plus-10 in his 22 minutes. Maik Kotsar had 12 rebounds, four of them offensive.

Trey Kell III led Utsunomiya with 20 points and eight rebounds, making three of 11 threes, including two in the fourth quarter. Gavin Edwards scored 15 with two blocks and three turnovers and was minus-13 in 33 minutes. Atsuya Ogawa had 14 points and four assists. Makoto Hiejima scored 12 off the bench in 26 minutes, and Utsunomiya was plus-13 with him on the floor, the best mark on either team. Hiejima and Shinji Takashima both finished with five fouls.

The Brex won Saturday's opener 88-78, leading by as many as 25. Kell scored 19 with all 10 of his free throws, Isaac Fotu added 16, and Kai King scored 15 for Yokohama on three-of-four three-point shooting. The crowd on Sunday was 5,595, and Saturday's was 6,206.
