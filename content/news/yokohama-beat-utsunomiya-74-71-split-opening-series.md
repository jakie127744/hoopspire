Narito Namizato pulled up from outside the paint with 23 seconds left and gave the Yokohama B-Corsairs their first lead since early in the third quarter. He then made two free throws with five seconds to go, and Yokohama beat the Utsunomiya Brex 74-71 on Sunday at Nikkan Arena Tochigi to split their opening series.

Utsunomiya led 70-63 with 3:39 left after two Atsuya Ogawa free throws. Yokohama scored 11 of the last 12 points. Kyle Richardson made a layup and the free throw after the foul, Namizato hit a jumper, and Takuma Sato tied it 70-70 with a layup with 49 seconds left. Gavin Edwards missed the first of two free throws and made the second to put the Brex back in front 71-70. Namizato answered on the next possession.

Yokohama had started fast, leading 24-14 after the first quarter. Utsunomiya won the second quarter 23-12 to lead 37-36 at halftime, and was ahead 56-49 after three.

Yokohama outrebounded the Brex 46-32 and had 16 offensive rebounds to Utsunomiya's seven, which turned into a 10-2 edge in second-chance points. The Brex made seven of 27 threes, and Yokohama made five of 25.

Isaiah Livers led Yokohama with 16 points and nine rebounds. Kameron McGusty scored 15 in 17 minutes, Namizato 12 with four assists, and Maik Kotsar had 12 rebounds. Trey Kell III led Utsunomiya with 20 points and eight rebounds, including two threes in the fourth quarter. Edwards scored 15 and Ogawa 14.

The Brex won Saturday's opener 88-78. The crowd on Sunday was 5,595.
