[Ryo Terashima](/player/BLeague/30400) had already saved Hiroshima once. With 30 seconds left in the second overtime at Hiroshima Sun Plaza Hall, Terashima hit a three to tie Shiga at 98-98. In the third overtime, [Kofi Cockburn](/player/BLeague/51000216) scored Hiroshima's first three baskets, and the Hiroshima Dragonflies beat the Shiga Lakes 111-105 on Saturday to stay unbeaten at 6-0.

Cockburn finished with 42 points and 21 rebounds. [Xavier Sneed](/player/BLeague/51000612) scored 38 for Shiga, with 11 rebounds and seven assists.

Elsewhere in the B.League Premier on Saturday, the Nagasaki Velca handed the Gunma Crane Thunders their first loss of the season, 70-59. The San-en NeoPhoenix won 83-81 at Shinshu to move to 5-0.

## Hiroshima 111, Shiga 105 (three overtimes)

Hiroshima led by 17 at 63-46 late in the third quarter. Shiga won the fourth quarter 23-13 and led 77-71 with 2:55 to play. Christopher Smith made a free throw, Cockburn scored on a second chance, and Smith scored twice inside, the second time with seven seconds left, to tie it 78-78.

The first overtime ended 11-11. Shiga led 89-87, and Terashima's layup with 32 seconds left tied it. The second ended 9-9, on Terashima's three. In the third, Cockburn scored at 100-98, 102-98 and 104-100, Terashima added a jumper, and Ryo Yamazaki made three free throws in the last 29 seconds. The crowd was 4,006.

## Nagasaki 70, Gunma 59

Gunma led 21-15 early in the second quarter. Nagasaki went ahead 31-30 with 3:21 left in the half and never trailed again. They led 42-35 at half-time, and after a third quarter that ended 9-10, they won the fourth 19-14. Nagasaki's biggest lead, 13 points, came with 14 seconds left.

[Jarrell Brantley](/player/BLeague/51000304) led Nagasaki with 15 points and nine rebounds, and Levi Randolph scored 14 with six assists. [Kerry Blackshear Jr.](/player/BLeague/51000145) had 16 points and 10 rebounds for Gunma, who are now 5-1. The crowd at Happiness Arena was 5,832.

## San-en 83, Shinshu 81

Shinshu led 50-45 in the third quarter, and the fourth quarter was tied at 60, 62, 65 and 70. San-en led 77-72 with 2:07 left after Jake Van Tubbergen's jumper. Mike Daum's three cut it to 77-75, then Vitto Brown's free throw and layup and a Van Tubbergen free throw made it 81-75. Daiki Tsuchiya hit a three with 11 seconds left, Van Tubbergen made two more free throws, and Takatoshi Furukawa's three with eight seconds left made it 83-81. Van Tubbergen and Brown scored 19 each. Mike Daum had 22 points and 10 rebounds for Shinshu.
