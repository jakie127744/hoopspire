[David Johnson](/player/NBL/2451257) made five of six threes, nine of 10 shots from the field and all nine of his free throws, and scored 32 points in 18 minutes off the bench, and the Tasmania JackJumpers beat the Illawarra Hawks 122-112 at the WIN Entertainment Centre on Friday night. The 122 points are a JackJumpers franchise record.

Tasmania are 4-1. They did it with Majok Deng, Ben Ayre, Bryce Hamilton and Anthony Drmic still to come into the line-up. Illawarra are 1-5. Their one win came at home a week earlier, against Adelaide.

## Twelve from Bannan, then 11 straight

[Josh Bannan](/player/NBL/4702058) scored 12 of Tasmania's 32 first-quarter points, and the JackJumpers shot 12 of 19 from the field in the quarter to lead 32-22. Illawarra had led 11-7 early. Tasmania went ahead for good at 15-13 with 3:52 left in the first quarter.

They opened the second quarter with 11 straight points for a 43-22 lead. At half-time it was 67-47, with Tasmania shooting 72% from the field and nine of 15 from three. Johnson had 16 by the break, and [Ben Gold](/player/NBL/5447899) 11.

## A 27-point lead, and a late run

Johnson added nine more in the third quarter. Tasmania's lead reached 27, at 78-51, with 7:34 left in the period, and it was 91-73 after three.

Illawarra had two short runs in the second half. They scored nine straight in the third quarter, from 85-58 down to 85-67 with 2:49 left in it, and eight straight in the fourth, from 112-90 to 112-98 with 2:13 left. Neither got them within single figures.

The Hawks scored 39 points in the fourth quarter to Tasmania's 31. They got as close as nine, at 116-107, after a Kyle Adnam three with 39 seconds left, but no closer.

## The lines

Tasmania shot 65% from the field, 16 of 28 from three and 26 of 32 at the free throw line, with 26 assists. Bannan finished with 25 points, seven rebounds and four assists. Captain [Will Magnay](/player/NBL/4066480) made all four of his shots from the field and had 14 points, nine rebounds, eight assists and two blocks. [Taran Armstrong](/player/NBL/5447897) added 17 points and five assists, Gold 14 with three threes, and [Nick Marshall](/player/NBL/5090851) eight points and five assists.

[Kelan Martin](/player/NBL/5447958) led Illawarra with 23 points. [William Hickey](/player/NBL/5090847) scored 21, [Milton Doyle](/player/NBL/2992564) 20 with three steals, Sam Froling 12 and Adnam 11, and Daniel Grida nine off the bench on three-of-three shooting. The Hawks shot 58% themselves and made 14 of 27 threes, but went to the free throw line 17 times to Tasmania's 32.

## What is next
