San-en NeoPhoenix had a four-point lead and 20 seconds to hold it. They did not. Dwayne Evans II hit a three with nine seconds left, and on the last play of the game Ryo Yamazaki, off a pass from Evans, made another. The Hiroshima Dragonflies beat San-en 77-75 on Monday night in front of 4,874 at Hiroshima Green Arena.

It was a meeting of two unbeaten West teams. Hiroshima are now 8-0 and top of the West. San-en fall to 5-1. Nagoya Diamond Dolphins, 92-77 winners at Shinshu, are 6-0, and they and Hiroshima are the only unbeaten clubs left in the Premier.

## Hiroshima 77, San-en 75

**Hiroshima's half.** San-en led 26-22 after one. Hiroshima won the second quarter 30-14 and led by 15 at 50-35 late in the half.

**San-en's answer.** The NeoPhoenix scored nine straight early in the third, from 54-40 to 54-49, and went ahead 57-56 on a Jake Van Tubbergen basket with 3:23 left in the quarter. They won the quarter 20-10.

**The finish.** Evans and Nick Mayo put Hiroshima up 70-65 with 3:46 left. San-en scored the next six, with Vitto Brown's dunk making it 71-70 with 1:41 to go. Smith's free throw tied it 71-71. Brown made two free throws with 36 seconds left, and Terence Davis two more with 20 left: 75-71. Evans answered from three, assisted by Christopher Smith. Ryusei Sasaki missed both of his free throws at the other end with eight seconds left, Mayo took the rebound, and Yamazaki's three went in at the end.

Smith led Hiroshima with 17. Kofi Cockburn had 16 points and 16 rebounds, and Mayo 12 points and 10 rebounds. Sasaki scored 15 for San-en with seven assists.

## SunRockers 107, Chiba Jets 64

The biggest crowd of the night, 9,464 at LaLa arena TOKYO-BAY, watched the home team lose by 43. Chiba Jets led 21-15 in the first quarter. The Tokyo SunRockers won the second 22-11 and the third 32-12, and the lead reached 43 at 95-52 with 5:13 left. It finished there.

Josh Hawkinson scored 21 and made all three of his threes. Bryson Williams added 20. Luke Maye led Chiba Jets with 12. The SunRockers are 6-3; Chiba Jets are 3-6.

## Sendai 80, Kobe 71

Two days after scoring 34 against Utsunomiya, Jarrett Culver scored 38 at Xebio Arena Sendai. He made seven of 12 threes. Sendai led 62-36 late in the third quarter. Kobe won the fourth 29-13 and finished nine behind. Yoeli Childs had 19 points and 10 rebounds for Kobe. Sendai have won five straight and are 6-3.

## The rest of Monday

**Alvark Tokyo 90, Akita 74.** Akita never led at Toyota Arena Tokyo. Keyontae Johnson scored 27 with five threes, and Dejon Jarreau added 21. Alvark Tokyo are 8-1 with seven straight wins and lead the East. Mark Smith had 27 points, eight rebounds and eight assists for Akita.

**Altiri Chiba 82, Nagasaki 65.** Nagasaki led 14-7 early at Happiness Arena. Altiri Chiba led by 19 late. D.J. Newbill had 24 points, 14 rebounds and six assists, and Dererk Pardon scored 20. Kai Sotto did not play. Io Nagano made five threes and scored 18 for Nagasaki.

**Nagoya 92, Shinshu 77.** Shinshu led 16-8 at White Ring. Nagoya won the second quarter 32-18 and led by 17 in the third. Scott Eatherton scored 19 and Takumi Saito 16.

**Utsunomiya 83, Gunma 80.** Gunma led 25-10 in the first quarter and 78-74 with 2:43 left. Makoto Hiejima's three made it 78-77, and Otis Frazier III scored inside with 34 seconds left to put Utsunomiya ahead 79-78. Isaac Fotu led Utsunomiya with 19. Stanley Johnson scored 22 for Gunma.

**Shimane 80, Kawasaki 69.** Kawasaki led by eight in the first quarter. Shimane led by 16 in the third. Dexter Dennis and Yuta Okada scored 17 each. Kawasaki have lost three in a row.

**Ryukyu 99, Shiga 67.** Ryukyu won by 32 at Okinawa Suntory Arena, in front of 7,302. Jack Cooley had 18 points and 11 rebounds, and Kiefer Ravena scored 15. Ryukyu are 4-4.

This follows our [weekend roundup](/story/keisei-tominaga-35-hokkaido-109-again-culver-34-sendai-hiroshima-7-0-bleague-weekend), when Hiroshima went 7-0 with an 89-71 win at Altiri Chiba.
