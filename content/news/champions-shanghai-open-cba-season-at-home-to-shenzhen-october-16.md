Brandon Goodwin averaged 29.2 points and 9.0 assists over the five games of last season's CBA Finals, and the Shanghai Sharks beat Zhejiang Guangsha 4-1 for their first title since the 2001-02 season. Goodwin was named Finals MVP. On Friday, October 16, the Sharks begin their defence at home against the Shenzhen Leopards in the opening game of the 2026-27 season.

The league published the regular season schedule on September 17. The season has 46 rounds.

## How Shanghai won it

Shanghai finished the 2025-26 regular season 38-4, five wins clear of every other club, and won all 21 of their home games. In the playoffs they swept the Shandong Heroes in the quarter-finals and beat the Beijing Ducks 3-1 in the semi-finals.

The Sharks took the first three games of the Finals. Zhejiang Guangsha won Game 4 to stay alive. Back at home on June 5, Shanghai won Game 5 103-82 and closed out the series.

Wang Zhelin had 29 points and 14 rebounds in the clinching game, and Goodwin scored 25. Goodwin had a double-double in points and assists in three of the five Finals games. Lu Wei coached the team to the title.

It was the second championship in the club's history. The first came in 2002, with Yao Ming at center. Yao spent five years with Shanghai and was the league's MVP in 2001, and his number 15 has been retired by the club.

## The core of the champions

Goodwin, 30, is entering his third season in China and his second with Shanghai, after a season with Shanxi in 2024-25. He averaged 15.9 points and 7.1 assists last season.

Inside, Shanghai have two Chinese national team players, Wang Zhelin, 32, and Zhang Zhenlin, 27, who joined from Liaoning before last season. Li Tianrong, entering his sixth straight season with the club, averaged 11.8 points last season and made 58.7% of his threes, on about four attempts a game.

## A hard September in Beijing

The title earned Shanghai a place at the FIBA Intercontinental Cup, a six-team club event played in Beijing from September 22 to 27. The teams played in two groups of three. The top two in each group went to the semi-finals, and the two last-placed teams met for fifth place.

Shanghai lost all three of their games and finished sixth.

The first, on September 23, was the closest. Goodwin scored 37 points, but the RSSB Tigers of Rwanda, the Basketball Africa League champions, won 117-115 in overtime. Since the Intercontinental Cup became a yearly event in 2013, no player has scored more in a single game. The next day Shanghai lost to Rytas Vilnius of Lithuania, the Basketball Champions League Europe winners, which sent them to the game for fifth place. On Saturday, September 26, the Beijing Royal Fighters, the hosts, won it 101-87.

The result left the Zhejiang Golden Bulls' third place in 2023 as the best finish by a Chinese club at the event. NBA G League United won the title, beating Rytas 89-86 in the final on September 27.

## What is next

The CBA season opens on Friday, October 16, in Shanghai: the Shanghai Sharks, champions, against the Shenzhen Leopards, in the first of the regular season's 46 rounds.
