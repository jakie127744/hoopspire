[Giannis Antetokounmpo](/player/NBA/3032977) won the opening tip for the Miami Heat, and 66 seconds later he found [Andrew Wiggins](/player/NBA/3059319) for a 26-foot three. It was the first of seven assists in his first game in a Heat uniform, and every one of them was a made three. Miami beat the Toronto Raptors 129-105 in the preseason opener for both teams on Saturday at the Videotron Centre in Quebec City.

Antetokounmpo played 14 minutes, all in the first half. He scored nine points on 3-of-3 shooting, made three of four free throws and had four rebounds and two turnovers. The Heat were plus-4 with him on the floor.

## The first quarter

Toronto led twice, at 2-0 and 4-3. Miami never trailed again after [Bam Adebayo](/player/NBA/4066261) made two free throws at 10:16 of the first quarter.

Antetokounmpo drove for a layup and the free throw at 9:15. He fed Wiggins for another three at 8:54, hit a 14-foot fadeaway at 7:39, found Adebayo for a three at 7:16 and [Davion Mitchell](/player/NBA/4278053) for a step-back three at 5:49. When he checked out at 5:30, Miami led 20-12.

The bench kept going. [Tim Hardaway Jr.](/player/NBA/2528210) scored eight points in less than two and a half minutes, a pull-up, a 27-foot three and three free throws, and the Heat led 41-23 after one quarter.

## Wiggins and the second unit

Antetokounmpo came back to open the second quarter. He scored on a driving layup and the free throw at 9:25, then set up Wiggins for threes at 8:54 and 6:44 and Hardaway for a step-back three at 4:33. He left for good at 3:58 with Miami ahead 60-48.

Wiggins finished 4 of 6 from three, all four assisted by Antetokounmpo, and scored 12 points in 13 minutes. Miami led 76-55 at halftime.

## Toronto's push

The Raptors won the third quarter 25-19. They scored eight straight points to cut it to 80-69 at 6:20, and got no closer. Miami closed the quarter with a free throw and opened the fourth with seven straight points, two free throws and a three from Schieffelin and a basket from Bez Mbeng, to lead 102-80 at 10:48. The Heat's biggest lead was 24, at 127-103 with 1:10 left.

## The numbers

Hardaway scored 14 points on 4-of-4 shooting in 11 minutes. [Pelle Larsson](/player/NBA/4601025) made all seven of his free throws for 13 points. [Ian Schieffelin](/player/NBA/4693954) went 4 for 4 for 11, and [Nikola Jović](/player/NBA/4997528) had 11 points and seven rebounds. Adebayo scored 10 with six rebounds. [Klay Thompson](/player/NBA/6475) started and went 0 for 2 in 13 minutes.

Miami had 31 assists and out-rebounded Toronto 55-43. It also turned the ball over 25 times, and Toronto had 17 steals. The Heat made 31 of 36 free throws to the Raptors' 19 of 30.

[RJ Barrett](/player/NBA/4395625) and [Tyreke Key](/player/NBA/4279143) led Toronto with 15 points each. Key made four of seven threes. [Scottie Barnes](/player/NBA/4433134) scored 11 with three steals in 12 minutes, and [Jakob Poeltl](/player/NBA/3134908) made all three of his shots for nine.

The crowd was 18,474.
