[Majok Deng](/player/NBL/3143550) scored a layup that tied the NBL season opener at 65-65, about 35 seconds after contact that fractured his fibula. The Tasmania JackJumpers announced on Tuesday that the forward will be out for at least four weeks.

The contact came with about three minutes and 30 seconds left in the third quarter on Monday, September 21, in the flow of the game, and involved South East Melbourne's [Akech Aliir](/player/NBL/5165254). Deng came off the court almost immediately after his layup.

Scans later showed that the 33-year-old has a hairline fracture in his fibula, the smaller of the two shin bones, along with a corked calf muscle (a deep bruise). The injury is a fracture of his left lower leg.

## The timeline

JackJumpers Head of High Performance Ryan Carroll said Deng is resting and doing rehabilitation. "For this kind of injury we would aim for a return in four to six weeks," Carroll said. "Time is one thing, but there will be constant assessment and rehabilitation to ensure Majok can return to his high level of performance."

The club did not give a return date or name a game. It said Development Player [Jed Richardson](/player/NBL/5332168) will step up for extra game time while Deng recovers.

The league lists Deng's lower leg injury with a return in Rounds 8 to 10, in an update on Tuesday.

## The opener and the game in Cairns

Deng started the opener and played 16 minutes. He scored six points on 2-of-9 shooting, with three rebounds and a block. Tasmania trailed 52-38 at halftime and won 96-91 by outscoring the Phoenix 58-39 in the second half, as our [report on the game](/story/jackjumpers-beat-phoenix-96-91-nbl-opener) covered at the time.

Two nights later Tasmania lost 93-87 at Cairns, without Deng and four other players who were already out. Cairns led 32-16 after the first quarter. Tasmania outscored the Taipans 71-61 over the last three quarters and trailed by six at the end.

[David Johnson](/player/NBL/2451257) started in Cairns and scored 18 points, after scoring 26 off the bench in the opener on 10-of-15 shooting, with six rebounds and six assists. Richardson played two minutes at Cairns and did not score. He was not used in the opener. Tasmania used nine players in Cairns. Tasmania is 1-1 after two rounds.

In the opener Deng was one of five starters, along with [Will Magnay](/player/NBL/4066480), [Taran Armstrong](/player/NBL/5447897), [Nick Marshall](/player/NBL/5090851) and [Dakota Mathias](/player/NBL/3136991). At Cairns, Johnson took his place in the starting five.

## Who else is out

Deng is the fifth JackJumper on the injury list. The other four:

- Bryce Hamilton, with an ACL injury and no return date
- Anthony Drmic, with a back injury and no return date
- Ben Ayre, with a hip flexor injury, listed as a test
- Josh Bannan, with a rib injury, listed as a game-time decision

Bannan, Drmic, Ayre and Hamilton all sat out the Cairns game. It is not known when any of the four was hurt.

## What is next

Tasmania hosts Melbourne United on Thursday, October 1, in Round 3 at MyState Bank Arena. Tip-off is at 7:30 p.m. AEST. Melbourne United beat South East Melbourne 86-61 on September 24. Bannan's status will be decided on the day. Deng is out, and his return depends on constant assessment.
