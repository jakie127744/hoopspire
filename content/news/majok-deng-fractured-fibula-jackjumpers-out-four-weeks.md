[Majok Deng](/player/NBL/3143550) scored a layup that tied the NBL season opener at 65-65, about 35 seconds after contact that the Tasmania JackJumpers now say fractured his fibula. The club announced on Tuesday that the forward will be out for at least four weeks.

The contact came with about three minutes and 30 seconds left in the third quarter on Monday, September 21, in the flow of the game, and involved South East Melbourne's [Akech Aliir](/player/NBL/5165254). Deng came off the court almost immediately after his layup, according to the [club's announcement](https://www.jackjumpers.com.au/news/heart-break-and-broken-bone-for-fan-favourite-majok-deng).

Scans later showed that the 33-year-old has a hairline fracture in his fibula, the smaller of the two shin bones, along with a corked calf muscle (a deep bruise), the club said. The club described the injury as a fracture of his left lower leg.

## What the club said

JackJumpers Head of High Performance Ryan Carroll said Deng is resting and doing rehabilitation. "For this kind of injury we would aim for a return in four to six weeks," Carroll said in the club's statement. "Time is one thing, but there will be constant assessment and rehabilitation to ensure Majok can return to his high level of performance."

The club did not give a return date or name a game. It said Development Player [Jed Richardson](/player/NBL/5332168) will step up for extra game time while Deng recovers.

The NBL's [injury list](https://www.nbl.com.au/news/nbl26-the-latest-injury-updates), updated on Tuesday, shows Deng's lower leg injury with a return in Rounds 8 to 10. The league's [own report](https://www.nbl.com.au/news/injury-sidelines-key-jackjumper-majok-deng) on the injury carries the same details as the club's.

## The opener and the game in Cairns

Deng started the opener and played 16 minutes. He scored six points on 2-of-9 shooting, with three rebounds and a block. Tasmania trailed 52-38 at halftime and won 96-91 by outscoring the Phoenix 58-39 in the second half, as our [report on the game](/story/jackjumpers-beat-phoenix-96-91-nbl-opener) covered at the time.

Two nights later Tasmania lost 93-87 at Cairns, and Deng did not play. The club's [round two report](https://www.jackjumpers.com.au/news/rd-2-report-shorthanded-jackjumpers-fight-hard-in-cairns) said Deng was on the sidelines that night, along with four players who were already out. Cairns led 32-16 after the first quarter. Tasmania outscored the Taipans 71-61 over the last three quarters and trailed by six at the end.

[David Johnson](/player/NBL/2451257) started in Cairns and scored 18 points, after scoring 26 off the bench in the opener. Richardson played two minutes at Cairns and did not score. He was not used in the opener. Tasmania used nine players in Cairns. Tasmania is 1-1 after two rounds.

## Who else is out

Deng is the fifth JackJumper on the league's injury list. The list shows:

- Bryce Hamilton, with an ACL injury and no return date
- Anthony Drmic, with a back injury and no return date
- Ben Ayre, with a hip flexor injury, listed as a test
- Josh Bannan, with a rib injury, listed as a game-time decision

The club's round two report said Bannan, Drmic, Ayre and Hamilton all sat out the Cairns game. The list did not say when any of the four was hurt.

## What is next

Tasmania hosts Melbourne United on Thursday, October 1, in Round 3 at MyState Bank Arena. Tip-off is at 7:30 p.m. AEST, according to the league's report. Bannan's status will be decided on the day. Deng is out, and the club said his return depends on constant assessment.
