The rematch of last season's Championship Series ended the way the series did not: with Adelaide on top. The 36ers beat the Sydney Kings 84-78 at Afterpay Arena on Sunday, handing the champions their first loss of NBL27.

It did not end quietly. Bryce Cotton and Kendric Davis, who clashed through the five-game series, had to be separated in the final minute, and each was called for a flagrant foul.

Adelaide had arrived at 2-4 after three straight losses. They leave at 3-4. Sydney are 3-1.

Also on Sunday, the Cairns Taipans won 110-100 at Illawarra. On Saturday, Melbourne United beat the South East Melbourne Phoenix 104-99, and the Perth Wildcats beat the New Zealand Breakers 100-84.

## Sydney 78, Adelaide 84

It was 18-18 after one quarter, after Matt Kenyon's buzzer-beater for Adelaide. Sydney won the second quarter 26-23 and led 44-41 at half-time, with Xavier Cooks on 15 points.

The third quarter swung it. Adelaide scored 20 and held Sydney to 12, on six of 16 shooting, none of five from three. The 36ers led 61-56 after three and stretched it to 12, at 76-64, with 4:48 left.

John Jenkins III scored 20 and made six of his 17 threes. Cotton had 17 points and eight assists. Nick Rakocevic added 16 points and nine rebounds, Zylan Cheatham 10 points and 10 rebounds, and Kenyon 11 points and eight rebounds, with all three of his threes.

Cooks led Sydney with 22 points and eight rebounds. Davis scored 16 with seven assists but shot six of 19.

## Illawarra 100, Cairns 110

Cairns made 18 of 38 threes and shot 58% from the field. Jack McVeigh scored 32 and made five of nine from deep. Jonah Bolden added 20 points, eight rebounds and four blocks, and made four of his five threes. Keanu Pinder had 19 points and six assists, Jaylon Brown 14 points and nine assists, and Reyne Smith 14 on four threes.

The Taipans led by 17 in the second quarter, 54-37, and 57-50 at half-time. They led 86-77 after three. Sam Froling scored five straight points to open the fourth and cut it to four, but Cairns scored the next five, and McVeigh's basket with just under four minutes left kept them seven clear.

Illawarra took 13 offensive rebounds to three and got to the line 10 more times, but shot 11 of 30 from three. Makur Maker scored 18 with nine rebounds, and Froling had 16 points and 12 rebounds. Cairns are 4-3 after three straight wins. Illawarra are 1-6, after losing twice at home this weekend.

Next Star Luke Paul had two points and three assists in 11 minutes for Cairns.

## Saturday: South East Melbourne 99, Melbourne United 104

The Phoenix led by 15, at 42-27 in the second quarter, and still led 65-55 midway through the third. United then scored 11 straight points to lead 66-65 with 3:01 left in the quarter. It was 74-74 after three.

The lead changed hands four times in the fourth quarter, the last when United went ahead 88-87 with 5:42 left. They did not trail again, and led by seven at 96-89. Luke Travers scored 24, Josh Oduro had 21 points and 10 rebounds, and Cole Anthony had 18 points and seven assists. Nathan Sobey scored 24 for the Phoenix and D'Shawn Schwartz 19. United are 3-3. The Phoenix are 2-4.

## Saturday: Perth 100, New Zealand 84

Sam Mennenga scored 40 points with 18 rebounds and five assists for the Breakers, and it was still a 16-point loss. The game was tied 55-55 midway through the third quarter. Perth led 70-66 after three, and from 78-74 with 6:18 left they went on a 16-2 run to lead 94-76.

Dylan Windler scored 32 for Perth with eight rebounds, and Elijah Pepper scored 25. Kristian Doolittle had nine assists. Brandon Knight, the former NBA guard who signed with Perth in September, scored six points in 20 minutes in his Wildcats debut. Perth are 4-1. New Zealand are 3-3.
