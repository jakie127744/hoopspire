Ben Bentil scored 29 points and grabbed 13 rebounds in Game 7 of the TPBL final in June, and the Formosa Dreamers beat the New Taipei Kings 90-78 to win the title. It was the franchise's first. The Kings had led that series 3-1.

The new season starts with the same two teams. The Dreamers host the Kings on Saturday, October 17, at 5:00 p.m. at Taichung's Intercontinental Basketball Stadium, the arena where Game 7 was played. Before that, all seven clubs play a preseason weekend at the Taipei Heping Basketball Gymnasium from Friday, October 9, to Sunday, October 11.

## How the final turned

The Kings took Game 1 in Taichung by a point, 91-90. The Dreamers won Game 2 there, 99-90. Then the series moved to Xinzhuang Gymnasium, and the Kings won twice: 109-84 in Game 3 and 96-93 in Game 4.

One more win would have given the Kings the title. They did not get it. The Dreamers won Game 5 at home 105-69, a 36-point margin. They won Game 6 in Xinzhuang 91-88. Then they won Game 7 in Taichung on June 6.

## Game 7, quarter by quarter

The Kings led 21-18 after the first quarter. The Dreamers won the second 28-17 and led 46-38 at the half. The Kings won the third 20-15 and cut it to 61-58 with a quarter left. The Dreamers won the fourth 29-20.

Bentil led everyone with his 29 and 13. Trey Thompkins III added 14 points and eight rebounds, Lin Chun-chi scored 12 and Randall Walko 10. For the Kings, Austin Daye scored 16, Jason Washburn 15, and Jalen Harris and Lin Shu-wei 14 each.

## The season

Seven clubs play a 126-game regular season, 36 games each, from October 17 to May 16, 2027. The seven are the Formosa Dreamers, the Hsinchu Toplus Lioneers, the Kaohsiung Aquas, the New Taipei CTBC DEA, the New Taipei Kings, the Taipei Taishin Mars and the Taoyuan Taiwan Beer Leopards.

The Dreamers open with two home games in Taichung. After the Kings on Saturday, they play the Kaohsiung Aquas there on Sunday, October 18, also at 5:00 p.m.

Three clubs start a week later. On Saturday, October 24, the Lioneers host the Dreamers at Hsinchu County Gymnasium and the DEA host the Mars at Xinzhuang Gymnasium. On Sunday, October 25, the Lioneers host the Aquas and the DEA host the Kings. The Leopards are last to start: their first game is at the Kings in Xinzhuang on Saturday, October 31.

## The preseason weekend

All seven preseason games are at the Heping Basketball Gymnasium in Taipei. Times are Taiwan time.

- **Friday, October 9:** New Taipei CTBC DEA v Kaohsiung Aquas, 2:30 p.m. Formosa Dreamers v Taipei Taishin Mars, 5:00 p.m.
- **Saturday, October 10:** New Taipei Kings v Taoyuan Taiwan Beer Leopards, noon. Kaohsiung Aquas v Hsinchu Toplus Lioneers, 2:30 p.m. New Taipei CTBC DEA v Formosa Dreamers, 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday, October 11:** Taoyuan Taiwan Beer Leopards v Taipei Taishin Mars, noon. Hsinchu Toplus Lioneers v New Taipei Kings, 2:30 p.m.

Seven games across seven clubs means every club plays twice that weekend.

## What is next

The preseason starts on Friday, October 9, at 2:30 p.m. in Taipei. The regular season opens on Saturday, October 17, at 5:00 p.m. in Taichung: the Formosa Dreamers, champions, against the New Taipei Kings, the team they beat in seven games in June.
