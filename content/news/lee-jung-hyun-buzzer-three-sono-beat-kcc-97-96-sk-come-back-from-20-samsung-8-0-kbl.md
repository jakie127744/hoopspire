KCC were ahead 96-94 with six seconds left in Goyang, and Lee Jae-do was at the line for Sono with three free throws. He made the first two to tie it. He missed the third. Johnny O'Bryant III got the rebound, the ball found Lee Jung-hyun, and his three went in as the clock hit zero. The Goyang Sono Skygunners beat Busan KCC 97-96 on Saturday.

It was a weekend of finishes. The Seoul SK Knights trailed Changwon LG by 20 on Saturday and won by a point. Wonju DB led all the way at Anyang and still needed a missed free throw to hold on. The Seoul Samsung Thunders won twice on the road and are 8-0.

This follows [Friday's KBL report](/story/marei-34-rebounds-lg-beat-hyundai-mobis-88-83-db-come-back-beat-kt-102-95-kbl), when DB came back to win at KT.

## Sono 97, KCC 96 (Saturday)

**KCC pull ahead.** The lead changed hands nine times. KCC led 92-85 with 3:53 left after a Shawn Long basket.

**Sono come back.** O'Bryant hit a three and Lim Dong-seop scored to make it 92-90. Heo Hoon scored, Lee Jung-hyun answered, and Heo Ung's basket made it 96-92 with 17 seconds left.

**The last play.** Kevin Quiambao missed a three, Lim got the offensive rebound, and Lee Jae-do was fouled. After his two free throws and the miss, O'Bryant's rebound and Quiambao's pass set up Lee Jung-hyun for the winning three.

O'Bryant led Sono with 23 points and eight rebounds. Lee Jung-hyun scored 19, and Quiambao had 16 points, five rebounds and four assists. Heo Hoon had 19 points and eight assists for KCC, and Long had 19 points and 14 rebounds. Sono have won three in a row. KCC have lost five straight.

## SK 86, LG 85 (Saturday)

LG scored 12 straight points late in the first quarter and led 32-13 after one. The lead reached 20 at 37-17 early in the second quarter.

SK outscored LG 73-53 over the last three quarters. Isaiah Hicks' basket with three seconds left in the third gave SK their first lead, 66-65. The lead changed hands six more times in the fourth. Yu Ki-sang's three put LG up 85-83 with 2:32 left, and Kim Nak-hyeon answered with a three for 86-85 with 1:31 left. Nobody scored again. Jameel Warney missed a three for SK, and Yu missed two threes for LG in the last three seconds.

Kim scored 19 for SK on five threes in eight tries. An Young-jun had 16, and Hicks had 15 points and 10 rebounds. Archie Goodwin III scored 19 for LG, Assem Marei had 17 points, 13 rebounds and seven assists, and Yang Jun-seok had 16 points and 12 assists. Carl Tamayo made three of his five threes for 10 points.

## SK 90, KT 79 (Sunday)

SK came back the next day and beat Suwon KT at home. KT led 61-58 midway through the third quarter, and SK scored the next 15 points. Warney scored 25, Hicks 18 and An 18, and An had six steals. Paris Bass scored 21 for KT, who have lost five in a row.

## DB 95, Jung Kwan Jang 93 (Sunday)

DB scored first and never trailed. They led by 15 early in the third quarter, at 67-52. Anyang Jung Kwan Jang came back with a 10-0 run later in the quarter and stayed close to the end. Davon Reed's basket made it 94-93 with three seconds left. Ethan Alvano made one of two free throws, and Reed's rebound came with a second left, too late for a shot.

Rayshaun Hammonds scored 31 with 11 rebounds, and Alvano 28 with six assists. DB have won three straight. Rhenz Abando had 22 points and 13 rebounds for Jung Kwan Jang, and Reed scored 21.

## Samsung win twice

**Saturday: Samsung 91, Hyundai Mobis 71.** Samsung led by 23 late in the fourth quarter in Ulsan. Kerem Kanter had 27 points and 11 rebounds, and Christopher McCullough scored 21 off the bench. Justin Gutang had 11 points and seven assists. Darius Bazley scored 33 with 11 rebounds for Mobis.

**Sunday: Samsung 76, Korea Gas 68.** Samsung scored 12 straight in the third quarter in Daegu for a 55-41 lead. Daegu Korea Gas got within four midway through the fourth and no closer. Kanter scored 23. Keve Aluma had 25 points and 11 rebounds for Korea Gas, and Sam Belangel scored 22 with seven assists.

## Standings

Samsung are 8-0. SK are second at 3-1, then Jung Kwan Jang at 6-3, DB at 5-3 and Sono at 4-4. KCC, KT and Hyundai Mobis are at the bottom with two or three wins.
