The game was tied 78-78 with seven seconds left, and [Sota Oura](/player/BLeague/30513) went to the line with one second on the clock. He missed both free throws. Ryukyu Golden Kings kept the ball, Oura passed to [Kiefer Ravena](/player/BLeague/5100000062), and Ravena made a three with the clock at 0:00. Ryukyu beat SunRockers Shibuya 81-78 on Thursday in front of 7,850 fans.

Ravena finished with 22 points on 6-of-10 three-point shooting. Shibuya had led by 13 in the third quarter.

## Shibuya's 13-point lead

SunRockers Shibuya led 28-24 after the first quarter. Ryukyu scored 11 in the second quarter, and Shibuya went into half-time ahead 44-35. [Reiya Nozaki](/player/BLeague/15853) hit two threes in the quarter, at 7:19 and 4:51, and Shibuya's lead was nine at the break.

It grew in the third quarter. After Ravena's three made it 44-40, Shibuya scored the next nine points. [Josh Hawkinson](/player/BLeague/12598) and [John Lawrence Harper Jr.](/player/BLeague/5100000034) scored layups, [Donte Grantham](/player/BLeague/51000496) made a three, and [Rawle Alkins](/player/BLeague/51000515) added a jumper off a fast break at 7:07 for 53-40. That was Shibuya's biggest lead, 13 points.

## Cooley and Ryukyu come back

Ryukyu outscored Shibuya 23-14 in the third quarter and tied it at 58-58. [Jack Cooley](/player/BLeague/26914) scored 10 of those 23 points, the last two with free throws at 0:56 that made it 58-58. Oura added a three at 3:22, and [David Nwaba](/player/BLeague/51000416) scored on a fast break off a turnover at 1:06.

Ryukyu went ahead 61-58 on [Shota Onodera](/player/BLeague/8756)'s three at 9:04 of the fourth quarter, and led by as many as six, 73-67, when Cooley made a three with 2:57 left.

## Shibuya's 8-0 run

Shibuya scored the next eight points. Hawkinson scored inside at 2:32, Alkins hit a fast-break layup at 2:02, Hawkinson made two free throws at 1:24, and Grantham's fast-break layup at 1:00 put Shibuya ahead 75-73. Ravena answered with a three at 0:52, 76-75, and Cooley's layup off a second chance made it 78-75 with 17 seconds left.

Alkins tied it with a three at 0:07, 78-78. Then came the foul on Oura, the two misses, and Ravena's three. The next entries in the official play-by-play are the end of the period and the end of the game.

## The numbers behind it

Ryukyu made 13 of 30 threes, 39 of its 81 points. Shibuya made 7 of 19 and scored more inside, 24 of 46 on two-point shots against Ryukyu's 17 of 33. At the free-throw line Ryukyu was 8 of 13 and Shibuya 9 of 12. Ryukyu had 13 turnovers and Shibuya 12.

Cooley had 20 points and 10 rebounds, and made 5 of 6 free throws. Oura had 15 points and eight assists and made all six of his two-point shots. Nwaba scored 8 with four assists and three steals, and Ryukyu outscored Shibuya by nine with him on the floor.

For Shibuya, Alkins led the way with 18 points on 8 of 18 shooting. [Ibu Yamazaki](/player/BLeague/11309) had 12 points and seven rebounds. Harper Jr. scored 9 and had six assists and five steals. Hawkinson scored nine with five free throws, four assists and three blocks.

The game had eight lead changes. Ryukyu is 8-8 and Shibuya 11-6.
