With one second left in the first half at LaLa arena TOKYO-BAY, Keyontae Johnson finished a fast break with a dunk. It was his 27th point. Alvark Tokyo had 47. Johnson finished with 37 points and 12 rebounds, and Alvark beat the Chiba Jets 96-71 on Sunday in front of 10,636 fans, the biggest crowd of the day in the B.League Premier.

Johnson did it in 27 minutes. He made nine of 14 twos, three of four threes and 10 of 12 free throws, took six offensive rebounds, had four assists and two steals, and did not commit a foul. Alvark were plus-20 with him on the floor.

## Chiba lead, then the run

For most of the first half this was close. The teams were tied three times in the first quarter, and Alvark led 22-18 after it. Chiba went ahead in the second. Nate Williams scored on a layup, a dunk off a turnover and two free throws, and Chiba led 30-24 with 6:40 left in the half, its biggest lead.

Johnson answered with a three and a dunk off his own steal. Chiba still led 39-36 after another Williams layup at 3:14.

Then Alvark scored 13 straight. [Kai Toews](/player/BLeague/rg-107229) scored on two layups, the second off a turnover, for a 40-39 lead at 2:12. Johnson hit a three at 1:22, scored on a second chance at 0:38 and dunked with one second left. Alvark led 47-39 at halftime, and Johnson's jumper on a fast break at 9:37 of the third quarter finished the run at 49-39. He scored nine of the 13 points.

## Hogg's 83 seconds

Chiba stayed close for a while. [Yuta Watanabe](/player/BLeague/rg-53707) hit a three and [Riku Segawa](/player/BLeague/rg-229316) scored inside to cut it to 49-44 with 8:26 left in the third. [Ryan Rossiter](/player/BLeague/rg-18410) answered with seven points in a row, a three and two jumpers, and Alvark led 61-50 by 3:40.

Then came a second 13-0 run. Johnson scored and drew the foul at 3:25. [D.J. Hogg](/player/BLeague/rg-73143) hit a three at 3:05, a jumper at 2:32, another three at 2:12 and two free throws at 1:42. That was 10 points in 83 seconds, and it was 74-50. Alvark won the quarter 27-14 and led 74-53 after three. The lead reached 28, at 93-65, with 3:31 to play.

## The numbers

Alvark won the boards 47-38 and took 15 offensive rebounds to Chiba's eight. They outscored Chiba 42-24 in the paint and 19-11 on the fast break, and scored 23 points off Chiba's 16 turnovers. Alvark made 12 of 39 threes, Chiba nine of 29. Both teams made 18 free throws.

Hogg scored 14 points in 15 minutes and Toews 11. Rossiter added seven, and [Gen Hiraiwa](/player/BLeague/rg-72650) took nine rebounds.

Nate Williams led Chiba with 17 points, making nine of 10 free throws. [Yuki Togashi](/player/BLeague/rg-73959) scored 15 off the bench with three threes. Watanabe and Segawa scored 12 each, Watanabe making one of eight twos and Segawa handing out six assists. Luke Maye had seven rebounds, two points and four turnovers.
