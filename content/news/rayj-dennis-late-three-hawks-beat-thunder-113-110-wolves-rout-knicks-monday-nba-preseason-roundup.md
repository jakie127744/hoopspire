Aday Mara caught an alley-oop from Bennett Stirtz and dunked it to put the Oklahoma City Thunder ahead 107-106 with 57.8 seconds left in Atlanta on Monday night. The Hawks scored the next seven points. [RayJ Dennis](/player/NBA/4431941) buried a 25-foot three with 11.6 seconds left to make it 111-107, then stole the ball and set up [Kobe Johnson](/player/NBA/4683857) for a layup, and Atlanta won the preseason game 113-110.

It was one of five preseason games on Monday. Minnesota beat the Knicks 122-104 at Madison Square Garden, Utah beat San Antonio 119-109, Brooklyn won 131-122 at Washington, and Portland beat the London Lions 119-66. These games do not count in the standings, and several teams rested regulars.

## Hawks 113, Thunder 110

Oklahoma City started [Bennett Stirtz](/player/NBA/5241364), [Brooks Barnhizer](/player/NBA/4684208), [Aday Mara](/player/NBA/5174983), [Josh Dix](/player/NBA/5105800) and Otega Oweh. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and the rest of the regular rotation did not appear in the box score. The young lineup kept it level for 48 minutes: there were 15 lead changes, and neither side led by more than nine.

Atlanta led 104-102 inside the last two minutes before Barnhizer's 25-foot three put the Thunder back in front 105-104 with 1:43 left. [Asa Newell](/player/NBA/4873201) answered with a turnaround jumper and, after Mara's dunk, a driving dunk for 108-107 with 42.9 seconds left. Then came Dennis. Stirtz hit a three with 0.5 seconds left for the final score.

Stirtz scored 26 points with six threes in 38 minutes, the most in the game. Barnhizer had 20 and eight rebounds, and Mara 17 points, 10 rebounds and six assists. [Jock Landale](/player/NBA/3146557) led Atlanta with 17, [Nickeil Alexander-Walker](/player/NBA/4278039) had 16 and [Zuby Ejiofor](/player/NBA/5106262) 15 off the bench. Dennis finished with 13 points and seven assists. The crowd at State Farm Arena was 15,446.
