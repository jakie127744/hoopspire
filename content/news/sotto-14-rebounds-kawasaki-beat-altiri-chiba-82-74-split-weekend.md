[Kai Sotto](/player/BLeague/rg-133946) went after the first miss of the game, a [Kotetsu Kurokawa](/player/BLeague/rg-219375) jumper, three times in six seconds. He grabbed the offensive rebound, missed the layup, grabbed it again, missed again, then grabbed a third and put it in. A minute later he grabbed another offensive rebound and dunked it for a 4-0 Altiri Chiba lead. Chiba never led by more. The Kawasaki Brave Thunders took the lead late in the first quarter, never gave it back and won 82-74 on Sunday at Chibagin Arena, a day after losing 99-90 in the same building.

The weekend series ends 1-1. Sotto's second game for Chiba gave him 12 points on 6-of-9 shooting, 14 rebounds, five of them offensive, and four assists in 30 minutes and 42 seconds. The crowd was 4,453.

## Kawasaki take over

That 4-0 start was Chiba's biggest lead. The teams were level at 4, and Chiba led 10-6 after a [D.J. Newbill](/player/BLeague/rg-22883) layup at 5:18 of the first quarter. Then Kawasaki scored eight in a row. [Hirohide Araya](/player/BLeague/rg-175842) hit a three on the break, and Muhammad-Ali Abdur-Rahkman scored inside at 4:29 for an 11-10 lead and made the free throw. Kawasaki led the rest of the way.

The second quarter belonged to the visitors, 23-14. Abdur-Rahkman scored eight in the quarter and [Matt Bonds](/player/BLeague/rg-70510) six, most of it at the free-throw line, and [Alex Kirk](/player/BLeague/rg-9391) scored on a second chance with 47 seconds left. Kawasaki led 41-29 at halftime.

## Sotto in the third

Sotto had a quiet second quarter, one assist and one rebound. He was back in the paint in the third. He scored on a layup at 7:48 and drew the foul, though he missed the free throw. He took two defensive rebounds, then an offensive one at 4:06 and put it back in a second later. Chiba scored 25 in the quarter. Kawasaki scored 27, and led 68-54 going into the fourth.

## The 16-point lead and the late push

Bonds hit a second-chance jumper at 9:08 of the fourth for a 70-54 lead, Kawasaki's biggest of the game. [Seiya Ando](/player/BLeague/rg-81768) answered with seven points in 59 seconds: a jumper, a three and a layup.

Kawasaki kept answering, and led 78-65 after two Abdur-Rahkman free throws with 3:12 left. Then Chiba made its run. Sotto dunked at 3:04. [Dererk Pardon](/player/BLeague/rg-84037) dunked on a fast break off a turnover at 2:20. Newbill hit a jumper at 2:10, and it was 78-71.

Jaheru Ryuto Yamauchi stopped it with a fast-break layup at 1:29. [Takaaki Kida](/player/BLeague/rg-251559) hit a three at 0:42 to make it 80-74, and Jaedon LeDee closed it with a putback dunk at 0:28. Chiba won the fourth quarter 20-14, and lost by eight.

## The numbers

Kawasaki outscored Chiba by 14 at the free-throw line. It made 17 of 18. Chiba went to the line seven times and made three. Chiba made seven of 29 threes, Kawasaki three of 17. Kawasaki blocked seven shots, Chiba none, and outscored Chiba 15-6 on the fast break.

Bonds led Kawasaki with 23 points, making eight of 12 twos and all four free throws, with six rebounds. Abdur-Rahkman had 18 points, seven rebounds and six assists, and made seven of eight at the line. Kirk had 14 points, 10 rebounds and three blocks, and LeDee 12 points, seven rebounds and five assists.

Newbill led Chiba with 20 points but made two of 10 threes. Kurokawa, who scored 30 on Saturday, had 15 on 7-of-20 shooting. Ando scored 10 and Pardon had seven points and six assists in 37 minutes.

Chiba took 16 offensive rebounds to Kawasaki's 13, and five of them were Sotto's.
