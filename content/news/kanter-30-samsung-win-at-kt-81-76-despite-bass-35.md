For more than 12 minutes of game time on Sunday, every point Suwon KT scored came from Paris Bass. He scored 15 straight, from a three-point play late in the third quarter to a basket with 2:58 left in the fourth, and it was not enough. Kerem Kanter kept answering at the other end. Kanter scored 30 points with 13 rebounds, and the Seoul Samsung Thunders won their KBL opener 81-76 at Suwon KT Sonicboom Arena.

Bass finished with 35 points and 11 rebounds in 38 minutes for KT, who started their season with a loss. [Kanter](/player/KBL/rg-42177) made nine of his 12 shots and 10 of 14 free throws.

## Level at the half

KT scored the first seven points, two of them from Bass and five from [Kang Sung-wook](/player/KBL/rg-202848), and led 12-4 after a [Moon Jeong-hyeon](/player/KBL/rg-133887) dunk at 4:08 of the first quarter. Samsung answered with eight straight, four of them from Kanter. [Miguel Oczon](/player/KBL/rg-151472) hit three threes in the quarter for KT, and Christopher McCullough tied it at 20 with 14 seconds left.

Samsung led by eight midway through the second quarter, 39-31 after a McCullough dunk at 5:07. KT came back with a basket and a dunk from Trey Porter and a three from Moon, and Kang's basket at 0:23 put KT up 45-43. McCullough dunked with two seconds left, and it was 45-45 at halftime.

## Samsung take over

Samsung went ahead early in the third and stayed there. [Shin Dong-hyuk](/player/KBL/rg-205789) hit a three at 7:59, Kanter another at 7:27, and Kanter's basket at 5:39 made it 57-49. When [Choi Seong-mo](/player/KBL/rg-25844) scored at 1:32, Samsung led 60-52.

## Bass against Kanter

That was when Bass took over KT's offense. He scored and drew the foul at 1:14, scored again at 0:50 to make it 60-57 after three, and opened the fourth with a basket that cut it to 60-59.

Kanter answered with a three at 8:50. Bass scored, Kanter scored. Bass made two free throws, Kanter scored again. Bass scored at 5:37 to make it 67-65, and Kanter made two free throws at 4:27. Kanter had scored all nine of Samsung's points in the quarter, and Samsung still led by four.

[Lee Kyu-tae](/player/KBL/rg-133890) scored at 3:53 and McCullough hit a three at 3:16, and Samsung led 74-65, its biggest lead of the game.

## KT's last push

Bass scored again at 2:58, his 15th straight KT point. Seo Min-soo hit a three at 1:41 and made two free throws at 1:25, and Bass scored at 0:56 to make it 77-74. Choi answered with a basket at 0:35, Kanter made two free throws at 0:16 for 81-74, and Bass's dunk with 12 seconds left closed the scoring.

Bass scored 14 points in the fourth quarter. Kanter scored 12.

## The numbers

KT took 82 shots to Samsung's 59 and made 29 of them, one more than Samsung, but made eight of 37 threes. Kang made four of 16 shots and one of 10 threes, and Jeon Seong-hyun one of nine threes. KT won the boards 44-34. Samsung made 16 of 20 free throws, KT 10 of 15.

McCullough scored 19 on 7-of-12 shooting with eight rebounds. [Han Ho-bin](/player/KBL/rg-52471) had seven points and eight assists, and [Justin Gutang](/player/KBL/rg-206104) had four points and three steals in 23 minutes, with Samsung plus-12 while he played. Kanter and Bass each committed six turnovers.

For KT, Oczon scored 11 on three of five threes, Moon had five points and 10 rebounds, and Porter six points and 11 rebounds.
