[Jack McVeigh](/player/NBL/3911893) found [Jonah Bolden](/player/NBL/3155533) wide open with 51 seconds left, and Bolden hit the three for a 95-91 lead. On the next Cairns trip McVeigh bounced a pass to [Keanu Pinder](/player/NBL/5402797) rolling to the rim, and the Cairns Taipans beat the Brisbane Bullets 97-91 at the Cairns Convention Centre on Thursday night.

It was the first all-Queensland game of NBL27. Cairns are 3-3 with two wins in a row. Brisbane, who won their first two games under new coach Will Weaver, have lost three straight and are 2-3.

## Brisbane's big second quarter

Cairns led 23-17 after one quarter, with Brisbane making one of 11 threes. The Bullets turned it around in the second. They scored 33 points on 13-of-21 shooting and led 50-47 at half-time. [Nate Hinton](/player/NBL/5447894) had 16 by the break and [Tyrell Harrison](/player/NBL/4278785) 14.

[Jaylon Brown](/player/NBL/5447257) kept Cairns in it with 21 first-half points, including five threes. He did not attempt a three in the third quarter, and others picked up the scoring. [Malique Lewis](/player/NBL/5184016) had 15 points by the end of the quarter and Bolden 11, and the Taipans took a 74-72 lead into the fourth.

## The fourth: McVeigh's 10 points

Brisbane scored the first six points of the final quarter for a 78-74 lead. McVeigh, who had five points through three quarters, scored 10 in the fourth, including two threes. Cairns went ahead for good at 83-82 with 5:53 left, the last of 15 lead changes in the game.

It stayed close. Harrison's dunk made it 90-88 with 1:32 left. Brown answered, and Hinton hit a three to cut it to 92-91 with 1:14 left. Then came Bolden's three, and Pinder's layup with 20 seconds left.

## Threes decided it

Cairns made 14 of 35 threes, Brisbane eight of 33. That was an 18-point gap. Brisbane won the points from two-point shots 58-42, helped by 89 shots to Cairns' 72 and nine offensive rebounds from Harrison, but Cairns made 13 free throws to Brisbane's nine. That left the Bullets six short. But Max Mackinnon and [Arnas Velička](/player/NBL/5447896) went a combined 3-of-19 from three.

Hinton finished with 30 points on 13-of-24 shooting. Harrison had 23 points, 18 rebounds and three blocks. Mackinnon added 13 rebounds and five assists, and Velička eight assists.

Brown had 25 points and four assists for Cairns, on 5-of-11 from three. Bolden had 16 points and nine rebounds, McVeigh 15 points, seven assists and four steals, and Lewis 15. Pinder finished with 12 points, nine rebounds and five assists.

## A debut and an injury

[Luke Paul](/player/NBL/5456356), the 17-year-old Next Star, made his NBL debut after missing Cairns' first five games with a calf injury. He played 11 minutes and scored two points, with two steals. Shaun Bruce returned from a groin problem and played 13 minutes.

Cairns will be without wing Sunday Dech for at least four weeks with a hamstring injury he picked up at training this week.
