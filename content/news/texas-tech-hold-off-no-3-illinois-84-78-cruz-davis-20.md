No. 16 Texas Tech led No. 3 Illinois by 22 points in the first half on Tuesday night, and then spent the second half keeping it from catching up. Illinois got to within one, 65-64, with 5:37 left. It never got in front, and Texas Tech won 84-78 at United Supermarkets Arena in front of 15,098 fans.

Texas Tech is 3-0. Illinois is 2-1 and lost for the first time this season.

The Illini scored 49 points in the second half, more than the Red Raiders' 35. It was not enough, because Texas Tech had built a 49-29 lead at half-time.

## The first half

The game was tied once, at 17-17 with 11:45 left in the first half. Texas Tech scored the next 32 points to Illinois's 12 and went into the break up 49-29. The lead reached 22, 49-27, when [Dra Gibbs-Lawhorn](/player/NCAAM/4711259) made a 26-foot three at 1:04. [Quentin Coleman](/player/NCAAM/5307569) made a layup at the end of the half for Illinois.

Texas Tech opened the game with a layup by [Bassala Bagayoko](/player/NCAAM/5399797) and a basket from Gibbs-Lawhorn for a 4-0 lead. [Cruz Davis](/player/NCAAM/5107198) made a layup at 3:05 and Gibbs-Lawhorn another at 2:46 for 43-26.

## The comeback that came up short

Illinois scored 29 points in the first half and 49 in the second, and it shot better, too, but it was always chasing. [Andrej Stojaković](/player/NCAAM/5175007) made a layup at 5:37 to get the lead down to one, 65-64. [Damarion Dennis](/player/NCAAM/5243127) answered with a 26-foot three for 68-64 on a Davis assist.

Stojaković made both free throws at 4:49 for 68-66. [Donovan Atwell](/player/NCAAM/5107355) hit a 27-foot three at 4:01 for 71-66, and made two free throws at 3:09 for 73-67. Davis made a pull-up jumper at 2:17 for 75-67 and another at 1:29 for 77-70.

Coleman made two threes in the last 90 seconds for Illinois, at 1:58 and at 0:34, and [Stefan Vaaks](/player/NCAAM/5311991) made one at 0:44. But Texas Tech made its free throws. Stillwell made two at 0:42 for 80-73, Atwell two at 0:29 for 82-76, and [Darrion Williams](/player/NCAAM/4937074) two at 0:14 for 84-76. Coleman's dunk at 0:06 made the final score.

## The free-throw line

Texas Tech made 23 of 26 free throws and Illinois made 8 of 19. That was the difference in the game. Illinois scored 70 points on field goals, and Texas Tech scored 61. The Red Raiders scored 23 points from the line to Illinois's eight.

Texas Tech also had the 43-32 edge in rebounds and the 18-10 edge in offensive rebounds. [Jamichael Stillwell](/player/NCAAM/5241667) had 11 points and 13 rebounds and made 7 of 8 free throws. The two teams were close on turnovers, 14 for Texas Tech and 16 for Illinois.

## The numbers behind it

Davis led Texas Tech with 20 points on 7-of-16 shooting, with five assists. Gibbs-Lawhorn scored 15 on 7 of 12, and Atwell had 13 points and five steals. Dennis scored 10.

Coleman led Illinois with 19 points and made 5 of 6 threes. Stojaković scored 18, and [Tomislav Ivišić](/player/NCAAM/5214642) had 14 points and six rebounds. Illinois shot 30 of 60 from the field but 10 of 34 from three, and Vaaks made 2 of 10 of those.
