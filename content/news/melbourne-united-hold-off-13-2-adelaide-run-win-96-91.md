With 2:17 left at the Adelaide Entertainment Centre, [Zylan Cheatham](/player/NBL/3137713) dunked for the second time in 37 seconds and a 20-point Melbourne United lead was down to three. [Cole Anthony](/player/NBL/5443472) answered from 29 feet. United held on to beat the Adelaide 36ers 96-91 on Wednesday night.

It was also an Ignite Cup game. United are now 2-3. Adelaide, last season's runners-up, are 2-4 and have lost three straight.

## The two-man show

[Bryce Cotton](/player/NBL/2531054) scored 33 points with nine assists and made six of 13 threes. [Nick Rakocevic](/player/NBL/4066437) also scored 33, on 15-of-25 shooting, with 14 rebounds. Between them they had 66 of Adelaide's 91 points. The rest of the 36ers made 10 of 29 shots.

Adelaide were without Flynn Cameron and Isaac Humphries again.

## United build the lead

The game was tied at 18 when John Jenkins III hit a three with 3:49 left in the first quarter. United scored 13 of the last 16 points of the quarter, an Anthony three among them, and [Joe Ingles](/player/NBL/5402938) finished a layup on the buzzer for a 31-21 lead.

The second quarter went the same way. Melbourne led 56-36 with 30 seconds left in the half, their biggest lead, and 56-38 at the break. [Luke Travers](/player/NBL/5402940) had 14 of his 16 points by half-time.

Cotton hit two threes in 45 seconds early in the third, and Rakocevic kept scoring inside, but United answered each time. [Tom Wilson](/player/NBL/5230864) hit a three with seven seconds left in the quarter to make it 76-59.

## The run

Melbourne still led 86-72 after a three-point play from [Kyle Bowen](/player/NBL/5091547) with 4:45 left. Adelaide then scored 13 of the next 15 points. Cotton hit two threes, Jenkins one, and Cheatham, who had not scored until then, dunked twice in a row to make it 88-85.

Anthony's three made it 91-85 with 1:52 left. He added three free throws in the last 19 seconds, and Wilson made two with two seconds to go.

Cheatham finished with eight points, all in the last 2:54.

## Anthony's night

Anthony, United's import guard, finished with 24 points, five rebounds, four assists and two steals, and made four of his seven threes and eight of 10 free throws. [Josh Oduro](/player/NBL/5402939) scored 16 on 6-of-6 shooting and Travers 16 with seven rebounds. Ingles, back in Adelaide, had 11 points and four assists.

"I loved our intent and urgency for the first three quarters," United coach Jacob Chance said.

United went to the line 23 times and made 20. Adelaide made nine of 16.

The teams met on opening night in Melbourne, when Adelaide won by two.
