Early in the first quarter at Daegu Gymnasium, Korea Gas led 5-4. Then Anyang Jung Kwan Jang scored the next 15 points. [Han Seung-hee](/player/KBL/rg-119766) scored and made the free throw, [Park Jung-woong](/player/KBL/rg-242465) scored, and Han hit a three. [Byun Jun-hyung](/player/KBL/rg-52293) added a basket and a three, and John Mooney dunked in between. By 3:54 it was 19-5, and Jung Kwan Jang did not trail again on their way to an 86-80 win on Monday.

They are the only team in the KBL with two wins, after their 88-85 win over Hyundai Mobis on Saturday. Korea Gas are 0-2. The crowd was 3,418.

## Mooney on the glass

Mooney finished with 23 points, 17 rebounds, five of them offensive, six assists and two blocks in 34 minutes. He made 10 of 17 shots. Jung Kwan Jang won the boards 40-28.

## Korea Gas keep coming

Korea Gas got back within four in the second quarter, at 30-26 after a [SJ Belangel](/player/KBL/rg-182444) three and free throw. Jung Kwan Jang led 48-38 at halftime and 68-59 after three quarters.

The fourth quarter brought them close again. [Jeong Seong-woo](/player/KBL/rg-99313) opened the scoring with a basket and a free throw, and Park Jun-young hit a three at 6:02 to make it 75-71. Jung Kwan Jang answered with nine of the next 11 points. [Park Ji-hoon](/player/KBL/rg-121129) scored a basket at 4:39, two free throws at 2:36 and another basket at 2:04, and Mooney added one in between. It was 84-73.

Belangel hit a three at 1:17, and [Ra Gun-ah](/player/KBL/rg-23297) scored at 0:44, but Han's basket at 0:57 had already made it 86-76. Belangel's two free throws with eight seconds left set the final score.

## The numbers

Korea Gas made 13 of 36 threes. Jung Kwan Jang made seven of 18, but shot better from two, 27 of 46 to 13 of 28. Jung Kwan Jang scored 15 second-chance points to Korea Gas's eight.

Belangel had 23 points, eight assists and three steals, and made four threes and seven of eight free throws. Keve Aluma scored 16 but turned the ball over five times. Ra Gun-ah scored 15 on 6-of-9 shooting, with three of four threes and eight rebounds.

Byun scored 15 for Jung Kwan Jang, and they were plus-17 with him on the floor, the best mark in the game. Han added 11 and Park Ji-hoon 10. Rashawn Thomas had eight points, seven rebounds and five assists.

In the day's other game, Busan KCC won 97-87 at Hyundai Mobis, two days after losing their opener to LG by 35.
