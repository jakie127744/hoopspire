[Donovan Mitchell](/player/NBA/3908809) missed a 25-foot pull-up three with 8.7 seconds left, rebounded it himself, and missed a 23-foot pull-up three with 1.4 seconds left. The Atlanta Hawks beat the Cleveland Cavaliers 114-111 at State Farm Arena on Thursday night.

Atlanta trailed 31-16 with 10:31 left in the second quarter. It outscored Cleveland 98-80 from there. [Onyeka Okongwu](/player/NBA/4431680) scored 29 points with 12 rebounds, and [Nickeil Alexander-Walker](/player/NBA/4278039) made two free throws with 15.3 seconds left to make it a three-point game.

The Hawks are 3-3. The Cavaliers had won three straight. They are 3-2, and 0-2 on the road.

## Cleveland starts fast

Cleveland scored 28 points in the first quarter to Atlanta's 16. An 8-0 run that began with 1:35 left in the quarter made it 31-16 early in the second. It was the biggest lead of the night for either team.

## The turn in the second quarter

[Jock Landale](/player/NBA/3146557) hit a three at 9:51 to make it 31-19. [Aaron Wiggins](/player/NBA/4397183) added a layup and two threes, and Atlanta pulled to within 35-30 at 5:52. [Evan Mobley](/player/NBA/4432158) hit a jumper for Cleveland at 5:25 to make it 37-30.

Then the Cavaliers stopped scoring. They had no points for the next 3:54, until Mobley's dunk at 1:31.

In that time Atlanta scored 13 straight. Okongwu scored five of them, a layup and a free throw at 5:14 and a running layup at 4:33 that tied it at 37. [Dyson Daniels](/player/NBA/4869342) had a layup. [CJ McCollum](/player/NBA/2490149) hit a floater at 3:12 for a 39-37 lead, the Hawks' first of the night, and Atlanta did not trail again. [Jalen Johnson](/player/NBA/4701230) and Alexander-Walker added layups, and it was 43-37 at 1:45.

Okongwu hit a 25-foot three at 1:22 to make it 46-39. [Sam Merrill](/player/NBA/4066757) answered with a three, and Harden made two of three free throws at 6.1 seconds. Atlanta led 46-44 at halftime. It had won the quarter 30-16.

## Atlanta pulls away, Cleveland keeps coming

The Hawks opened the third quarter with an 11-2 run. Okongwu hit threes at 11:33 and 10:02, Alexander-Walker made three free throws, and Johnson scored from a foot out. It was 57-46 with 10:02 left, Atlanta's biggest lead of the night.

Cleveland cut it to one, 83-82, with 1:21 left in the quarter. Atlanta answered with eight straight points, six before the buzzer and two after it, and led 91-82 at 11:28 of the fourth.

The Cavaliers did not go away. [James Harden](/player/NBA/3992) scored on a driving layup and made the free throw at 1:45 to make it 108-104. Alexander-Walker answered with a layup at 1:27. Mobley dunked off a Mitchell pass at 1:19 to make it 110-106. Mitchell made three free throws at 43.2 seconds to make it 110-109.

Okongwu answered with a two-point shot at 28.2 seconds for 112-109. Mitchell scored from five feet at 20.6 seconds to make it 112-111. Alexander-Walker then made both free throws at 15.3 seconds after a Cavaliers foul.

Mitchell had the last two looks. He missed from 25 feet at 8.7 seconds and rebounded it. He missed from 23 feet at 1.4 seconds.

## The numbers

Okongwu made 11 of 17 shots, including four of six threes, and had two assists. Alexander-Walker scored 25 on 8-of-17 shooting, 4 of 12 from three. Wiggins scored 20 in 24 minutes off the bench, going 8 of 14 with four threes. Johnson had 17 points, eight rebounds and seven assists. McCollum scored five on 1-of-8 shooting and had eight assists.

For Cleveland, Harden scored 24 on 7-of-14 shooting with five rebounds and four assists. Mitchell scored 22 on 7-of-19 and made one of nine threes. Merrill scored 16 off the bench with four threes on eight tries. [Jarrett Allen](/player/NBA/4066328) had 14 points and eight rebounds, and Mobley had 11 points and seven assists.

Atlanta made 17 of 43 threes. Cleveland made 13 of 42. That was a 12-point gap from the arc, 51-39. The Cavaliers made 22 of 29 free throws to Atlanta's 13 of 18, but the Hawks won the rebounding 53-45, with 15 offensive rebounds to nine. They also had 34 assists to 26 and won the fast-break points 15-4. Atlanta had 11 turnovers and Cleveland nine.

Both teams played on Wednesday. Atlanta lost at home to Miami 129-117, and Cleveland beat Washington 121-111 at home. The crowd on Thursday was 15,025.

## What is next

Atlanta plays at New Orleans on Saturday, October 31, at 7 p.m. ET. Cleveland plays at Milwaukee the same night at 8 p.m. ET.

Atlanta's first two wins of the season came on the road, at Orlando on October 21, 132-125, and at Charlotte on October 23, 128-81. The Charlotte game is covered in [the Friday NBA roundup](/story/grizzlies-17-0-run-hawks-win-by-47-nba-friday-roundup).
