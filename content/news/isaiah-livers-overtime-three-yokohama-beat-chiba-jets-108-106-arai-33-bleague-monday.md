Chiba led 106-104 with 30 seconds left in overtime at Yokohama Buntai on Monday. Isaiah Livers caught a pass from James Bell and made a three. Yokohama led 107-106, Livers added a free throw with 12 seconds left, and Luke Maye missed a three for Chiba with three seconds to go. The Yokohama B-Corsairs won 108-106 in front of 4,945.

It was the longest and closest of Monday's five B.League Premier games. Alvark Tokyo won 103-78 at Osaka, Gunma won 72-61 at Saga, Shiga beat Shinshu 95-76, and Nagasaki beat Kobe 85-69.

## Yokohama 108, Chiba Jets 106 (OT)

**The first half.** Chiba led 25-22 after the first quarter. Yokohama won the second 22-18 and led 44-43 at the break.

**The Jets' push.** Chiba won the third quarter 25-18, 10 of those points from the free-throw line, and led by as many as 12. Yokohama trailed 68-62 with a quarter left.

**The fourth quarter.** Gakuto Arai kept Yokohama close. He scored 33 points in 30 minutes, with 6 of 9 from three. A Chiba coach's technical foul gave Livers a free throw for 93-93 with 28 seconds left. Narito Namizato's jumper made it 95-93 with 14 seconds left, and Nate Williams answered with a layup from Maye's pass to tie it at 95-95 with eight seconds left. Maik Kotsar missed a layup and Ren Kanechika missed a three as time ran out.

**Overtime.** Yuki Togashi's three-point play put Chiba ahead 106-104 with 46 seconds left. Then came Livers.

Kotsar finished with 25 points and 17 rebounds, and Livers had 19 points and 11 rebounds. Namizato had 10 assists. Togashi and Nate Williams scored 20 each for Chiba, and Yuta Watanabe had 10 points and 10 rebounds. The lead changed hands 16 times. Yokohama are 4-3 and the Jets 2-5.
