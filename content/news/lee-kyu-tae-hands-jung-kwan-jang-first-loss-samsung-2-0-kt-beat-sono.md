Seoul Samsung led 48-47 going into the fourth quarter against Anyang Jung Kwan Jang on Friday afternoon. [Lee Kyu-tae](/player/KBL/kbl-291853) then scored 11 of Samsung's 18 points in the period, including two threes and three free throws with 2:19 left, and the Thunders won 66-58 at Jamsil Student Gymnasium in front of 3,617 fans.

Jung Kwan Jang had won their first two games and came in top of the table. They are now 2-1. Samsung are 2-0, the only team in the league with two wins and no losses. Seoul SK have not played yet.

In the other game, Suwon KT beat Goyang Sono 88-79 at Suwon KT Sonicboom Arena.

## Samsung 66, Jung Kwan Jang 58

### First half: six lead changes in four minutes

Samsung won the first quarter 22-16. Jung Kwan Jang answered with a 16-11 second quarter, and the lead changed hands six times in the last 4:07 of the half. Han Ho-bin's basket with three seconds left put Samsung ahead 33-32 at half-time.

### Third quarter: still one point

The third quarter finished 15-15. Jung Kwan Jang's biggest lead of the game was three, at 44-41 with 2:10 left in it. Samsung went back in front at 45-44 with 48 seconds left, the last of 10 lead changes, and never trailed again.

### Fourth quarter: Lee's run

Lee opened the fourth with a basket, then hit a three for 53-49 with 6:04 left. After [John Mooney](/player/KBL/kbl-291883) cut it to 57-54 with a three, Lee answered with another three and then made all three of his free throws for a 63-54 lead with 2:19 left, Samsung's biggest of the night. [Kerem Kanter](/player/KBL/rg-42177) finished it with a basket with 21 seconds left.

Lee scored 18 points in 22 minutes on 6-of-7 shooting, 3-of-4 from three. Han had 11 points and four assists, Kanter 10 points and nine rebounds, and [Justin Gutang](/player/KBL/rg-206104) nine points, seven rebounds and six assists.

Mooney led Jung Kwan Jang with 24 points and 12 rebounds. No one else scored more than nine.

Samsung made seven of 27 threes, Jung Kwan Jang three of 25. That gap was 12 points. Jung Kwan Jang made more two-point shots, 21 to 17, and the free throws went 11-7 to Samsung.

## KT 88, Sono 79

KT led by 16 at 72-56 late in the third quarter and 76-61 after three. Sono scored the first 12 points of the fourth. Lee Jung-hyun scored five of them, [Johnny O'Bryant III](/player/KBL/rg-24170) four and Lim Dong-seop three, and it was 76-73 with 7:18 left.

[Paris Bass](/player/KBL/kbl-291671) stopped the run with a basket. Sono got to 78-76 with 5:59 left, and [Miguel Oczon](/player/KBL/kbl-291692) answered with a three and a basket for 83-76. Sono did not get within five again.

Bass scored 30 points with nine rebounds. Oczon had 21 points and five assists, and Moon Jeong-hyeon 12 on four threes. O'Bryant scored 26 points with 15 rebounds and four steals for Sono, who made 12 of 42 threes. KT won the two-point shooting 27 of 40 to 18 of 32.

KT are 2-1. Sono are 1-2.
