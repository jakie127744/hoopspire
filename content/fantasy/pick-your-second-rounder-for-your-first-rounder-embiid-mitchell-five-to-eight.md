Here is the rule for the turn at the end of round two: do not just take the next name on the board. Take the player who covers your first pick's weak categories, as long as he sits within about four places of the best available. In eight of 12 draft slots, that swap adds at least one category. In five of those eight it costs about two places on the board or less, and in the other three about four.

**Format:** head-to-head nine-category, 12 teams, snake draft. In head-to-head you win categories one at a time each week, so a pair that is above average in eight categories beats a slightly stronger pair that is above average in five. **Pool:** the same 150 players as our recent columns. **Minutes:** every player is valued at his 2025-26 per-game numbers, so each is assumed to play the minutes he played last season. Games missed are not priced in, and that matters for one name below.

## How I measured it

A player's value is the sum of his nine z-scores. A z-score is how far he sits above or below the pool average in one category, measured in standard deviations.

For each slot, I took the player ranked at that slot as your first pick, and assumed every manager drafts off the same board. In a snake draft, slot 1 picks again at 24, slot 7 at 18, slot 12 at 13. At that second pick, I looked at the player ranked there and the four ranked just behind him, since those are the ones still on the board.

For each pair I added the two players' z-scores in every category and counted how many categories came out above zero, which means above two average players. The **board pick** is the highest-ranked player. The **best fit** is the one that gives the pair the most categories above zero, with total value as the tiebreak.

## Three slots in detail

- **Slot 7, [Donovan Mitchell](/player/NBA/3908809).** Mitchell is below average in rebounds, blocks, field goal percentage and turnovers. [Cade Cunningham](/player/NBA/4432166) at 18 fixes none of them, and the pair is above average in five categories. [Joel Embiid](/player/NBA/3059318), two places later, fixes the first three. The pair is above average in eight, for a cost of 0.22.
- **Slot 2, [Victor Wembanyama](/player/NBA/5104157).** Wembanyama is below average in assists, steals and turnovers. [Austin Reaves](/player/NBA/4066457) at 26 brings the pair's assists above zero, and the pair is above average in eight categories. [Michael Porter Jr.](/player/NBA/4278104) at 23 leaves it at six. The cost is 0.47, about three and a half places.
- **Slot 11, [Stephen Curry](/player/NBA/3975).** [Chet Holmgren](/player/NBA/4433255) fixes Curry's rebounds, blocks and field goal percentage. He also costs the pair its assists and steals, which [Kevin Porter Jr.](/player/NBA/4397140) would have added. Six categories against five, so take Holmgren, but know what you are giving up.

## Turnovers: the hole almost nobody fills

Ten of the 12 first-round picks are below average in turnovers. Murphy and Markkanen are the exceptions. Among the other 10, only two have a second-round candidate who brings the pair back above zero: [Donovan Clingan](/player/NBA/5105565) next to Wembanyama, and Jimmy Butler III or Derrick White next to Leonard. If you are going to punt a category, turnovers is the one your first two picks may have chosen for you.

## The Embiid catch

Embiid is the best fit in three slots, and the board pick in a fourth. These numbers use per-game value, and Embiid played 38 games last season. In our [games missed column](/story/games-missed-who-moves-embiid-butler-fall-barnes-durant-rise), pricing those missed games dropped him 11 places. If you think he plays 38 again, he is not a bargain at 20. Take the next-best fit instead.

## What to do with it

- **At the turn, list your first pick's weak categories.** Then check the next four or five names for the player who covers the most of them.
- **Accept a cost of up to about 0.5,** three or four places, for two or more extra categories. For one extra category, keep the cost to about two places.
- **Do not chase turnovers in round two.** It is almost never on offer. Plan for it.
- **Check the games.** A fit who sits out does not fill those categories on the nights he misses.
