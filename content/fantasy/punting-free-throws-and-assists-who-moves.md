If you plan to punt free throws and assists together, you are drafting centers. [Rudy Gobert](/player/NBA/3032976) goes from 79th of 150 to 9th. [Jalen Duren](/player/NBA/4433621) goes from 28th to 11th, [Donovan Clingan](/player/NBA/5105565) from 27th to 12th and [Evan Mobley](/player/NBA/4432158) from 45th to 13th. On the other side, [James Harden](/player/NBA/3992) falls from 21st to 116th, and [Devin Booker](/player/NBA/3136193) from 59th to 143rd.

This is the first two-category column after the nine single punts. Punting means giving up a category on purpose and building around the rest. **Format:** nine-category head-to-head, where you win a week by taking five of nine categories. Punt two and you need five of the remaining seven: points, rebounds, steals, blocks, threes, field goal percentage and turnovers.

**Pool:** the 150 players with the most nine-category value among the 247 NBA players who played at least 30 games and 20 minutes a night in 2025-26. Every figure is a 2025-26 per-game average at that player's own minutes, built from box score totals. Free throw percentages come from season totals. A player traded during the season counts his games for both teams. Nothing here is a projection of 2026-27.

## Why this pair

Free throws and assists do not come from the same players. Across the 150, the two z-scores correlate only 0.20, so each punt removes something different. What links them is who is bad at both. The pool has 32 players listed at center. They average 2.7 assists a game against 3.77 for everyone, and they shoot 73.9% from the line on season totals against 79.9% for the pool. A center starts behind in both columns, so when both stop counting, he is the one who comes out ahead.

The free throw score is volume weighted. It is a player's free throws attempted times the gap between his shooting percentage and the pool's, so a poor shooter who rarely gets to the line barely moves it. Gobert, at 52.6% on 4.0 attempts a game, is -3.40 in it. [Giannis Antetokounmpo](/player/NBA/3032977), at 65.0% on 9.9 attempts, is -4.70, the lowest in the pool.

## How the punt ranking is built

Each player has a z-score in each category, which measures how many standard deviations he sits from the pool average. To build the punt ranking I subtracted each player's free throw z-score and his assist z-score from his nine-category total and re-ranked the 150. I did not rebuild the pool without those two categories, so a player just outside the 150 could rank differently in a true punt draft. Read the ranks as a guide to direction and size, not as exact draft positions.

## What barely changes

The top does not care. [Victor Wembanyama](/player/NBA/5104157) and [Nikola Jokić](/player/NBA/3112335) swap places at the top, and [Kawhi Leonard](/player/NBA/6450), [Shai Gilgeous-Alexander](/player/NBA/4278073) and [Luka Dončić](/player/NBA/3945274) follow in the top five. Four of the five best nine-category players are still in the top five. The fifth, [Tyrese Maxey](/player/NBA/4431678), goes from 5th to 8th. The top 10 then fills with [Chet Holmgren](/player/NBA/4433255) (15th to 6th), Antetokounmpo (53rd to 7th), Gobert and [Anthony Edwards](/player/NBA/4594268) (13th to 10th).

Six of the top 10 hold, 12 of the top 20 and 36 of the top 50. For comparison, punting free throws alone keeps 43 of the top 50 in place and punting assists alone keeps 40. Two punts move more of the board than either one.

Eight players leave the top 20: [Jamal Murray](/player/NBA/3936299) (8th to 38th), [Stephen Curry](/player/NBA/3975) (11th to 31st), [Kevin Porter Jr.](/player/NBA/4397140) (14th to 45th), [Kevin Durant](/player/NBA/3202) (16th to 32nd), [Jalen Johnson](/player/NBA/4701230) (17th to 40th), [Cade Cunningham](/player/NBA/4432166) (18th to 73rd), [Karl-Anthony Towns](/player/NBA/3136195) (19th to 24th) and [Joel Embiid](/player/NBA/3059318) (20th to 37th). Eight come in: Clingan, Duren, [Alex Sarr](/player/NBA/5160992), Mobley, [Kel'el Ware](/player/NBA/5105623), Antetokounmpo, [Jarrett Allen](/player/NBA/4066328) and Gobert.

Of the 25, 15 are listed at center, eight at forward and two at guard. They averaged 14.3 points, 2.2 assists and 1.5 turnovers a game, against a pool average of 1.9 turnovers. Twenty-two of the 32 centers rise 15 places or more. Embiid is the only center who falls that far, and at 3.9 assists he is well above the 2.7 the pool's centers average.

The pairing does work that neither punt does alone. Gobert is 17th punting free throws and 48th punting assists, and 9th punting both. He is -3.40 at the line and -1.04 in assists, so each punt hands him something. Duren (-0.87 and -0.89), Clingan (-0.87 and -0.84) and [Daniel Gafford](/player/NBA/4278049) (-0.99 and -1.34) are the same kind of player on a smaller scale.

Antetokounmpo is the opposite case, and the one to read carefully. His whole gain comes from free throws. He is 7th punting free throws, 78th punting assists and 7th punting both, because his 5.4 assists are a plus (+0.82) that you give up for nothing. If Giannis is the reason you punt, punt free throws alone. He also played 36 games.

Mobley is similar. He is 10th punting free throws alone and 13th with assists punted too, so the second punt costs him three places. [Ausar Thompson](/player/NBA/4684742), at 57.1% from the line, is 39th with free throws punted alone and 29th with both.

Twenty-seven players fall 30 places or more. Twenty-five are listed at guard, two at forward and none at center. They averaged 6.0 assists, 18.9 points and 2.4 turnovers a game, and they shot 85.9% from the line. The pool averages 3.77 assists and 1.89 turnovers.

That is the cost of the pair. A guard who runs an offense gets his value from assists and free throws, and you have stopped counting both. His turnovers, 2.4 a game for this group, still count. Brunson at 35.0 minutes and Harden at 34.8 are making plays that cannot show up in your totals.

## The minutes assumption

Take a hypothetical center, not a real player, who grabs 0.36 rebounds, blocks 0.05 shots and makes 0.06 assists per minute.

- At 24 minutes: 8.64 rebounds (z-score +1.29), 1.20 blocks (+1.18) and 1.44 assists (-1.17).
- At 32 minutes: 11.52 rebounds (+2.61), 1.60 blocks (+2.05) and 1.92 assists (-0.93).

Eight extra minutes add 1.32 from rebounds and 0.87 from blocks, in any league. In a standard league the assist line adds 0.24 more and still leaves him deep in the red. In a punt-assist league it is not on the sheet at all. His assist score at 24 minutes is -1.17, and it costs you nothing. So a big man's minutes are worth more to you than to the manager across from you. When camp news says a center is moving into the starting lineup, this is the build that profits.

A guard is the opposite. Another eight minutes for Harden brings more assists and more turnovers, and only the turnovers count.

## What this does not say

This is 2025-26 data, one season of it, and it says nothing about 2026-27 roles. Antetokounmpo played 36 games, so per-game ranks flatter him if he misses time again. The pool was fixed before the two categories were removed, and a real punt draft changes who is available. The z-scores add as if the categories were independent, and they are not entirely: assists correlate -0.76 with turnovers, which is why high-assist guards keep paying a price you did not remove.

The finding that holds is the shape. A two-punt build is a center build. Fifteen centers and eight forwards rise, 25 guards fall, and the top five hardly move. If you commit to it, do it at the draft: take the centers in the rounds where other managers take guards, and keep your last picks for the few guards who score without the assists, such as Edwards.
