Rudy Gobert ranks 78th of 150 in a nine-category pool. Remove free throws from the scoring and he ranks 17th. Giannis Antetokounmpo goes from 54th to 7th. Those are the two largest jumps among players who end up in the top 40, and both come from one number: free throw impact.

This column is about punting, which means deliberately giving up one category and building a roster around the other eight. **Format:** nine-category head-to-head, where you win a week by taking five of nine categories. Punt free throws and you need five of the remaining eight.

**Pool:** the same 150 players as in the nine-cat top 50 and the column on blocks, taken from 468 NBA players with a 2025-26 season row, keeping the 240 with at least 30 games and 20 minutes a night, then the top 150 by nine-category value. Every figure is a 2025-26 per-game average at that player's own minutes, from ESPN. Nothing here is a projection of 2026-27.

## How the punt ranking is built

Each player has a z-score in each category, which measures how many standard deviations he sits from the pool average. For free throws the score is volume weighted: it is his free throws attempted times the gap between his shooting percentage and the pool's 79.9%. A player who shoots 90% on two attempts a game barely moves your team number. A player who shoots 52.5% on four attempts a game moves it a lot. Gobert's raw figure is -1.10 made free throws a game against an average shooter at the same volume.

To build the punt ranking I subtracted each player's free throw z-score from his nine-category total and re-ranked the 150. I did not rebuild the pool without free throws, so a player just outside the 150 could rank differently in a true punt draft. Read the ranks as a good guide to direction and size, not as exact draft positions.

## What barely changes

The top of the board holds. Six of the ten best players in the nine-category ranking are still in the top ten with free throws removed: Nikola Jokic, Victor Wembanyama, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Tyrese Maxey, Kawhi Leonard and Luka Doncic. Fourteen of the top 20 hold, and 43 of the top 50. Punting does not reshuffle the whole draft. It moves a group of players at the edges.

## The minutes assumption

A free throw hit is a per-minute rate multiplied by a guess about minutes. Here is the arithmetic for a hypothetical center, not a real player, who attempts 0.14 free throws a minute and shoots 55%. Against the pool's 79.9% that is a gap of about 0.25 per attempt.

- At 26 minutes a game: 3.64 attempts, about -0.91 made free throws relative to an average shooter.
- At 30 minutes: 4.2 attempts, about -1.05.
- At 34 minutes: 4.76 attempts, about -1.19.

Eight minutes of playing time moves the cost by about 0.28 made free throws a night. If you are punting, that cost is one you have already agreed to pay, so a minutes bump for this player is close to pure gain in the other eight categories. If you are not punting, the same bump makes him worse. The minutes guess matters most for exactly the players in the table above, and where a coach's rotation is unknown, treat the 30-minute line as a middle case and the other two as the range.

## What to do with it

1. Decide before the draft, not at pick 40. The punt ranking only helps if you are willing to take the risers at their new ranks, and that means passing on some of the fallers.
2. Check the other eight categories before you commit. The eight risers in the table above are above the pool average in rebounds, blocks and field goal impact in seven cases out of eight, and none of the eight is above average in threes. A punt build therefore has to find its threes elsewhere, so pair these players with guards and wings who supply them.
3. Do not punt on the strength of one player. One star with a bad free throw line is a player you accept. A whole punt build is a plan.
4. Treat games played as a cost. Antetokounmpo, Embiid and Butler all played 38 games or fewer last season, and a punt build only pays off in the weeks they are in the lineup.

This column uses last season's averages and has no projection for 2026-27 minutes. Where a rotation changes in camp, the arithmetic above shows how far the answer moves.
